diet too optimistically. That's the main finding of a new study by researchers at the U.S.

eat and how they actually do. For the study,the researchers asked participants to rate their
diet as excellent, very good, good,  fair or poor. Folks also completed 24-hour food
questionnaires. Then, the researchers compared answers to see how well responses to the
two exercises matched up.
