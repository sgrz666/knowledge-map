world at first hand. In Philadelphia and Portland, Oregon, plans are being drawn up for

public schools that won't have any school buildings at all. That will take the students out

into the city and help them to use it and its people as a resource. In other words, students,

perhaps  in groups, perhaps independently,  will go  to  libraries, museums,  exhibitions,

courtrooms, radio and TV stations, meetings, businesses, and laboratories to learn about

their world and society at first hand. A small private school in Washington is already doing

this. It makes sense. We need more of it.
