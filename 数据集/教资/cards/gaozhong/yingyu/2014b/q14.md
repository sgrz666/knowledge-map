【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

When a teacher says "Next please pay attention to the time of arrival and departure of the planes in the recording." he/she intends to develop students' skill of__________.

- A. predicting
- B. getting the general picture
- C. distinguishing sounds
- D. getting specific information

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher says "Next please pay attention to the time of arrival and departure of the planes in the recording." he/she intends to develop students' skill of__________.。选项：Apredicting；Bgetting the general picture；Cdistinguishing sounds；Dgetting specific information。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师提示学生注意录音中飞机的起降时间，要求抓取具体时刻信息。D「getting specific information」符合。A「predicting」预测、B「getting the general picture」把握整体、C「distinguishing sounds」辨音，均不描述抓取具体时间信息的技能。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
