【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

When any of the maxims under the Cooperative Principle is flouted on purpose__________might arise.

- A. ambiguous structure
- B. contradictory proposition
- C. mutual understanding
- D. conversational implicature

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When any of the maxims under the Cooperative Principle is flouted on purpose__________might arise.。选项：Aambiguous structure；Bcontradictory proposition；Cmutual understanding；Dconversational implicature。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 故意违反合作原则中的某一准则，会促使听话人推断说话人的言外之意，从而产生会话含义。D「conversational implicature」符合。A「ambiguous structure」歧义结构、B「contradictory proposition」矛盾命题均非合作原则违反的必然结果；C「mutual understanding」相互理解是正常交际的结果而非故意违约的产物。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
