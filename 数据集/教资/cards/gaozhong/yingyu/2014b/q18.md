【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following teacher’s instructions could seI've the purpose of eliciting ideas?

- A. Shall we move on?
- B. Read after me everyone.
- C. What can you see in this picture?
- D. What does the world "quickly" mean?

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following teacher’s instructions could seI've the purpose of eliciting ideas?。选项：AShall we move on?；BRead after me everyone.；CWhat can you see in this picture?；DWhat does the world "quickly" mean?。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 让学生观察图片并说出所见，能激发学生基于已有知识与观察自行输出内容，属启发诱导。C「What can you see in this picture?」符合。A「Shall we move on?」是推进课堂；B「Read after me」是跟读；D「What does the word 'quickly' mean?」询问词义，答案教师已知，属展示性提问。故选C。
