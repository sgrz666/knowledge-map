【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

Unlike her__________ sister Judith is a shy unsociable person who dislikes to go to parties or to make new friends.

- A. charming
- B. friendly
- C. gracious
- D. gregarious

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Unlike her__________ sister Judith is a shy unsociable person who dislikes to go to parties or to make new friends.。选项：Acharming；Bfriendly；Cgracious；Dgregarious。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干说她「shy, unsociable, dislikes parties or making new friends」，与姐姐形成对比，故姐姐应是相反的性格——爱交际的。D「gregarious」意为群居的、爱社交的，符合对比。A「charming」有魅力的、B「friendly」友好的、C「gracious」亲切的，三者虽为褒义但不构成「爱交际 vs 不爱交际」的直接对立。故选D。
