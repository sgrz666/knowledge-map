【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

I will never know all that was in his head at the time__________.

- A. nor will anyone else
- B. nor anyone else will
- C. nor won't anyone else
- D. nor anyone else won't

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：I will never know all that was in his head at the time__________.。选项：Anor will anyone else；Bnor anyone else will；Cnor won't anyone else；Dnor anyone else won't。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 前句为否定句，后句表示「其他人也不会知道」，用nor引导倒装：nor will anyone else。A「nor will anyone else」符合。B「nor anyone else will」未倒装；C「nor won't anyone else」与D「nor anyone else won't」双重否定，语义变成肯定。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
