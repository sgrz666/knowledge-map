【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

She doesn't want to work right now because she thinks that if she __________a job she probably wouldn't be able to visit her friends very often.

- A. has to get
- B. were to get
- C. had got
- D. could have got

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：She doesn't want to work right now because she thinks that if she __________a job she probably wouldn't be able to visit her friends very often.。选项：Ahas to get；Bwere to get；Chad got；Dcould have got。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 该句为虚拟条件句，表示与将来事实相反的假设：if she were to get a job，主句用wouldn't be able to。B「were to get」符合。A「has to get」为陈述语气、C「had got」与D「could have got」均与从句中隐含的将来假设不符（wouldn't be able to提示虚拟）。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
