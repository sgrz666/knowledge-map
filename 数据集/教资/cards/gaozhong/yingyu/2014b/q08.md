【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

What is the correct way to read the decimal "106.16" in English?

- A. One hundred and six point one six.
- B. One hundred and six point sixteen.
- C. One hundred and six points one six.
- D. One hundred and six points sixteen.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What is the correct way to read the decimal "106.16" in English?。选项：AOne hundred and six point one six.；BOne hundred and six point sixteen.；COne hundred and six points one six.；DOne hundred and six points sixteen.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 英语中小数点的读法为point，且小数点后的数字须逐位读出：106.16读作one hundred and six point one six。A「One hundred and six point one six」符合。B「point sixteen」错误地把小数部分当作整数读；C「points」错误地用了复数；D「points sixteen」两处均误。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
