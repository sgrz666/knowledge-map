【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

Which of the following shows the proper rhythmical pattern of the sentence?

- A. ´Come to ´see us at our ´new a´partment.
- B. ´Come to ´see us at´our new ´apartment.
- C. ´Come to ´see us ´at our ´new ´apartment.
- D. ´Come to ´see us ´at our ´new a´partment.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following shows the proper rhythmical pattern of the sentence?。选项：A´Come to ´see us at our ´new a´partment.；B´Come to ´see us at´our new ´apartment.；C´Come to ´see us ´at our ´new ´apartment.；D´Come to ´see us ´at our ´new a´partment.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 英语句子的节奏以重读音节为核心，实词（come、see、new、apartment的关键音节）重读，虚词（to、us、at、our）弱读。「Come to see us at our new apartment」的重音应落在come、see、new、-part-上。A项标注的重音位置与英语节奏规律一致。B项把our误作重读、C项把at与our都标重、D项把at标重，均违反「虚词不重读」的基本规律。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
