【2014b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

He pledged support for__________care" where youngsters were looked after by close relatives like aunts or uncles but not parents.

- A. family
- B. kinship
- C. sibling
- D. relative

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：He pledged support for__________care" where youngsters were looked after by close relatives like aunts or uncles but not parents.。选项：Afamily；Bkinship；Csibling；Drelative。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「由近亲如叔伯姨舅照顾而非父母」，涉亲属关系网络，kinship care是专业术语，指由亲属承担的照护。B「kinship」意为亲属关系，符合。A「family」与relative一词重复且不构成该术语；C「sibling」指兄弟姐妹，范围过窄；D「relative」是具体的人，不能修饰care构成该专业术语。故选B。
