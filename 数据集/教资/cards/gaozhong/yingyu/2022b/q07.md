【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

He wasn't appointed chairman of the committee not very popular with all its members.

- A. to be considered
- B. considering
- C. being considered
- D. having considered

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：He wasn't appointed chairman of the committee not very popular with all its members.。选项：Ato be considered；Bconsidering；Cbeing considered；Dhaving considered。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「他未被任命为委员会主席，因为人们认为他不太受全体成员欢迎」，需用动名词的被动式作原因状语。C「being considered」符合，构成He wasn't appointed... being considered not very popular（因被认为不太受欢迎）。A「to be considered」为不定式、B「considering」为主动、D「having considered」为主动完成，三者均与「他被人认为」的被动关系不符。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
