【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

Which of the following may comprehensively reflect the students' English language development?

- A. Records of students' homework.
- B. Comments from the previous English teachers.
- C. Portfolios that include students' writing presentations test papers etc.
- D. Standardized tests scores that demonstrate students' academic performance.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following may comprehensively reflect the students' English language development?。选项：ARecords of students' homework.；BComments from the previous English teachers.；CPortfolios that include students' writing presentations test papers etc.；DStandardized tests scores that demonstrate students' academic performance.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 作品集（portfolio）汇集学生写作、演示、试卷等多种作品，能纵向、多维地反映语言发展过程。C「Portfolios that include students' writing, presentations, test papers etc.」符合。A仅作业记录、B仅前任教师评语、D仅标准化测试成绩，三者都是单一维度，不能全面反映发展。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
