【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

Before conducting an activity a teacher should give instructions so that students will know what to do and how to do it.

- A. abstract and general
- B. clear and concise
- C. euphemistic and implicit
- D. strict and detailed

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Before conducting an activity a teacher should give instructions so that students will know what to do and how to do it.。选项：Aabstract and general；Bclear and concise；Ceuphemistic and implicit；Dstrict and detailed。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 布置活动前的指令必须清晰简练，学生才能准确理解做什么与怎么做。B「clear and concise」符合。A「abstract and general」过于抽象笼统、C「euphemistic and implicit」委婉含蓄会降低可懂度、D「strict and detailed」过于繁琐冗长，三者均不利于学生快速准确地把握任务要求。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
