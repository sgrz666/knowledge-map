【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

When a teacher asks students questions that involve making judgments based on a certain criterion he/she aims at developing students' ability of .

- A. comprehension
- B. reinterpretation
- C. reflection
- D. evaluation

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher asks students questions that involve making judgments based on a certain criterion he/she aims at developing students' ability of .。选项：Acomprehension；Breinterpretation；Creflection；Devaluation。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 要求依据一定标准作出判断，属评价（evaluation）层次的思维活动。D「evaluation」符合。A「comprehension」理解、B「reinterpretation」重新解释、C「reflection」反思，三者均不含「依标准作判断」这一要求。故选D。
