【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

Which of the following is least suitable to develop students' cultural awareness?

- A. Classical western literature.
- B. Translated Chinese classics.
- C. Newspapers and magazines.
- D. English grammar books.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is least suitable to develop students' cultural awareness?。选项：AClassical western literature.；BTranslated Chinese classics.；CNewspapers and magazines.；DEnglish grammar books.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 培养文化意识需接触目的语文化与本国文化的内容载体。A「Classical western literature」西方文学、B「Translated Chinese classics」中国典籍英译、C「Newspapers and magazines」报刊，三者均承载文化信息。D「English grammar books」语法书只讲语言结构，几乎不含文化内容，最不适合。故选D。
