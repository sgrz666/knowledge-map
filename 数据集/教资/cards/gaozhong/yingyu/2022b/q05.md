【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

That wonderful performance by Larry last night enthusiastic applause of the audience.

- A. drew in
- B. drew forth
- C. won over
- D. won up

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：That wonderful performance by Larry last night enthusiastic applause of the audience.。选项：Adrew in；Bdrew forth；Cwon over；Dwon up。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** draw forth意为「引出、博得」，符合「Larry精彩的表演博得了观众热烈的掌声」。B「drew forth」符合。A「drew in」意为「吸引参加、吸入」；C「won over」意为「说服、争取过来」（宾语应是人）；D「won up」不成立。故选B。
