【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

Evidence came up specific speech sounds are recognized by babies as young as months.

- A. what
- B. which
- C. whose
- D. that

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Evidence came up specific speech sounds are recognized by babies as young as months.。选项：Awhat；Bwhich；Cwhose；Dthat。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** Evidence后接同位语从句，说明证据的具体内容，从句成分完整，需用that引导。D「that」符合。A「what」须在从句中作成分（此处成分完整）；B「which」不引导同位语从句；C「whose」表所属。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
