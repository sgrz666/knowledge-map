【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Which of the following is the most effective in teaching grammar for the purpose of communication?

- A. Explaining grammar rules.
- B. Teaching grammar in a context.
- C. Asking students to memorize grammar rules.
- D. Presenting grammar rules from the simple to the complex.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is the most effective in teaching grammar for the purpose of communication?。选项：AExplaining grammar rules.；BTeaching grammar in a context.；CAsking students to memorize grammar rules.；DPresenting grammar rules from the simple to the complex.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 为交际目的教语法，应把语法置于真实语境中呈现与操练，使学生在使用中理解其功能。B「Teaching grammar in a context」符合。A「Explaining grammar rules」仅讲解规则、C「memorize grammar rules」机械记忆、D「from the simple to the complex」是呈现顺序而非交际化处理，三者均脱离语境。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
