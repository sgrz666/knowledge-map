【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

Which of the following activities might involve more integration of language skills?

- A. Writing a letter.
- B. Listening to the radio.
- C. Reading a book on your own.
- D. Discussing an article with a friend.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities might involve more integration of language skills?。选项：AWriting a letter.；BListening to the radio.；CReading a book on your own.；DDiscussing an article with a friend.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 技能综合活动需同时调动多种语言技能。D「Discussing an article with a friend」讨论文章需先阅读（读）再口头交流（说）并倾听对方（听），涉及多种技能整合。A「Writing a letter」仅写、B「Listening to the radio」仅听、C「Reading a book on your own」仅读，均为单一技能活动。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、英语写作与篇章；关键词：audio-lingual、coherence、cohesion、communicative、composition、essay、listening、oral
