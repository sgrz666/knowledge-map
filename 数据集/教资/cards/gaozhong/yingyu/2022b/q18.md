【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following feedback should be avoided in developing students' writing skills?

- A. Pointing out areas for improvement.
- B. Sharing personal views on the topic.
- C. Giving students only a grade.
- D. Giving positive comments.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following feedback should be avoided in developing students' writing skills?。选项：APointing out areas for improvement.；BSharing personal views on the topic.；CGiving students only a grade.；DGiving positive comments.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 只给分数而不作具体反馈，学生无法知道问题所在与改进方向，不利于写作能力发展。C「Giving students only a grade」应避免。A「Pointing out areas for improvement」指出改进方向、B「Sharing personal views」分享观点、D「Giving positive comments」给正面评价，三者均为有效反馈。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
