【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

Which of the following methods could help teachers make the best use of body language to increase learners’ involvement?

- A. Grammar Translation Method.
- B. Total Physical Response.
- C. Suggestopedia.
- D. Direct Method.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following methods could help teachers make the best use of body language to increase learners’ involvement?。选项：AGrammar Translation Method.；BTotal Physical Response.；CSuggestopedia.；DDirect Method.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 全身反应法（TPR）以教师发指令、学生用身体动作回应的方式组织教学，最大程度运用肢体语言促进参与。B「Total Physical Response」符合。A「Grammar Translation Method」以翻译讲解为主、C「Suggestopedia」以暗示与放松为主、D「Direct Method」强调用目标语直接教学但不着重身体动作回应。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
