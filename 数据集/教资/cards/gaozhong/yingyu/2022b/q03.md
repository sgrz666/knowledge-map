【2022b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

We have invited fifty people to attend my daughter's wedding.

- A. all together
- B. altogether
- C. together
- D. together all

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：We have invited fifty people to attend my daughter's wedding.。选项：Aall together；Baltogether；Ctogether；Dtogether all。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** altogether是副词，意为「总共」，修饰数词或表示总结。B「altogether」符合「我们总共邀请了五十人」。A「all together」意为「一起、同时」，表方式或状态；C「together」意为「一起」；D「together all」不成立。故选B。
