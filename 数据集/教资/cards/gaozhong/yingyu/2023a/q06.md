【2023a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

There must be ________ empty or high-sound talk but more hard work in this highly competitive world.

- A. fewer
- B. no
- C. the least
- D. less

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：There must be ________ empty or high-sound talk but more hard work in this highly competitive world.。选项：Afewer；Bno；Cthe least；Dless。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 空高谈与实干对比，empty or high-sound talk为不可数，须用less（更少）而非fewer。D「less」符合。A「fewer」用于可数名词复数；B「no」语义为「没有」，与后半句more hard work的对比不成立；C「the least」表最高级，与more的对比关系不符。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
