【2023a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

It is said that federal government will raise interest rates by a quarter percentage point ** the 15th-consecutive year in an ________ to avoid inflation.

- A. effort
- B. intention
- C. assumption
- D. influence

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It is said that federal government will raise interest rates by a quarter percentage point ** the 15th-consecutive year in an ________ to avoid inflation.。选项：Aeffort；Bintention；Cassumption；Dinfluence。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** in an effort to do sth是固定搭配，意为「为做某事而作出努力」，符合「联邦政府连续第15年加息以避免通胀」。A「effort」符合。B「intention」意图、C「assumption」假设、D「influence」影响，三者均不构成in an...to这一表目的的结构。故选A。
