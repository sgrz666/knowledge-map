【2023a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

The driver checked the engine of his car carefully lest it ______ on the way.

- A. breaks down
- B. broke down
- C. would break down
- D. break down

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The driver checked the engine of his car carefully lest it ______ on the way.。选项：Abreaks down；Bbroke down；Cwould break down；Dbreak down。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** lest引导的目的状语从句需用虚拟语气should do，且should可省略，故动词用原形break down。D「break down」符合。A「breaks down」为陈述语气、B「broke down」为过去时、C「would break down」为过去将来，三者均不符合lest从句的虚拟语气要求。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
