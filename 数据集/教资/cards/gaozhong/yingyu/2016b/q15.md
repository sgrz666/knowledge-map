【2016b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

Teachers who adopt the __________ model for reading comprehension may start teaching a text by introducing new vocabulary and structures.

- A. parallel
- B. serial
- C. top-down
- D. bottom-up

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Teachers who adopt the __________ model for reading comprehension may start teaching a text by introducing new vocabulary and structures.。选项：Aparallel；Bserial；Ctop-down；Dbottom-up。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** bottom-up模式从词汇与语法结构等最小单位出发，故从教新词与结构入手。D「bottom-up」符合。C「top-down」从背景知识出发，方向相反。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇、英语阅读理解；关键词：according to the、author、infer、lexical、main idea、morpheme、morphology、passage
