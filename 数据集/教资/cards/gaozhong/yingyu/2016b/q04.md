【2016b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

All the flights __________ because of the hurricane they decided to take the train instead.

- A. had been canceled
- B. having canceled
- C. have been canceled
- D. having been canceled

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：All the flights __________ because of the hurricane they decided to take the train instead.。选项：Ahad been canceled；Bhaving canceled；Chave been canceled；Dhaving been canceled。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 前后两句无连接词，前半为原因状语，需用独立主格；cancel与flights为被动且先于decided发生，故用完成被动分词。D「having been canceled」符合。A「had been canceled」为完整谓语、B「having canceled」为主动、C「have been canceled」为限定形式。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
