【2016b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

The pair“__________ ” are dialectal synonyms in British and American English.

- A. tap and faucet
- B. stroll and trot
- C. liberty and freedom
- D. statesman and politician

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The pair“__________ ” are dialectal synonyms in British and American English.。选项：Atap and faucet；Bstroll and trot；Cliberty and freedom；Dstatesman and politician。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** tap（英式）与faucet（美式）均指水龙头，是英式与美式英语的方言同义词。A符合。B「stroll and trot」语义不同；C「liberty and freedom」非地域方言差异；D「statesman and politician」属褒贬色彩差异。故选A。
