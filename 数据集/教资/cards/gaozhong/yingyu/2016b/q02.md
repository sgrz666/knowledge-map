【2016b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

There is every sign that new Asian immigrants are willing to __________ with Americans.

- A. oblige
- B. absorb
- C. assimilate
- D. stabilize

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：There is every sign that new Asian immigrants are willing to __________ with Americans.。选项：Aoblige；Babsorb；Cassimilate；Dstabilize。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「新亚洲移民愿意与美国人___」，需表示融入同化。C「assimilate」意为同化、融入，与with连用表示与…融合。A「oblige」使有义务、B「absorb」吸收（及物，不与with连用）、D「stabilize」使稳定。故选C。
