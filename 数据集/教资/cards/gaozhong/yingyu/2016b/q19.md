【2016b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

Which ofthe following writing activities may be used to develop students’skill of planning?

- A. Editing their writing in groups.
- B. Self-checking punctuations in their writing.
- C. Sorting out ideas and putting them in order.
- D. Cross-checking the language in their writing.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which ofthe following writing activities may be used to develop students’skill of planning?。选项：AEditing their writing in groups.；BSelf-checking punctuations in their writing.；CSorting out ideas and putting them in order.；DCross-checking the language in their writing.。给定答案：C
