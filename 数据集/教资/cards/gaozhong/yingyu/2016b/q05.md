【2016b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

Which of the following shows how the fraction "7/9" reads in English?

- A. Seventh-nine.
- B. Sevenths-nine.
- C. Seven-ninth.
- D. Seven-ninths.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following shows how the fraction "7/9" reads in English?。选项：ASeventh-nine.；BSevenths-nine.；CSeven-ninth.；DSeven-ninths.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 分数读法为「分子基数词、分母序数词」，分子大于1时分母加-s。7/9读作seven-ninths。D符合。A、B把分子误用序数词；C未在分子大于1时给分母加-s。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇；关键词：lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary、word formation、构词、词汇
