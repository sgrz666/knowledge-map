【2021a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

In English teaching such mechanical activities as ______ may be helpful for teaching particular structures but they should not be overused.（ ）

- A. transformation and role play
- B. translation and word-matching
- C. gap-filling and topic discussion
- D. memorization and pattern drilling

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In English teaching such mechanical activities as ______ may be helpful for teaching particular structures but they should not be overused.（ ）。选项：Atransformation and role play；Btranslation and word-matching；Cgap-filling and topic discussion；Dmemorization and pattern drilling。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 机械性活动指不需真实交际、以重复记忆和形式操练为主的活动。D「memorization and pattern drilling」背诵与句型操练属典型机械活动。A含角色扮演、B含词义匹配、C含话题讨论，均含交际或意义加工成分。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
