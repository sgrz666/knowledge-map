【2021a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

In a sequence of two related utterances by two speakers the second utterance is always a response to the first. This is known as______.（ ）

- A. pair work
- B. pair practice
- C. adjacency pairs
- D. minimal pairs

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In a sequence of two related utterances by two speakers the second utterance is always a response to the first. This is known as______.（ ）。选项：Apair work；Bpair practice；Cadjacency pairs；Dminimal pairs。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 会话中相邻两个说话人的话语构成的功能对，如问候—问候、提问—回答，称相邻对。C「adjacency pairs」符合。A「pair work」是课堂两人活动、B「pair practice」是两人练习、D「minimal pairs」是最小对立对（语音学概念）。故选C。
