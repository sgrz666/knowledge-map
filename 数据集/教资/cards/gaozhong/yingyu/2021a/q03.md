【2021a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

Astronomer Heidi Hammer a proponent of science education conveys a passion for planetary science that her fascinated audience find ______.（ ）

- A. equivocal
- B. archaic
- C. timid
- D. infectious

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Astronomer Heidi Hammer a proponent of science education conveys a passion for planetary science that her fascinated audience find ______.（ ）。选项：Aequivocal；Barchaic；Ctimid；Dinfectious。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「天文学家Heidi Hammer对行星科学的热情令着迷的听众觉得___」，需表示感染力强。D「infectious」意为有感染力的，符合热情传递给他人的语义。A「equivocal」含混的、B「archaic」古老的、C「timid」胆怯的，均与「感染听众」不符。故选D。
