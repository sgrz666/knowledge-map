【2021a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

Little is known of Oliver’s childhood ______ at the factory at the early age of eight.（ ）

- A. suppose that he worked
- B. provided that he worked
- C. providing that he worked
- D. save that he began to work

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Little is known of Oliver’s childhood ______ at the factory at the early age of eight.（ ）。选项：Asuppose that he worked；Bprovided that he worked；Cproviding that he worked；Dsave that he began to work。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** save that是连词，意为「除了…以外」，符合「关于Oliver的童年所知甚少，除了他八岁就到工厂做工」。D「save that he began to work」符合。A「suppose that」假设、B「provided that」只要、C「providing that」只要，三者均与「除了」的语义不符。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
