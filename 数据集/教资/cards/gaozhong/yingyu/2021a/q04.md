The discoveries made by Nobel Prize winning geneticist Barbars McClintock are considered to be among the major ______ of 20th century biology science.（ ）

- A. proposals
- B. elaborations
- C. accomplishments
- D. deliberations

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
