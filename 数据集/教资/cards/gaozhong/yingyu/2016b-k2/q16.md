【2016b-k2 · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

It is suggested that lower-level EFL learners learn to read by reading _____materials.

- A. simple and authentic
- B. academic and authentic
- C. original and classical
- D. classical and authentic

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It is suggested that lower-level EFL learners learn to read by reading _____materials.。选项：Asimple and authentic；Bacademic and authentic；Coriginal and classical；Dclassical and authentic。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 低水平EFL学习者的阅读材料应兼具简单（难度适中）与真实（保留真实语料特征）。A「simple and authentic」符合。B「academic」材料难度过高、C「original and classical」与D「classical and authentic」中的classical语言难度大、时代感强，均不适合低水平学习者。故选A。
