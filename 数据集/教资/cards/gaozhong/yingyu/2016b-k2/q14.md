【2016b-k2 · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

When asking students to quickly run their eyes over a whole text to get the gist，we are training their skill of______.

- A. scanning
- B. mapping
- C. predicting
- D. skimming

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When asking students to quickly run their eyes over a whole text to get the gist，we are training their skill of______.。选项：Ascanning；Bmapping；Cpredicting；Dskimming。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 快速浏览全文获取大意是略读技能。D「skimming」符合。A「scanning」是快速查找特定信息、B「mapping」是图式化、C「predicting」是根据线索预测。故选D。
