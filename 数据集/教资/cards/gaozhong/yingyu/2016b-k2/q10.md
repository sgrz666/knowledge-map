【2016b-k2 · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Which of the following might be Jane's primary concern when she sincerely asks her friend“Does your farm contain 500 acres？”

- A. Quantity maxim.
- B. Quality maxim.
- C. Relation maxim.
- D. Manner maxim.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following might be Jane's primary concern when she sincerely asks her friend“Does your farm contain 500 acres？”。选项：AQuantity maxim.；BQuality maxim.；CRelation maxim.；DManner maxim.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** Jane真诚询问「你的农场有500英亩吗」，在真实语境中她的关注点在于「农场面积」这一信息与话题的相关性，即关系准则。C「Relation maxim」符合。A「Quantity maxim」关涉信息量多少、B「Quality maxim」关涉真实性、D「Manner maxim」关涉表达清晰，均不解释「为何问这个」。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
