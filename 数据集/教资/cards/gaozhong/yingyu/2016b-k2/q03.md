【2016b-k2 · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

In the United States，a（n）______general is the chief lawyer in a government or city department.

- A. senator
- B. solicitor
- C. councilor
- D. inspector

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In the United States，a（n）______general is the chief lawyer in a government or city department.。选项：Asenator；Bsolicitor；Ccouncilor；Dinspector。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 美国政府或市政部门的最高法律顾问称为solicitor general。B「solicitor」符合该专业术语。A「senator」是参议员（立法者）、C「councilor」是议员、D「inspector」是检查员，三者均非首席法律顾问。故选B。
