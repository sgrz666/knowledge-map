【2016b-k2 · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

All the flights______because of the hurricane，they decided to take the train instead.

- A. had been canceled
- B. having canceled
- C. have been canceled
- D. having been canceled

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：All the flights______because of the hurricane，they decided to take the train instead.。选项：Ahad been canceled；Bhaving canceled；Chave been canceled；Dhaving been canceled。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干前后两部分之间无连接词，前半句为原因状语，需用独立主格结构，且cancel与flights为被动关系，且发生在decided之前，故用完成式被动分词。D「having been canceled」符合。A「had been canceled」为完整谓语形式，缺少连词会造成句子结构错误；B「having canceled」为主动；C「have been canceled」为限定形式。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
