【2015a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

Through doing this the teacher will be able to ascertain the extent to __________the children understand what they are reading.

- A. how
- B. which
- C. that
- D. what

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Through doing this the teacher will be able to ascertain the extent to __________the children understand what they are reading.。选项：Ahow；Bwhich；Cthat；Dwhat。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** to the extent to which是固定结构，意为「到…的程度」，which引导定语从句修饰extent。B「which」符合。A「how」不能作介词to的宾语引导定语从句；C「that」不能紧接介词to；D「what」不引导定语从句。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
