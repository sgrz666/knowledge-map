【2015a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

__________ she heard her grandfather was born in Germany.

- A. That was from her mum
- B. It was her mum that
- C. It was from her mum that
- D. It was her mum whom

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：__________ she heard her grandfather was born in Germany.。选项：AThat was from her mum；BIt was her mum that；CIt was from her mum that；DIt was her mum whom。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 该句为强调句，强调被强调部分from her mum（来源状语），结构为It was from her mum that she heard...。C「It was from her mum that」符合。A「That was from her mum」不能构成强调结构；B「It was her mum that」漏掉介词from，无法表示「从妈妈那里」；D「It was her mum whom」同样漏掉from且whom用于指人作宾语。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
