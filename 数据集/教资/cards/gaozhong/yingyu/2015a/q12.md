【2015a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

The main objective of mechanical practice is to help learners to absorb thoroughly the __________of a language item.

- A. meaning
- B. function
- C. context
- D. form

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The main objective of mechanical practice is to help learners to absorb thoroughly the __________of a language item.。选项：Ameaning；Bfunction；Ccontext；Dform。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 机械性练习（mechanical practice）以重复、替换等方式操练语言形式，目的是让学生牢固掌握语言项目的形式。D「form」符合。A「meaning」意义、B「function」功能、C「context」语境，属于意义性练习和交际性练习的关注点。故选D。
