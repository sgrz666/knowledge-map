【2015a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

A systematic textbook evaluation is NOT to examine whether a textbook__________.

- A. covers all grammatical rules
- B. provides authentic language
- C. matches the needs of learners
- D. can help realize the objectives of a language program

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：A systematic textbook evaluation is NOT to examine whether a textbook__________.。选项：Acovers all grammatical rules；Bprovides authentic language；Cmatches the needs of learners；Dcan help realize the objectives of a language program。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教材评价关注语言真实性、学习者需求匹配、课程目标实现，而是否涵盖全部语法规则不是评价标准——语言学习不等于穷尽语法规则。A「covers all grammatical rules」是EXCEPT项。B、C、D均为合理的评价维度。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：学情分析与教材分析；关键词：学习基础、学情、学生基础、教材内容、教材分析、教材片段
