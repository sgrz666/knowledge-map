【2015a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

He immediately replied with an __________"YES" to the request that he attend the public hearing.

- A. effective
- B. eloquent
- C. emotional
- D. emphatic

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：He immediately replied with an __________"YES" to the request that he attend the public hearing.。选项：Aeffective；Beloquent；Cemotional；Demphatic。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「他立即以一声___的『是』回应了出席听证会的请求」，需表示语气强烈的。D「emphatic」意为强调的、语气强烈的，符合修饰YES表示郑重应允。A「effective」有效的、B「eloquent」雄辩的、C「emotional」情绪化的，均不描述应答的强调意味。故选D。
