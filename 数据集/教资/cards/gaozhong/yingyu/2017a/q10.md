【2017a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

The expression"As far as I know ..." suggests that people usually observe the Maxim of __________ in their daily conversations.

- A. Quantity
- B. Quality
- C. Relevance
- D. Manner

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The expression"As far as I know ..." suggests that people usually observe the Maxim of __________ in their daily conversations.。选项：AQuantity；BQuality；CRelevance；DManner。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「As far as I know」意为「据我所知」，是表明自己陈述有根据、未必完全确定的说法，体现的是质量准则（只说有把握的话）。B「Quality」符合。A「Quantity」关涉信息量、C「Relevance」关涉相关性、D「Manner」关涉清晰。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
