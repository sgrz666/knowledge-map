【2017a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

Though the government encourages foreign investment__________ investors are reluctant to commit fimds in the current climate situation in the country.

- A. potential
- B. affluent
- C. optimistic
- D. solid

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Though the government encourages foreign investment__________ investors are reluctant to commit fimds in the current climate situation in the country.。选项：Apotential；Baffluent；Coptimistic；Dsolid。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「政府鼓励外资，但___投资者在当前气候下不愿投入资金」，需表示「潜在的」。A「potential」意为潜在的，potential investors即潜在投资者。B「affluent」富裕的、C「optimistic」乐观的、D「solid」稳固的，均不修饰「尚未投资的投资者」。故选A。
