【2017a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

There is no need__________ to teach children how to behave.

- A. however
- B. whatsoever
- C. forever
- D. whenever

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：There is no need__________ to teach children how to behave.。选项：Ahowever；Bwhatsoever；Cforever；Dwhenever。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** whatsoever用于否定句或no need之后表示强调，意为「丝毫、根本」。B「whatsoever」符合「根本没必要教孩子如何举止」。A「however」表方式、C「forever」表时间、D「whenever」表时间。故选B。
