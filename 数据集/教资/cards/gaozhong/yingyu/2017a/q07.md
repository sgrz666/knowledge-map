【2017a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

__________advance seems to be following advance on almost a monthly basis.

- A. So rapid is the rate of progress that
- B. Rapid as the rate of progress is that
- C. So rapid is the rate of progress as
- D. Rapid as the rate of progress as

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：__________advance seems to be following advance on almost a monthly basis.。选项：ASo rapid is the rate of progress that；BRapid as the rate of progress is that；CSo rapid is the rate of progress as；DRapid as the rate of progress as。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** so...that引导结果状语从句时，若so+形容词置于句首则需倒装。A「So rapid is the rate of progress that」符合倒装结构。B「Rapid as...is that」中as引导让步与that搭配不当；C「as」不能引导结果从句；D「Rapid as...as」重复使用as。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
