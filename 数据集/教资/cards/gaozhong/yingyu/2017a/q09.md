【2017a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

What rhetoric device is used in the sentence "This is a successful failure"?

- A. Simile
- B. Metonymy
- C. Metaphor
- D. Oxymoron

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What rhetoric device is used in the sentence "This is a successful failure"?。选项：ASimile；BMetonymy；CMetaphor；DOxymoron。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「successful failure」（成功的失败）把语义相反的词并置构成矛盾修辞。D「Oxymoron」符合。A「Simile」明喻需like/as；B「Metonymy」转喻；C「Metaphor」暗喻，均不描述矛盾并置。故选D。
