【2017b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

The most suitable question type to check students´ comprehension and develop their critical thinking is __________.

- A. rhetorical questions
- B. referential questions
- C. close questions
- D. display questions

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The most suitable question type to check students´ comprehension and develop their critical thinking is __________.。选项：Arhetorical questions；Breferential questions；Cclose questions；Ddisplay questions。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 兼顾检查理解与发展批判性思维，需用答案开放、能引发思考的问题。B「referential questions」符合。A「rhetorical questions」不要求回答、C「close questions」答案唯一、D「display questions」答案教师已知。故选B。
