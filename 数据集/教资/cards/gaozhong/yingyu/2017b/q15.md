【2017b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

If a teacher asks students to make their own learning plan he/she is trying to develop their__________.

- A. cognitive strategy
- B. affective strategy
- C. communicative strategy
- D. metacognitive strategy

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher asks students to make their own learning plan he/she is trying to develop their__________.。选项：Acognitive strategy；Baffective strategy；Ccommunicative strategy；Dmetacognitive strategy。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 制定并监控个人学习计划属元认知策略。D「metacognitive strategy」符合。A「cognitive strategy」处理学习材料的具体方法、B「affective strategy」调节情绪、C「communicative strategy」克服交际障碍。故选D。
