【2017b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

What is the main rhetoric device used in "The Pentagon was divided on the air strike. "?

- A. Synecdoche.
- B. Metonymy.
- C. Metaphor.
- D. Oxymoron.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What is the main rhetoric device used in "The Pentagon was divided on the air strike. "?。选项：ASynecdoche.；BMetonymy.；CMetaphor.；DOxymoron.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「The Pentagon was divided on the air strike」中用五角大楼（建筑）代指美国国防部（机构），是转喻。B「Metonymy」符合。A「Synecdoche」提喻是部分代整体；C「Metaphor」暗喻；D「Oxymoron」矛盾修辞。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
