【2017b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Which inference in the brackets of the following sentences is a presupposition?

- A. Ede caught a trout. (Ede caught a fish.)
- B. Don' t sit on Carol' s bed. (Carol has a bed.)
- C. This blimp is over the house. (The house is under the blimp.)
- D. Coffee would keep me awake all night. (I don' t want coffee.)

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which inference in the brackets of the following sentences is a presupposition?。选项：AEde caught a trout. (Ede caught a fish.)；BDon' t sit on Carol' s bed. (Carol has a bed.)；CThis blimp is over the house. (The house is under the blimp.)；DCoffee would keep me awake all night. (I don' t want coffee.)。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 预设（presupposition）指句子成立所必需的前提，否定后仍成立。B「Don't sit on Carol's bed」预设「Carol有张床」——即使改为肯定句该前提依然成立。A「Ede caught a trout → Ede caught a fish」是蕴含（由前者推出后者）；C是同义改写；D是说话人的意图推断，非预设。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
