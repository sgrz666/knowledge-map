【2017b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

If a teacher asks students to talk about their hobbies in groups he/she is trying to encourage __________.

- A. peer correction
- B. peer feedback
- C. peer interaction
- D. peer assessment

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher asks students to talk about their hobbies in groups he/she is trying to encourage __________.。选项：Apeer correction；Bpeer feedback；Cpeer interaction；Dpeer assessment。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 让学生分组谈论爱好，是促使学生用英语真实交流，发展同伴互动。C「peer interaction」符合。A「peer correction」同伴纠错、B「peer feedback」同伴反馈、D「peer assessment」同伴互评，三者均以评价纠错为目的。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织、听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
