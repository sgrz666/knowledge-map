【2017b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

When a teacher tells the students that the word "dog" may imply "loyalty" he/she is teaching the __________of the word.

- A. denotative meaning
- B. collocative meaning
- C. conceptual meaning
- D. connotative meaning

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher tells the students that the word "dog" may imply "loyalty" he/she is teaching the __________of the word.。选项：Adenotative meaning；Bcollocative meaning；Cconceptual meaning；Dconnotative meaning。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** dog暗示「忠诚」，是概念意义之外的联想意义。D「connotative meaning」符合。A「denotative meaning」与C「conceptual meaning」均指字面所指；B「collocative meaning」是搭配意义。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
