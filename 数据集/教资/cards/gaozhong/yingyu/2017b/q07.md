【2017b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

I look back on this pleasant holiday in Beijing with __________ pleasure.

- A. anything but
- B. all but
- C. everything but
- D. nothing but

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：I look back on this pleasant holiday in Beijing with __________ pleasure.。选项：Aanything but；Ball but；Ceverything but；Dnothing but。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** nothing but意为「只不过、仅仅」，with nothing but pleasure即「满怀喜悦地」。D「nothing but」符合。A「anything but」意为「绝不是」，语义相反；B「all but」意为「几乎」；C「everything but」不构成固定搭配。故选D。
