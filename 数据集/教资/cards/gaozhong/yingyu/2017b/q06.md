【2017b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

Mr. Woods I am here just in case anything out of the ordinary __________.

- A. happens
- B. happen
- C. would happen
- D. will happen

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Mr. Woods I am here just in case anything out of the ordinary __________.。选项：Ahappens；Bhappen；Cwould happen；Dwill happen。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** in case引导的条件状语从句用一般现在时表将来。A「happens」符合。B「happen」主语是anything（单数），须用第三人称单数；C「would happen」与D「will happen」在in case从句中一般不用将来时。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
