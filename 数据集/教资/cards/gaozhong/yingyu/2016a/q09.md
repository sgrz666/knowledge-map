【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

When saying "It´ s noisy outside" to get someone to close the window the speaker intends to perform a(n) __________.

- A. direct speech act
- B. locutionary act
- C. indirect speech act
- D. perlocutionary act

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When saying "It´ s noisy outside" to get someone to close the window the speaker intends to perform a(n) __________.。选项：Adirect speech act；Blocutionary act；Cindirect speech act；Dperlocutionary act。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 说话人说「It's noisy outside」以描述事实的方式间接要求对方关窗，字面意义与言外之力不一致，属间接言语行为。C「indirect speech act」符合。A「direct speech act」要求字面与言外之力一致；B「locutionary act」指说话本身；D「perlocutionary act」指话语产生的效果。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
