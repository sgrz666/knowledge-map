【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Fluent and appropriate language use requires knowledge of__________ and this suggests that we should teach lexical chunks rather than single words.

- A. denotation
- B. connotation
- C. morphology
- D. collocation

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Fluent and appropriate language use requires knowledge of__________ and this suggests that we should teach lexical chunks rather than single words.。选项：Adenotation；Bconnotation；Cmorphology；Dcollocation。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 流利得体的语言使用需要掌握词与词之间的搭配关系。D「collocation」符合，故应教词块而非孤立单词。A「denotation」外延、B「connotation」内涵属词义层面；C「morphology」形态学关注构词与屈折。故选D。
