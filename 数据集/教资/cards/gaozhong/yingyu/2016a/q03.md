【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

The girls are afraid that being friendly to strangers could be misinterpreted by their __________neighbours.

- A. ever-present
- B. ever-presented
- C. ever-presenting
- D. ever-presently

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The girls are afraid that being friendly to strangers could be misinterpreted by their __________neighbours.。选项：Aever-present；Bever-presented；Cever-presenting；Dever-presently。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** ever-present（始终存在的）由副词ever与分词present构成复合形容词，符合「周围始终存在的邻居」。A「ever-present」符合。B「ever-presented」为被动形式、C「ever-presenting」为主动进行形式、D「ever-presently」为副词形式，均不能作定语修饰neighbours。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇；关键词：lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary、word formation、构词、词汇
