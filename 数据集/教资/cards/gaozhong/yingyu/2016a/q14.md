【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

When teaching students how to give appropriate responses to a congratulation or an apology the teacher is probably teaching at__________.

- A. lexical level
- B. sentence level
- C. grammatical level
- D. discourse level

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When teaching students how to give appropriate responses to a congratulation or an apology the teacher is probably teaching at__________.。选项：Alexical level；Bsentence level；Cgrammatical level；Ddiscourse level。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 对祝贺或道歉作恰当回应，涉及话语在语境中的得体性与前后语篇衔接，属语篇层面。D「discourse level」符合。A「lexical level」词汇、B「sentence level」句子、C「grammatical level」语法，均不能涵盖语境得体性这一维度。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学、词法与词汇；关键词：discourse、implicature、lexical、linguistic、morpheme、morphology、pragmatics、presupposition
