【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

Portfolios daily reports and speech delivering are typical means of__________.

- A. norm-referenced test
- B. criterion-referenced test
- C. summative assessment
- D. formative assessment

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Portfolios daily reports and speech delivering are typical means of__________.。选项：Anorm-referenced test；Bcriterion-referenced test；Csummative assessment；Dformative assessment。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 作品集、日常报告、演讲展示均属持续收集学习证据以促进学习的过程性评价手段。D「formative assessment」符合。A「norm-referenced test」与B「criterion-referenced test」是测试类型划分、C「summative assessment」在教学结束后进行，与持续收集的性质不符。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：学习评价方法与证据；关键词：作业、学习任务单、形成性、测试、测验、终结性、评价、评分
