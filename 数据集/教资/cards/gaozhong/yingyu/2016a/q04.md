【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

His presentation will show you can be used in other contexts.

- A. that you have observed
- B. that how you have observed
- C. how that you have observed
- D. how what you have observed

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：His presentation will show you can be used in other contexts.。选项：Athat you have observed；Bthat how you have observed；Chow that you have observed；Dhow what you have observed。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「他的演示将向你展示你观察到的东西如何能用于其他情境」，how引导宾语从句表方式，what you have observed作从句主语。D「how what you have observed」符合。A「that you have observed」缺how，无法表示方式；B「that how you have observed」两个引导词重复；C「how that you have observed」多出that。故选D。
