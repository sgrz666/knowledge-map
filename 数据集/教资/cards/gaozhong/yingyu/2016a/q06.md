【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

Which of the following is correct in its use of punctuation?

- A. The teacher asked "Who said ' Give me liberty or give me death' ?"
- B. The teacher asked "Who said 'Give me liberty or give me death?'"
- C. The teacher asked "Who said 'Give me liberty or give me death'"?
- D. The teacher asked "Who said 'Give me liberty or give me death'?"

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is correct in its use of punctuation?。选项：AThe teacher asked "Who said ' Give me liberty or give me death' ?"；BThe teacher asked "Who said 'Give me liberty or give me death?'"；CThe teacher asked "Who said 'Give me liberty or give me death'"?；DThe teacher asked "Who said 'Give me liberty or give me death'?"。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 标点使用规则：直接引语末的句号或问号应放在引号内；引语内又有引语时，内层引语用单引号。D项「The teacher asked "Who said 'Give me liberty or give me death'?"」中内层单引号引语完整、外层问号置于外层引号内，规范正确。A项问号置于引号外、B项把问号放入内层引语（内层是祈使式名言，不应用问号）、C项问号在外层引号之外，均不合规范。故选D。
