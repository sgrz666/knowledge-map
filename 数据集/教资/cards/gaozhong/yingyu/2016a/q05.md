【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

Many students start each term with an award check but by the time books are bought food is paid for and a bit of social life __________ it looks rather emaciated.

- A. lives
- B. lived
- C. was lived
- D. has lived

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Many students start each term with an award check but by the time books are bought food is paid for and a bit of social life __________ it looks rather emaciated.。选项：Alives；Blived；Cwas lived；Dhas lived。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 本句主句谓语为looks，by the time引导时间状语从句需用一般过去时表示将来某个时间点之前，lived可作不及物动词表示「维持生活、过日子」，a bit of social life lived（还能有点社交生活）符合语境。B「lived」符合。A「lives」时态与主句不符、C「was lived」为被动、D「has lived」为现在完成，均不协调。故选B。
