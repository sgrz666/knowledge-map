【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

That a Japanese child adopted at birth by an American couple will grow up speaking English indicates __________ of human language.

- A. duality
- B. cultural transmission
- C. arbitrariness
- D. cognitive creativity

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：That a Japanese child adopted at birth by an American couple will grow up speaking English indicates __________ of human language.。选项：Aduality；Bcultural transmission；Carbitrariness；Dcognitive creativity。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 日本婴儿由美国家庭收养后成长为说英语者，说明语言不是生物遗传而是通过文化环境传递习得的，体现语言的文化传递性。B「cultural transmission」符合。A「duality」二重性、C「arbitrariness」任意性、D「cognitive creativity」认知创造性，均不描述语言依赖文化环境习得这一特性。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语国家文化与文学、听说与交际教学；关键词：american、audio-lingual、britain、communicative、listening、literature、novel、oral
