【2016a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

As Alice believed him to be a man of integrity she refused to consider the possibility that his statement was__________.

- A. in'elevant
- B. facetious
- C. fictitious
- D. illogical

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：As Alice believed him to be a man of integrity she refused to consider the possibility that his statement was__________.。选项：Ain'elevant；Bfacetious；Cfictitious；Dillogical。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「Alice相信他是正直之人，因此拒绝考虑他的陈述是___的可能」，需要一个与integrity（正直）相反的贬义形容词。C「fictitious」意为虚构的、不真实的，符合「(他的话)可能是编造的」。A「in'elevant」（irrelevant的变体）不相关、B「facetious」诙谐的、D「illogical」不合逻辑的，三者与integrity的对立面均不如fictitious直接。故选C。
