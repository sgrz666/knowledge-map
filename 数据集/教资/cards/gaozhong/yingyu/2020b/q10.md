【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Which of the following describes the language of an individual speaker with its unique characteristics?

- A. Idiolect.
- B. Taboo.
- C. Regional dialect.
- D. Social dialect.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following describes the language of an individual speaker with its unique characteristics?。选项：AIdiolect.；BTaboo.；CRegional dialect.；DSocial dialect.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 个人独特的语言特征总称idiolect（个人方言/个人语型）。A「Idiolect」符合。B「Taboo」禁忌语、C「Regional dialect」地域方言、D「Social dialect」社会方言，均指群体而非个体的语言特征。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
