【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

Which of the following best describes the relation between “piece” and “peace”?

- A. Synonymy.
- B. Homonymy.
- C. Antonymy.
- D. Hyponymy.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following best describes the relation between “piece” and “peace”?。选项：ASynonymy.；BHomonymy.；CAntonymy.；DHyponymy.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** piece（片、块）与peace（和平）拼写不同但读音完全相同，属同音异形异义关系。B「Homonymy」符合。A「Synonymy」同义、C「Antonymy」反义、D「Hyponymy」上下义，均不描述同音关系。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
