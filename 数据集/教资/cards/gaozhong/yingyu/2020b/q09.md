【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Which of the following refers to “the part of input that has been internalized by learners”?

- A. Feedback.
- B. Output.
- C. Intake.
- D. Wash back.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following refers to “the part of input that has been internalized by learners”?。选项：AFeedback.；BOutput.；CIntake.；DWash back.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** intake指被学习者内化吸收的那部分输入。C「Intake」符合。A「Feedback」反馈、B「Output」输出、D「Wash back」反拨效应，均不描述已内化的输入。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：二语习得与学习策略；关键词：acquisition、interlanguage、learning strateg、motivation、transfer、二语、语言习得
