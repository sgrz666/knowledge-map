【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第1题】

/s/ and /z/ can be distinguished by the ________.

- A. place of articulation
- B. state of tongue
- C. state of vocal cords
- D. manner of articulation

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：/s/ and /z/ can be distinguished by the ________.。选项：Aplace of articulation；Bstate of tongue；Cstate of vocal cords；Dmanner of articulation。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** /s/与/z/发音部位与方式均相同（均为齿龈摩擦音），区别在于声带是否振动：/s/为清音、/z/为浊音。C「state of vocal cords」符合。A「place of articulation」二者相同、B「state of tongue」不区分二者、D「manner of articulation」二者相同。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
