【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

She was not impressed ________ by the story Paul shared with her for she had already heard of it.

- A. in the least
- B. at the most
- C. least of all
- D. for the most

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：She was not impressed ________ by the story Paul shared with her for she had already heard of it.。选项：Ain the least；Bat the most；Cleast of all；Dfor the most。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** not in the least是固定搭配，意为「一点也不」，符合「她一点也不觉得印象深刻」。A「in the least」符合。B「at the most」至多、C「least of all」尤其不、D「for the most」不成立。故选A。
