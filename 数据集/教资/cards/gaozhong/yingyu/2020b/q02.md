【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

The word “realization” consists of ________ syllables and ________ morphemes.

- A. five; five
- B. five; four
- C. four; three
- D. four; four

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The word “realization” consists of ________ syllables and ________ morphemes.。选项：Afive; five；Bfive; four；Cfour; three；Dfour; four。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** realization的音节为re-a-li-za-tion共四个音节；语素为real（词根）+ -ize（动词后缀）+ -ation（名词后缀）共三个语素。C「four; three」符合。A、B把音节数误作五个，D把语素数误作四个。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系、词法与词汇；关键词：consonant、intonation、lexical、morpheme、morphology、phoneme、phonetic、phonology
