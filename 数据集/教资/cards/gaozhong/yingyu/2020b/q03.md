【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

Which of the following is least associated with newspaper publishing?

- A. Proofreading.
- B. Editorial.
- C. Censorship.
- D. Citizenship.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is least associated with newspaper publishing?。选项：AProofreading.；BEditorial.；CCensorship.；DCitizenship.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 校对、社论、审查均与报刊出版直接相关；而citizenship（公民身份/公民性）属政治法律概念，与报刊出版关联最弱。D「Citizenship」符合「least associated」。A「Proofreading」校对、B「Editorial」社论、C「Censorship」审查均为出版环节。故选D。
