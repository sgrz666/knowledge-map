【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

If the focus is placed on ________ students are supposed to go through the stages of drafting receiving feedback and revising before submitting the final version of their writings.

- A. product
- B. process
- C. genre
- D. format

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If the focus is placed on ________ students are supposed to go through the stages of drafting receiving feedback and revising before submitting the final version of their writings.。选项：Aproduct；Bprocess；Cgenre；Dformat。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 学生经历起草、接受反馈、修改再定稿的完整过程，是过程写作法的核心特征。B「process」符合。A关注成品、C关注文体特征、D指格式。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
