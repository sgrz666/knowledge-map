【2020b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

He is helpless under such circumstances ________.

- A. however brilliant a mind he may have
- B. however a brilliant mind he may have
- C. however brilliant a mind may he have
- D. however a brilliant mind may he have

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：He is helpless under such circumstances ________.。选项：Ahowever brilliant a mind he may have；Bhowever a brilliant mind he may have；Chowever brilliant a mind may he have；Dhowever a brilliant mind may he have。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** however引导让步状语从句时，其后紧跟形容词，语序为however + adj. + a/an + n. + 主谓：however brilliant a mind he may have。A符合。B与D把冠词位置放错、C错误地倒装了从句主谓。故选A。
