【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

We'd better make a clear __________between competence and performance for the convince of our discussion.

- A. separation
- B. division
- C. distinction
- D. difference

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：We'd better make a clear __________between competence and performance for the convince of our discussion.。选项：Aseparation；Bdivision；Cdistinction；Ddifference。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** make a distinction between A and B是固定搭配，意为「区分A与B」，符合「为便于讨论需明确区分语言能力与语言表现」。C「distinction」符合。A「separation」侧重分离、B「division」侧重划分、D「difference」侧重差异本身，均不与make a...between构成该术语性搭配。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
