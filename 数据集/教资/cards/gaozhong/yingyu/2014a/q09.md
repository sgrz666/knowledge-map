【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

What maxim is flouted in such extreme examples of tautologies as "Boys are boys" and"Lies are lies"?

- A. The maxim of quantity.
- B. The maxim of quality.
- C. The maxim of relevance.
- D. The maxim of manner.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What maxim is flouted in such extreme examples of tautologies as "Boys are boys" and"Lies are lies"?。选项：AThe maxim of quantity.；BThe maxim of quality.；CThe maxim of relevance.；DThe maxim of manner.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「Boys are boys」「Lies are lies」这类同义反复（tautology）没有提供任何新信息，违反的是数量准则——数量准则要求提供恰好所需的信息量，而重复已知内容使信息量为零。A「The maxim of quantity」符合。B「质量准则」关涉真实、C「关联准则」关涉切题、D「方式准则」关涉清晰，均不涉及信息量不足。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
