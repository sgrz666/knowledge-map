【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

Criticism and self-criticism are necessary __________they may help us to find out and correct our mistakes.

- A. by that
- B. at that
- C. on that
- D. in that

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Criticism and self-criticism are necessary __________they may help us to find out and correct our mistakes.。选项：Aby that；Bat that；Con that；Din that。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** in that是固定连词，意为「因为、在于」，用于引导原因状语从句。D「in that」符合「批评与自我批评是必要的，因为它们能帮助我们找出并改正错误」。A「by that」、B「at that」、C「on that」均不构成表示原因的连词。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
