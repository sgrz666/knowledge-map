【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

__________enough time and money the investigators would have been able to discover more evidence in this case.

- A. Giving
- B. Given
- C. To give
- D. Being given

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：__________enough time and money the investigators would have been able to discover more evidence in this case.。选项：AGiving；BGiven；CTo give；DBeing given。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** Given可作介词，意为「考虑到、如果有」，Given enough time and money即「如果有足够的时间和金钱」，构成过去分词短语作条件状语。B「Given」符合。A「Giving」表主动进行、C「To give」表目的、D「Being given」表被动进行，三者均不能构成条件状语表达「如果有」。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
