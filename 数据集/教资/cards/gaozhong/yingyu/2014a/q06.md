【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

It was not until dawn__________ the snowcapped peak in the remote area in Tibet.

- A. that they sighted
- B. did they sight
- C. that they did not sight
- D. had they sighted

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It was not until dawn__________ the snowcapped peak in the remote area in Tibet.。选项：Athat they sighted；Bdid they sight；Cthat they did not sight；Dhad they sighted。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 该句为强调句：It was not until dawn that they sighted the snowcapped peak，被强调部分是时间状语not until dawn，其后用that连接。A「that they sighted」符合。B「did they sight」缺少that，不能构成强调句；C「that they did not sight」多出not，与句首的not until重复导致语义反转；D「had they sighted」时态与结构均不符。故选A。
