【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Sociolinguists study varieties of language and accordingly will be interested in the analysis of all of the following EXCEPT__________.

- A. regional dialect
- B. register
- C. figures of speech
- D. social dialect

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Sociolinguists study varieties of language and accordingly will be interested in the analysis of all of the following EXCEPT__________.。选项：Aregional dialect；Bregister；Cfigures of speech；Dsocial dialect。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 社会语言学研究语言的社会变异，包括地域方言（A）、语域（B）、社会方言（D）。C「figures of speech」修辞格属修辞学或文体学的研究对象，研究的是语言表达的修辞效果，不属语言变体范畴。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
