【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

Which of the following does a teacher want his/her students to develop if he/she guides them to take notes using key words abbreviations and symbols?

- A. Cultural awareness.
- B. Language awareness.
- C. Learning strategies.
- D. Language knowledge.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following does a teacher want his/her students to develop if he/she guides them to take notes using key words abbreviations and symbols?。选项：ACultural awareness.；BLanguage awareness.；CLearning strategies.；DLanguage knowledge.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 引导学生用关键词、缩写和符号做笔记，是在教授具体的学习方法，属学习策略的培养。C「Learning strategies」符合。A「Cultural awareness」文化意识、B「Language awareness」语言意识、D「Language knowledge」语言知识，均不描述做笔记技巧这一方法论层面的内容。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：二语习得与学习策略；关键词：acquisition、interlanguage、learning strateg、motivation、transfer、二语、语言习得
