【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

In a listening activity students are asked to note down the time and places of events. The aim of this activity is to develop the skill of__________.

- A. listening for gist
- B. listening for specific information
- C. listening for structure
- D. listening for vocabulary

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In a listening activity students are asked to note down the time and places of events. The aim of this activity is to develop the skill of__________.。选项：Alistening for gist；Blistening for specific information；Clistening for structure；Dlistening for vocabulary。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 让学生记录事件的时间与地点，是抓取特定的事实细节。B「listening for specific information」符合。A「listening for gist」是把握大意、C「listening for structure」关注意篇结构、D「listening for vocabulary」关注词汇，均不描述抓取时间地点这类具体信息。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、词法与词汇；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、lexical、listening、morpheme、morphology、oral、role play
