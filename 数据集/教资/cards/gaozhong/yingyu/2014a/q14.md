【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

If a teacher gives commands in English and asks students to show understanding by action or gestures he/she is most probably using__________.

- A. Communicative Approach
- B. Audio-lingual Approach
- C. Grammar Translation Method
- D. Total Physical Response

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher gives commands in English and asks students to show understanding by action or gestures he/she is most probably using__________.。选项：ACommunicative Approach；BAudio-lingual Approach；CGrammar Translation Method；DTotal Physical Response。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师用英语发指令、学生以动作或手势表示理解，是全身反应法（TPR）的典型做法。D「Total Physical Response」符合。A「Communicative Approach」以交际任务为核心、B「Audio-lingual Approach」以句型操练为主、C「Grammar Translation Method」以翻译和语法讲解为主，均不采用「指令—动作」的回应方式。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、audio-lingual、clause、communicative、gerund、grammar、listening、oral
