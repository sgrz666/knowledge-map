【2014a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

If a teacher starts with language examples and guides students to work out the rules he/ she is using the __________ method.

- A. deductive
- B. conducive
- C. inductive
- D. constructive

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher starts with language examples and guides students to work out the rules he/ she is using the __________ method.。选项：Adeductive；Bconducive；Cinductive；Dconstructive。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 从语言实例出发、引导学生自行总结规则，是归纳法。C「inductive」符合。A「deductive」演绎法先给规则再举例，方向相反；B「conducive」意为「有助于…的」，不是教学法术语；D「constructive」指建构性的，也不指该教学方法。故选C。
