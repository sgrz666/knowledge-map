【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

Which of the following words contains an inflectional morpheme?

- A. Disappear.
- B. Blacken.
- C. Oxen.
- D. Anti-pollution.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following words contains an inflectional morpheme?。选项：ADisappear.；BBlacken.；COxen.；DAnti-pollution.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 屈折语素（inflectional morpheme）只改变词的语法形式而不改变词性，oxen中的-en是古英语遗留的复数屈折形式。C「Oxen」符合。A「Disappear」含派生前缀dis-、B「Blacken」含派生后缀-en（使…变黑，改变词性）、D「Anti-pollution」含派生前缀anti-，三者均为派生语素。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇；关键词：lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary、word formation、构词、词汇
