【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

If a teacher asks students to collect compare and analyze certain sentence patterns he/she aims at developing students'__________.

- A. discourse awareness
- B. cultural awareness
- C. strategic competence
- D. linguistic competence

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher asks students to collect compare and analyze certain sentence patterns he/she aims at developing students'__________.。选项：Adiscourse awareness；Bcultural awareness；Cstrategic competence；Dlinguistic competence。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 收集、比较、分析句型属于对语言形式本身的观察归纳，发展的是语言能力。D「linguistic competence」符合。A「discourse awareness」语篇意识、B「cultural awareness」文化意识、C「strategic competence」交际策略能力，均不描述对句型的观察分析。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
