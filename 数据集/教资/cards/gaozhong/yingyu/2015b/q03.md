【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

You'll find this Travel Guide to be of great__________ in helping you and your children to get around Malaysia.

- A. cost
- B. price
- C. value
- D. expenditure

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：You'll find this Travel Guide to be of great__________ in helping you and your children to get around Malaysia.。选项：Acost；Bprice；Cvalue；Dexpenditure。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** be of great value in doing sth是固定表达，意为「在做…方面很有价值」。C「value」符合。A「cost」成本、B「price」价格、D「expenditure」支出，三者均不构成of great...in helping这一评价性表达。故选C。
