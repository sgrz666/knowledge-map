【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

/s/and/θ/can be distinguished by__________.

- A. manner of articulation
- B. place of articulation
- C. vibration of the vocal cords
- D. aspiration of articulation

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：/s/and/θ/can be distinguished by__________.。选项：Amanner of articulation；Bplace of articulation；Cvibration of the vocal cords；Daspiration of articulation。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** /s/与/θ/均为清辅音，发音方式同为摩擦音，区别在于发音部位：/s/为齿龈音（舌尖靠近上齿龈），/θ/为齿间音（舌尖置于上下齿之间）。B「place of articulation」符合。A「manner of articulation」二者相同；C「vibration of the vocal cords」二者均为清音不带声；D「aspiration」不区分二者。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
