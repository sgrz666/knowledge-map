【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

When the train __________ all the school students were surprised to see that the Carlisle team had one man only.

- A. pulled down
- B. pulled on
- C. pulled off
- D. pulled in

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When the train __________ all the school students were surprised to see that the Carlisle team had one man only.。选项：Apulled down；Bpulled on；Cpulled off；Dpulled in。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「当火车___，所有学生都惊讶地发现Carlisle队只有一个人」，需表示火车进站。D「pulled in」意为（火车）进站、驶入。A「pulled down」拆除、B「pulled on」穿上、C「pulled off」成功完成或靠边停下，均不表示火车进站。故选D。
