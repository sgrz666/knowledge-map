【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

The sense relation of the following pair of sentences is that__________.
X: Mary's pet cat was stolen.
Y: Marry has a pet cat.

- A. X entails Y
- B. X presupposes Y
- C. X is inconsistent with Y
- D. X is synonymous with Y

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The sense relation of the following pair of sentences is that__________.
X: Mary's pet cat was stolen.
Y: Marry has a pet cat.。选项：AX entails Y；BX presupposes Y；CX is inconsistent with Y；DX is synonymous with Y。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** Y（Mary有只宠物猫）是X（Mary的宠物猫被偷了）得以成立的前提——若X为真，则Y必为真；若Y为假，则X无从谈起。这属预设关系。B「X presupposes Y」符合。A「entails」蕴含要求由X必然推出Y，而此处Y先于X存在而非被X推出；C「inconsistent」矛盾、D「synonymous」同义，均不成立。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
