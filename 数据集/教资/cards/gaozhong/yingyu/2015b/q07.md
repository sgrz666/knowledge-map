【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

He had no time and energy to play with his children or shop with his wife bu! he__________ home a regular salary.

- A. did bring
- B. does bring
- C. did get
- D. does get

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：He had no time and energy to play with his children or shop with his wife bu! he__________ home a regular salary.。选项：Adid bring；Bdoes bring；Cdid get；Ddoes get。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** did用于强调谓语动词，表示「确实」，与后半句的对比语气相符；bring home a salary 意为「把薪水带回家」。A「did bring」符合。C「did get」虽也为强调，但get home a salary的搭配不如bring home a salary自然（bring home意为「带回家」）。B「does bring」与D「does get」时态为现在时，与句首的had no time的过去时不一致。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
