【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

When a teacher says to the whole class "Stand up and act out the dialogue" he/she is playing the role of a (n)__________.

- A. monitor
- B. organizer
- C. assessor
- D. prompter

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher says to the whole class "Stand up and act out the dialogue" he/she is playing the role of a (n)__________.。选项：Amonitor；Borganizer；Cassessor；Dprompter。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师对全班发出「Stand up and act out the dialogue」的指令，是在发动组织课堂活动，扮演组织者。B「organizer」符合。A「monitor」监督、C「assessor」评价、D「prompter」在卡壳时提示，均非布置活动的角色。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
