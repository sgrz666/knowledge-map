【2015b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

Skill-integrated activities allow teachers to build in more __________ into a lesson for the
range of activities will be wider.

- A. certainty
- B. simplicity
- C. variety
- D. accuracy

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Skill-integrated activities allow teachers to build in more __________ into a lesson for the
range of activities will be wider.。选项：Acertainty；Bsimplicity；Cvariety；Daccuracy。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 技能综合活动因涉及的活动类型更广，能让课堂融入更多变化。C「variety」符合。A「certainty」确定性、B「simplicity」简单性、D「accuracy」准确性，均不描述活动类型丰富带来的效果。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语言技能综合教学；关键词：post-reading、pre-reading、reading lesson、scanning、skimming、writing lesson、语言技能
