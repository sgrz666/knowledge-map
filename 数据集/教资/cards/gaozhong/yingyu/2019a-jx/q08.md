【2019a-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

In fact，they would rather have left for London in Birmingham.

- A. to stay
- B. in order to stay
- C. than have stayed
- D. instead of having stayed

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In fact，they would rather have left for London in Birmingham.。选项：Ato stay；Bin order to stay；Cthan have stayed；Dinstead of having stayed。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** would rather have done A than have done B是固定结构，前后须平行使用完成式不定式。C「than have stayed」符合。A「to stay」与B「in order to stay」为不定式，不能构成该平行结构；D「instead of having stayed」为介词短语，同样不构成平行。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
