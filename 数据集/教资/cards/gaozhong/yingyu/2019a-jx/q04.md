【2019a-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

Smoking heavily at home will expose children to amount of smoke， endangering their health.

- A. multiple
- B. surplus
- C. durable
- D. excessive

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Smoking heavily at home will expose children to amount of smoke， endangering their health.。选项：Amultiple；Bsurplus；Cdurable；Dexcessive。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「在家大量吸烟会使儿童接触___量的烟雾」，需表示过量的。D「excessive」符合excessive amount of smoke（过量烟雾）。A「multiple」多个的、B「surplus」剩余的、C「durable」持久的，均不描述「量过大」这一含义。故选D。
