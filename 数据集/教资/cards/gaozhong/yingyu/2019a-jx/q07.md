【2019a-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

If he had fought in the First World War，he might have returned .

- A. a different man
- B. with a different man
- C. as a different man
- D. to be a different man

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If he had fought in the First World War，he might have returned .。选项：Aa different man；Bwith a different man；Cas a different man；Dto be a different man。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** as a different man意为「作为一个不同的人（归来）」，as表身份，符合「若他参加过一战，归来时可能已判若两人」。C「as a different man」符合。A「a different man」缺介词as，无法表示身份；B「with a different man」表伴随，语义变成带了另一个人；D「to be a different man」为不定式，与return搭配不当。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
