【2019a-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

Which of the following pairs of words are gradable antonyms？

- A. Buy and sell.
- B. Big and small.
- C. Male and female.
- D. Red and green.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following pairs of words are gradable antonyms？。选项：ABuy and sell.；BBig and small.；CMale and female.；DRed and green.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 层级反义词（gradable antonyms）指可在两极之间存在中间程度的反义对。B「Big and small」符合——大与小之间可有「中等」等程度差异。A「Buy and sell」是反向关系、C「Male and female」是互补反义（无中间态）、D「Red and green」是同类并列而非反义。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
