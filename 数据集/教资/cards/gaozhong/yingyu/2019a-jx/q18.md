【2019a-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following are controlled activities in an English class？—______ .

- A. Reporting，role-play and games
- B. Reading aloud，dictation and translation
- C. Role-play，problem solving and discussion
- D. Information exchange，narration and interview

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following are controlled activities in an English class？—______ .。选项：AReporting，role-play and games；BReading aloud，dictation and translation；CRole-play，problem solving and discussion；DInformation exchange，narration and interview。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 控制性活动指教师高度掌控、答案限定、以形式准确为目标的活动。B「Reading aloud, dictation and translation」朗读、听写、翻译均属控制性活动。A、C、D三项含角色扮演、讨论、访谈等开放性活动。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：教学活动与问题链设计、语言技能综合教学；关键词：post-reading、pre-reading、reading lesson、scanning、skimming、writing lesson、教学环节、教学设计
