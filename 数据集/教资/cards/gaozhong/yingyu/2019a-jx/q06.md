【2019a-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

Naturally，she that once there was a new film everybody would be eager to go and see it.

- A. had assumed
- B. assumed
- C. has assumed
- D. was assuming

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Naturally，she that once there was a new film everybody would be eager to go and see it.。选项：Ahad assumed；Bassumed；Chas assumed；Dwas assuming。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** naturally与once引导的从句提示这是叙述过去的一般情况，用一般过去时。B「assumed」符合。A「had assumed」过去完成时缺少参照的过去时间点；C「has assumed」现在完成时与过去语境不符；D「was assuming」进行时表暂时性动作，不合语境。故选B。
