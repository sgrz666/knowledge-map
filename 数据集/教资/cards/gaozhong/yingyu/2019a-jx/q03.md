【2019a-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

In the economic established recently， more progress has been made by the European countries in harmonizing their countries.

- A. regulation
- B. climate
- C. circumstance
- D. requirement

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In the economic established recently， more progress has been made by the European countries in harmonizing their countries.。选项：Aregulation；Bclimate；Ccircumstance；Drequirement。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「在最近建立的经济___中，欧洲国家在协调其国家___方面取得更多进展」，经济regulation（规制/监管）是常与harmonize（协调统一）搭配的对象。A「regulation」符合。B「climate」气候、C「circumstance」环境、D「requirement」要求，均不与harmonize构成「协调各国监管」的固定表述。故选A。
