【2019a-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

What kind of speech act is performed in utterance“come round on Saturday” when it is said as an invitation rather than a demand？

- A. Direct speech act
- B. Locutionary act
- C. Indirect speech act
- D. Perlocutionary act

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What kind of speech act is performed in utterance“come round on Saturday” when it is said as an invitation rather than a demand？。选项：ADirect speech act；BLocutionary act；CIndirect speech act；DPerlocutionary act。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「Come round on Saturday」以祈使形式表达邀请，字面形式与施事意图不同，属间接言语行为。C「Indirect speech act」符合。A「Direct speech act」要求字面与意图一致；B「Locutionary act」指说话本身；D「Perlocutionary act」指效果。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
