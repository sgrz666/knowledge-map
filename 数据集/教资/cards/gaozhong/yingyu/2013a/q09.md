【2013a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

The phenomenon of“ （ ）”may be the factor to cause the fewest changes in the English language in recent years.

- A. moving toward greater informality
- B. the influence of American English
- C. the influence of science and technology
- D. the conflicts between or among nations

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The phenomenon of“ （ ）”may be the factor to cause the fewest changes in the English language in recent years.。选项：Amoving toward greater informality；Bthe influence of American English；Cthe influence of science and technology；Dthe conflicts between or among nations。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干问「___这一现象可能是近年英语变化最少的原因」——需要找影响最弱的因素。国家间的冲突（D「the conflicts between or among nations」）主要在特定历史时期（如二战后民族独立浪潮）影响英语，不构成持续而广泛的演变动因；而走向非正式化（A）、美式英语影响（B）、科技影响（C）都是近年最持续且显著的因素。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语国家文化与文学；关键词：american、britain、literature、novel、poet、shakespeare、文学、英美
