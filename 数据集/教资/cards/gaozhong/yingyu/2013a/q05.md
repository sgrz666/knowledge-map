【2013a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

Last Sunday she came to visit us out of the blue. The italicized phrase means（ ）.

- A. unexpectly
- B. unhappily
- C. untidily
- D. unofficially

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Last Sunday she came to visit us out of the blue. The italicized phrase means（ ）.。选项：Aunexpectly；Bunhappily；Cuntidily；Dunofficially。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** out of the blue是习语，意为「突然地、出乎意料地」，对应unexpectedly。A「unexpectly」为unexpectedly的拼写变体，符合语义（来访是突然的）。B「unhappily」不高兴地、C「untidily」不整洁地、D「unofficially」非正式地，均与该习语的含义无关。故选A。
