【2013a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

The newly-married couple wanted a restaurant that services good food and has a bit of （ ）as well.

- A. conditions
- B. situations
- C. environment
- D. atmosphere

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The newly-married couple wanted a restaurant that services good food and has a bit of （ ）as well.。选项：Aconditions；Bsituations；Cenvironment；Datmosphere。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 餐厅除了食物好，还需有「氛围」，atmosphere专指场所给人带来的整体气氛感受。D「atmosphere」符合。A「conditions」指条件状况、B「situations」指处境、C「environment」指自然环境或大环境，三者均不用于描述餐厅的气氛格调。故选D。
