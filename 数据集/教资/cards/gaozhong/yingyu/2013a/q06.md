【2013a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

—What happened to the glass?—（ ）.

- A. The glass is broken.
- B. The glass was broken.
- C. Tom broke the glass.
- D. Tom has the glass.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：—What happened to the glass?—（ ）.。选项：AThe glass is broken.；BThe glass was broken.；CTom broke the glass.；DTom has the glass.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 问「What happened to the glass?」，答句应以「玻璃」为主语说明其遭遇，且happened是过去时，回答需用过去时。B「The glass was broken」符合。A「The glass is broken」用现在时，与问句的过去时不一致；C「Tom broke the glass」虽语法正确但把主语换成了Tom，未承接问句的主语焦点；D「Tom has the glass」答非所问。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
