【2013a · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

“It seems that she was there at the conference.”The sentence means that（ ）.

- A. She seems to be there at the conference.
- B. She seemed to be there at the conference.
- C. She seems to have been there at the conference.
- D. She seems to being there at the conference.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：“It seems that she was there at the conference.”The sentence means that（ ）.。选项：AShe seems to be there at the conference.；BShe seemed to be there at the conference.；CShe seems to have been there at the conference.；DShe seems to being there at the conference.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「It seems that she was there at the conference」转化为简单句时，从句的过去时was需变为不定式的完成式to have been，以表示过去。C「She seems to have been there at the conference」符合。A「to be there」用一般式，不能表示过去；B「She seemed to be」把seems误改为过去时；D「to being」形式错误。故选C。
