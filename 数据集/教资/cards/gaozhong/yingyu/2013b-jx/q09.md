【2013b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

he sense relation of the following pair of sentences (see X and Y) is（ ）. X. Mary has been to Japan. Y. Mary has been to Asia.

- A. X ventails Y
- B. X presupposes Y
- C. X is inconsistent with Y
- D. X is synonymous with Y

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：he sense relation of the following pair of sentences (see X and Y) is（ ）. X. Mary has been to Japan. Y. Mary has been to Asia.。选项：AX ventails Y；BX presupposes Y；CX is inconsistent with Y；DX is synonymous with Y。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** X（Mary去过日本）可推出Y（Mary去过亚洲），因为日本属于亚洲，由X必然得出Y，故X蕴含Y。A「X entails Y」符合（拼写变体ventails即entails）。B「presupposes」预设关系要求Y是X成立的前提，此处方向相反；C「inconsistent with」矛盾关系不成立；D「synonymous with」同义关系要求互相蕴含，而Y不能推出X。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
