【2013b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

Over the past ten years China has ( ) a series of great changes in its infrastructure and economy.

- A. witnessed
- B. found
- C. occurred
- D. taken

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Over the past ten years China has ( ) a series of great changes in its infrastructure and economy.。选项：Awitnessed；Bfound；Coccurred；Dtaken。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「过去十年中国___了基础设施与经济的一系列巨变」，需表示「见证、经历」。A「witnessed」符合，中国作为地域或时代主体「见证」了变化。B「found」发现、C「occurred」发生（不及物，主语应为事件本身，不能带宾语changes）、D「taken」语义不符。故选A。
