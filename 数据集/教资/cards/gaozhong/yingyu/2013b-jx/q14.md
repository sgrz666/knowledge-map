【2013b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

If a teacher attempts to implement the top-down model to teach listening he/she is likely to present（ ）.

- A. new words after playing the tape
- B. new words before playing the tape
- C. background information after playing the tape
- D. background information before playing the tape

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher attempts to implement the top-down model to teach listening he/she is likely to present（ ）.。选项：Anew words after playing the tape；Bnew words before playing the tape；Cbackground information after playing the tape；Dbackground information before playing the tape。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 自上而下（top-down）听力模式强调利用背景知识与语境预测意义，故播放录音前应先提供背景信息。D「background information before playing the tape」符合。A「new words after playing」与B「new words before playing」均属自下而上（bottom-up）模式的做法（先处理词汇等语言单位）；C「background information after playing」顺序不当，背景信息应在听前提供才能发挥作用。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
