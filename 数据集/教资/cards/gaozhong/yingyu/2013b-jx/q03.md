【2013b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

At first Claire refused to admit that he had stolen but when he was shown the videotape he ( ) and admitted everything.

- A. broke up
- B. broke out
- C. broke down
- D. broke in

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：At first Claire refused to admit that he had stolen but when he was shown the videotape he ( ) and admitted everything.。选项：Abroke up；Bbroke out；Cbroke down；Dbroke in。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「Claire起初拒绝承认偷窃，但看到录像后___并全部承认」，需表示心理崩溃、控制不住情绪。C「broke down」意为（情绪）崩溃、失声痛哭，符合语境。A「broke up」意为分手、解散；B「broke out」意为爆发（战争、火灾）；D「broke in」意为闯入、插话。故选C。
