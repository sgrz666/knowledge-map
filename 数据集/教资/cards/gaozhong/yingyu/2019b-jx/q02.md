【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

In the______of human life honors and rewards tend to fall to those who show their good qualities in action.

- A. ring
- B. terrace
- C. arena
- D. ground

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In the______of human life honors and rewards tend to fall to those who show their good qualities in action.。选项：Aring；Bterrace；Carena；Dground。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** in the arena of human life意为「在人生这个竞技场中」。C「arena」符合，arena喻指竞争活动的舞台。A「ring」拳击台、B「terrace」露台、D「ground」场地，均不构成「人生竞技场」的固定比喻。故选C。
