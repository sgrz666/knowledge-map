【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

Which of the following italicized phrase indicates a purpose? ( )

- A. She said it for fun but others took it seriously.
- B. For all its efforts the team didn’t win the match.
- C. Linda has worked for the firms for 20 years.
- D. He set out for Beijing yesterday.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following italicized phrase indicates a purpose? ( )。选项：AShe said it for fun but others took it seriously.；BFor all its efforts the team didn’t win the match.；CLinda has worked for the firms for 20 years.；DHe set out for Beijing yesterday.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** for fun意为「为了取乐」，表目的。A项中for fun表目的。B「For all its efforts」表让步、C「for the firms」表服务对象、D「set out for Beijing」中for表去向。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
