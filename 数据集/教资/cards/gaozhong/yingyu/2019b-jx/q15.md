【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

Activities such as problem solving decision making and opinion exchanging are typical practices in______.

- A. Audio-lingual method
- B. Grammar-translation method
- C. Situation language teaching
- D. Task-based language teaching

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Activities such as problem solving decision making and opinion exchanging are typical practices in______.。选项：AAudio-lingual method；BGrammar-translation method；CSituation language teaching；DTask-based language teaching。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 解决问题、决策、交换意见均为以完成任务为核心的活动，是任务型语言教学的典型做法。D「Task-based language teaching」符合。A以句型操练为主、B以翻译与语法讲解为主、C以情景呈现句型为主。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
