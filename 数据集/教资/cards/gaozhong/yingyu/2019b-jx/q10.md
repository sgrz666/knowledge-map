【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

The inductive method differs from the deductive one in that it enables learners to arrive at______on the basis of examples.

- A. generalizations
- B. solutions
- C. understanding
- D. decision

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The inductive method differs from the deductive one in that it enables learners to arrive at______on the basis of examples.。选项：Ageneralizations；Bsolutions；Cunderstanding；Ddecision。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 归纳法让学生从例子出发得出概括性结论。A「generalizations」符合。B「solutions」、C「understanding」、D「decision」均不能描述由例子到规则的归纳结果。故选A。
