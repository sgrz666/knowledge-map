【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

It is politely requested by the hotel management that radios______after 10 o’clock at night.

- A. did not play
- B. not to play
- C. not be played
- D. were not be played

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It is politely requested by the hotel management that radios______after 10 o’clock at night.。选项：Adid not play；Bnot to play；Cnot be played；Dwere not be played。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** requested后主语从句须用虚拟语气should do，radio与play为被动且should可省略，故为not be played。C符合。A与D时态形式错误；B为不定式，不能作从句谓语。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
