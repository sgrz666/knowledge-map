【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

The coupon______you to at least 50% off the regular price of either frames or lenses when you buy both.

- A. entitles
- B. tips
- C. presents
- D. credits

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The coupon______you to at least 50% off the regular price of either frames or lenses when you buy both.。选项：Aentitles；Btips；Cpresents；Dcredits。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** entitle sb to sth是固定搭配，意为「使某人有资格获得」。A「entitles」符合。B「tips」给小费、C「presents」赠送、D「credits」记入账户。故选A。
