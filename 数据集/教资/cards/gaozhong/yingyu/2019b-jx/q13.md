【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

What would a teacher ask students to do if he/she intends to develop their skimming skill? ( )

- A. To rewrite the text
- B. To read for specific information
- C. To predict the context of the text
- D. To read for the general idea of the text

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What would a teacher ask students to do if he/she intends to develop their skimming skill? ( )。选项：ATo rewrite the text；BTo read for specific information；CTo predict the context of the text；DTo read for the general idea of the text。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 略读是快速浏览全文把握大意。D「To read for the general idea of the text」符合。A改写、B查读具体信息、C预测内容，均非略读。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语言技能综合教学；关键词：post-reading、pre-reading、reading lesson、scanning、skimming、writing lesson、语言技能
