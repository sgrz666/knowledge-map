【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

What does a teacher focus on when he/she says “find out two things your partner likes to eat but you don’t and two things you both like to eat using what do you like to eat”? ( )

- A. Form
- B. Meaning
- C. Meaning and use
- D. Form and meaning

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What does a teacher focus on when he/she says “find out two things your partner likes to eat but you don’t and two things you both like to eat using what do you like to eat”? ( )。选项：AForm；BMeaning；CMeaning and use；DForm and meaning。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 要求学生相互采访，既要理解意义又要用该句式真实交流，故同时关注意义与运用。C「Meaning and use」符合。A仅形式、B仅意义、D缺运用。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、教学活动与问题链设计；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
