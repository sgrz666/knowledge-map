【2019b-jx · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

What is not the major purpose of asking students to give a report after the group discussion in a reading class? ( )

- A. To pool ideas
- B. To correct mistakes
- C. To encourages language use
- D. To practice oral English

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What is not the major purpose of asking students to give a report after the group discussion in a reading class? ( )。选项：ATo pool ideas；BTo correct mistakes；CTo encourages language use；DTo practice oral English。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 小组讨论后作报告的目的是汇集观点、促进语言运用、练习口语，纠正错误并非主要目的。B「To correct mistakes」符合。A、C、D均为合理目的。故选B。
