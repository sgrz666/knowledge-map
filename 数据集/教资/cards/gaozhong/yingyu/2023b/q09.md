【2023b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

If a teacher systematically analyzes classroom interactions such as turn-taking，overlapping feedback and communication breakdown he/she typically does ______.

- A. contrastive analysis
- B. conversation analysis
- C. needs analysis
- D. error analysis

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher systematically analyzes classroom interactions such as turn-taking，overlapping feedback and communication breakdown he/she typically does ______.。选项：Acontrastive analysis；Bconversation analysis；Cneeds analysis；Derror analysis。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 系统分析课堂互动中的话轮转换、话语重叠、反馈与交际中断等现象，属会话分析。B「conversation analysis」符合。A「contrastive analysis」对比分析比较两种语言结构差异、C「needs analysis」需求分析、D「error analysis」错误分析，三者均不针对会话互动结构。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学、学科教学方法与学习理论；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、合作学习
