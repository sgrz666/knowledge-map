【2023b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

Teachers and students from many countries participated in the workshops ______ the theme of the book was presented.

- A. that
- B. where
- C. which
- D. what

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Teachers and students from many countries participated in the workshops ______ the theme of the book was presented.。选项：Athat；Bwhere；Cwhich；Dwhat。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 先行词workshops（研讨会）表地点场合，从句the theme of the book was presented不缺主语或宾语，需用关系副词where引导。B「where」符合。A「that」与C「which」为关系代词，须在从句中充当成分（此处成分完整）；D「what」不引导定语从句。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
