【2023b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

What a relationship exists in the utterance “In Britain the power of the unions increase an extra dread making British politics a special case”?

- A. Cause-effect
- B. Problem-situation
- C. Tool-achievement
- D. Phenomenon-example

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What a relationship exists in the utterance “In Britain the power of the unions increase an extra dread making British politics a special case”?。选项：ACause-effect；BProblem-situation；CTool-achievement；DPhenomenon-example。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 该句先提出「英国工会力量增强引发额外恐惧」这一现象，再以「使英国政治成为特例」作为例证说明，构成现象—例证关系。D「Phenomenon-example」符合。A「Cause-effect」要求前后为因与果、B「Problem-situation」需问题与情境、C「Tool-achievement」需工具与目标，均与「陈述+例证」的模式不同。故选D。
