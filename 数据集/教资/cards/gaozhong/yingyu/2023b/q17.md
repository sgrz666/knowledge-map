【2023b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

Which of the following actives can be used to engage students in communication at the post-reading stage?

- A. Reciting the text.
- B. Reading the text aloud.
- C. Exchanging personal views on the text.
- D. Filling in the blank of the text summary.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following actives can be used to engage students in communication at the post-reading stage?。选项：AReciting the text.；BReading the text aloud.；CExchanging personal views on the text.；DFilling in the blank of the text summary.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 读后阶段应引导学生超越文本进行输出与交流。C「Exchanging personal views on the text」交换对文本的看法，属读后交流活动。A背诵、B朗读属机械练习、D填空仍停留于文本理解层面。故选C。
