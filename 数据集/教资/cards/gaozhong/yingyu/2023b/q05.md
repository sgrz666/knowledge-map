【2023b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

Which of the following is formed by prefixation?

- A. gangster
- B. decrease
- C. clockwise
- D. picturesque

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is formed by prefixation?。选项：Agangster；Bdecrease；Cclockwise；Dpicturesque。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 加前缀构词指在词根前添加派生词缀。B「decrease」由前缀de-（向下、减少）加词根crease构成，属前缀法。A「gangster」含后缀-ster、C「clockwise」含后缀-wise、D「picturesque」含后缀-esque，三者均为加后缀。故选B。
