【2023b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

______ a man who could express himself effectively is more likely to succeed than one who can not.

- A. Other things to be equal
- B. To be equal in other things
- C. Other things being equal
- D. To be equal to other things

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：______ a man who could express himself effectively is more likely to succeed than one who can not.。选项：AOther things to be equal；BTo be equal in other things；COther things being equal；DTo be equal to other things。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** Other things being equal是独立主格结构，意为「其他条件相同时」，作条件状语。C「Other things being equal」符合。A「Other things to be equal」为不定式结构，不表条件；B「To be equal in other things」为不定式，语义不通；D「To be equal to other things」同样为不定式且语义错。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
