【2023b · gaozhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

Some specialists in ceramic technology have developed a unique tube that ______ installed in walls draws water out and ensures that it stays out for good.

- A. which
- B. where
- C. when
- D. while

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Some specialists in ceramic technology have developed a unique tube that ______ installed in walls draws water out and ensures that it stays out for good.。选项：Awhich；Bwhere；Cwhen；Dwhile。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「一种独特的管子，当被安装在墙内时能把水排出并长期保持干燥」，when (it is) installed in walls是时间状语的省略形式，修饰该定语从句内的动作。C「when」符合。A「which」引导定语从句时需有完整的主谓结构，与installed搭配不当；B「where」表地点；D「while」强调同时但不如when贴合「一经安装即」的语义。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
