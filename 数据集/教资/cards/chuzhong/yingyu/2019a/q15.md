【2019a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

When a teacher says “What do you mean by that？” he/she is asking the student for .

- A. repetition
- B. suggestion
- C. introduction
- D. clarification

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher says “What do you mean by that？” he/she is asking the student for .。选项：Arepetition；Bsuggestion；Cintroduction；Dclarification。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师问「What do you mean by that?」是要求学生对先前表达作进一步说明，属请求澄清。D「clarification」符合。A「repetition」是重复；B「suggestion」是建议；C「introduction」是介绍。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
