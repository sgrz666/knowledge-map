【2019a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

When a teacher says“You'd better talk in a more polite way when speaking to the elderly”， he/she is drawing the students' attention to the of language use.

- A. fluency
- B. complexity
- C. accuracy
- D. appropriacy

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher says“You'd better talk in a more polite way when speaking to the elderly”， he/she is drawing the students' attention to the of language use.。选项：Afluency；Bcomplexity；Caccuracy；Dappropriacy。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 提示学生「对长辈说话要更礼貌」，涉及的是语言在特定场合是否得体，属语言使用的得体性。D「appropriacy」符合。A「fluency」是流利度；B「complexity」是复杂度；C「accuracy」是准确性——语法正确但可能不得体，故不是accuracy。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
