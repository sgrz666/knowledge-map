【2019a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Which of the following is employed by a speaker who addresses senior people as“the elderly” rather than“the old”？

- A. Social dialect
- B. Taboo
- C. Lingua franca
- D. Euphemism

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is employed by a speaker who addresses senior people as“the elderly” rather than“the old”？。选项：ASocial dialect；BTaboo；CLingua franca；DEuphemism。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 用「the elderly」代替「the old」称年长者，是用较委婉得体的说法替代可能冒犯的表达，属委婉语。D「Euphemism」符合。A「Social dialect」是社会方言；B「Taboo」是禁忌语本身；C「Lingua franca」是通用语。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
