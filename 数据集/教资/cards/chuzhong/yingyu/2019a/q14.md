【2019a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

If a teacher asks students to put jumbled sentences in order in a reading class， he/she intends to develop their ability of .

- A. word-guessing through context
- B. summarizing the main idea
- C. understanding textual coherence
- D. scanning for detailed information

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher asks students to put jumbled sentences in order in a reading class， he/she intends to develop their ability of .。选项：Aword-guessing through context；Bsummarizing the main idea；Cunderstanding textual coherence；Dscanning for detailed information。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 让学生把打乱的句子重新排列成通顺段落，需把握句子之间的逻辑顺序与衔接关系，发展的是语篇连贯理解能力。C「understanding textual coherence」符合。A「word-guessing through context」是猜词；B「summarizing the main idea」是概括主旨；D「scanning for detailed information」是查找细节信息。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语阅读理解、英语写作与篇章、语言技能综合教学；关键词：according to the、author、coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、infer、main idea
