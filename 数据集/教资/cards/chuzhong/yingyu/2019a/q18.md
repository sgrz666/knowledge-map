【2019a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following represents a contextualized way of practicing“How often…”？

- A. Make some sentences with“How often”
- B. Use “How often”and the words given to make a sentence
- C. I go shopping twice a week. How often do you go shopping？
- D. Please change the statement into a question with“How often”

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following represents a contextualized way of practicing“How often…”？。选项：AMake some sentences with“How often”；BUse “How often”and the words given to make a sentence；CI go shopping twice a week. How often do you go shopping？；DPlease change the statement into a question with“How often”。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 情境化练习要求把语言点置于真实交际语境中。C项以「I go shopping twice a week. How often do you go shopping?」呈现真实对话情境，学生在语境中运用How often。A「Make some sentences」、B「Use...to make a sentence」、D「change the statement into a question」均为脱离语境的形式操练。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、教学活动与问题链设计；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
