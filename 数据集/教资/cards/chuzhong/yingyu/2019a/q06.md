【2019a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

we are successful，we can be sure that we did our best.

- A. Provided that
- B. If only
- C. If or not
- D. Whether or not

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：we are successful，we can be sure that we did our best.。选项：AProvided that；BIf only；CIf or not；DWhether or not。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「___ we are successful, we can be sure that we did our best」意为「无论成功与否，我们都能确信已尽力」，需要表示让步的连词。D「Whether or not」符合。A「Provided that」表条件「只要」；B「If only」表强烈愿望「要是…就好了」；C「If or not」不是正确搭配（条件从句应为if...or not）。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
