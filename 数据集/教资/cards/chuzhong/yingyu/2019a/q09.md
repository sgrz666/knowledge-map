【2019a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

There are different words for paternal grandmother（nainai） and maternal grandmother（waipo） in Chinese， but in English the word“grandmother” is generally used in both cases， which suggests that .

- A. equal importance is given to maternal and paternal grandparents in China
- B. equal importance is given to maternal and paternal grandparents in Britain
- C. language may influence people's ways of thinking to a large extent
- D. people of different languages categorize things in different ways

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：There are different words for paternal grandmother（nainai） and maternal grandmother（waipo） in Chinese， but in English the word“grandmother” is generally used in both cases， which suggests that .。选项：Aequal importance is given to maternal and paternal grandparents in China；Bequal importance is given to maternal and paternal grandparents in Britain；Clanguage may influence people's ways of thinking to a large extent；Dpeople of different languages categorize things in different ways。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 汉语区分奶奶与外婆，英语则统称grandmother，这说明不同语言的使用者对同一客观关系作了不同的范畴切分，反映了语言的分类方式差异。D「people of different languages categorize things in different ways」符合。A、B两项把语言现象误读为社会重视程度问题，且A项与汉语实际区分的情况不符；C项「语言影响思维」属萨丕尔-沃尔夫假说的强式主张，仅凭此例不足以支撑。故选D。
