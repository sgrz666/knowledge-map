【2019a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

Which of the following are controlled activities in an English class？

- A. Reporting， role-play and games
- B. Reading aloud， dictation and translation
- C. Role-play， problem solving and discussion
- D. Information exchange， narration and interview

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following are controlled activities in an English class？。选项：AReporting， role-play and games；BReading aloud， dictation and translation；CRole-play， problem solving and discussion；DInformation exchange， narration and interview。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 控制性活动（controlled activities）是教师高度掌控、答案限定、以形式准确为目标的活动。B「Reading aloud, dictation and translation」朗读、听写、翻译均属控制性活动。A「Reporting, role-play and games」与C「Role-play, problem solving and discussion」含开放性的角色扮演与讨论；D「Information exchange, narration and interview」属交际性活动。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语言技能综合教学、教学活动与问题链设计；关键词：post-reading、pre-reading、reading lesson、scanning、skimming、writing lesson、教学环节、教学设计
