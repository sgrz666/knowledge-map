【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

Skill-integrated activities allow teachers to build in more _________ into a lesson for the range of activities will be wider.

- A. certainty
- B. simplicity
- C. variety
- D. accuracy

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Skill-integrated activities allow teachers to build in more _________ into a lesson for the range of activities will be wider.。选项：Acertainty；Bsimplicity；Cvariety；Daccuracy。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 技能综合活动（skill-integrated activities）因活动范围更广，能让课堂融入更多变化，对应C「variety」。A「certainty」（确定性）与活动范围扩大无因果；B「simplicity」（简单性）与技能综合的复杂性相反；D「accuracy」（准确性）不是活动形式多样的直接结果。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语言技能综合教学；关键词：post-reading、pre-reading、reading lesson、scanning、skimming、writing lesson、语言技能
