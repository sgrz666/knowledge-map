【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

The following conversation clearly violates the_________.
A、How did you finally go to school?
B、The bus was so fast so I got to school very early.

- A. Maxim of Quantity
- B. Maxim of Relation
- C. Maxim of Quality
- D. Maxim of Manner

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The following conversation clearly violates the_________.
A、How did you finally go to school?
B、The bus was so fast so I got to school very early.。选项：AMaxim of Quantity；BMaxim of Relation；CMaxim of Quality；DMaxim of Manner。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** A问「你最后怎么去学校的」，B答「公交车很快，所以我很早到校」。B除回答方式外还补充了多余信息（so fast、very early），提供了超出问题所需的信息量，违反A「Maxim of Quantity」（数量准则）。B「Maxim of Relation」关涉答非所问；C「Maxim of Quality」关涉真实；D「Maxim of Manner」关涉表达清晰。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
