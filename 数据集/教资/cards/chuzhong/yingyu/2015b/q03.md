【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

The gold medal was _________to Ms．Barrette for her excellent performance in the drama.

- A. distributed
- B. contributed
- C. awarded
- D. rewarded

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The gold medal was _________to Ms．Barrette for her excellent performance in the drama.。选项：Adistributed；Bcontributed；Cawarded；Drewarded。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「奖章被授予某人」用award，其搭配为award sth to sb或sb be awarded sth。C「awarded」正合「the gold medal was awarded to Ms. Barrette」。A「distributed」意为分发、分配，多用于物资；B「contributed」意为贡献、捐助；D「rewarded」意为酬谢、奖赏，多指因付出而给回报，不与奖牌授予搭配。故选C。
