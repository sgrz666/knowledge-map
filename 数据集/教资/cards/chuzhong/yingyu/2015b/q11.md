【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Which of the following statements about a lesson plan is inappropriate?

- A. It is a teaching guide.
- B. It is a blueprint to be strictly followed.
- C. It takes into account syllabus and students.
- D. It describes in advance what and how to teach.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following statements about a lesson plan is inappropriate?。选项：AIt is a teaching guide.；BIt is a blueprint to be strictly followed.；CIt takes into account syllabus and students.；DIt describes in advance what and how to teach.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教案是教学的指南，但教学需根据课堂实际灵活调整，故B「It is a blueprint to be strictly followed」（它是必须严格遵循的蓝图）说法不当。A「It is a teaching guide」教案是教学指南，正确；C「It takes into account syllabus and students」教案需考虑大纲与学生，正确；D「It describes in advance what and how to teach」教案预先描述教什么与怎么教，正确。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：教学目标与重难点设计、学科教学方法与学习理论；关键词：合作学习、建构主义、探究教学、教学原则、教学方法、教学理论、教学目标、教学策略
