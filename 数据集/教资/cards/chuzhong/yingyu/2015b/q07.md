【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

A person needs to know who he/she is before being able to know what _________ makes him or her happy.

- A. is it that
- B. it is that
- C. is it which
- D. it is which

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：A person needs to know who he/she is before being able to know what _________ makes him or her happy.。选项：Ais it that；Bit is that；Cis it which；Dit is which。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 此处为宾语从句中的强调句，正常语序为what it is that makes him or her happy。B「it is that」符合强调句结构与陈述语序。A「is it that」为疑问语序，不能用于从句；C「is it which」兼具语序错误；D「it is which」中which不能用于此处强调句型。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
