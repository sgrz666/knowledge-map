【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

Which of the following may better check students´ ability of using a grammatical structure?

- A. Having them work out the rule.
- B. Having them give some examples.
- C. Having them explain the meaning.
- D. Having them explain the structure.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following may better check students´ ability of using a grammatical structure?。选项：AHaving them work out the rule.；BHaving them give some examples.；CHaving them explain the meaning.；DHaving them explain the structure.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 检验学生是否会使用某语法结构，需让其产出运用该结构的实例，B「Having them give some examples」符合。A「Having them work out the rule」、C「Having them explain the meaning」、D「Having them explain the structure」考查的是学生对规则的元语言陈述能力，而非实际运用能力。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法、学习评价方法与证据；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
