【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

If a teacher asks students to collect compare and analyze certain sentence patterns he/she aims at developing students´

- A. discourse awareness
- B. cultural awareness
- C. strategic competence
- D. linguistic competence

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher asks students to collect compare and analyze certain sentence patterns he/she aims at developing students´。选项：Adiscourse awareness；Bcultural awareness；Cstrategic competence；Dlinguistic competence。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 收集、比较、分析句型属于对语言形式本身的观察与归纳，发展的是D「linguistic competence」（语言能力）。A「discourse awareness」指语篇层面的意识；B「cultural awareness」指文化意识；C「strategic competence」指交际策略能力。故选D。
