【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

There _________ nothing more for discussion the conference came to an end 20 minutes ear-lier.

- A. be
- B. to be
- C. being
- D. to have been

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：There _________ nothing more for discussion the conference came to an end 20 minutes ear-lier.。选项：Abe；Bto be；Cbeing；Dto have been。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 句首There ____ nothing more for discussion 作独立主格结构表原因，需用非谓语形式。C「being」构成There being nothing more for discussion，意为「由于没有更多可讨论的」，在句中作原因状语。A「be」为动词原形；B「to be」为不定式；D「to have been」为完成不定式，均不能构成独立主格。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
