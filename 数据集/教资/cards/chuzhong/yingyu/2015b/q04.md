【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

The visitors had made so much_________that Mr Water had to spend several days cleaning up afterwards.

- A. trouble
- B. disturbance
- C. damage
- D. mess

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The visitors had made so much_________that Mr Water had to spend several days cleaning up afterwards.。选项：Atrouble；Bdisturbance；Cdamage；Dmess。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「made so much ___ that Mr Water had to spend several days cleaning up」强调需要花数天清理的脏乱状态，对应D「mess」（一团糟）。A「trouble」搭make trouble表示制造麻烦，但不需要清理；B「disturbance」指干扰、骚乱；C「damage」指损害，与cleaning up的语境不够贴合。故选D。
