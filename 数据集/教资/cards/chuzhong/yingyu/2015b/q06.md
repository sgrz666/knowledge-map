【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

In the foreign languages bookstoreto be found books in various languages.

- A. is
- B. is been
- C. are
- D. are been

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In the foreign languages bookstoreto be found books in various languages.。选项：Ais；Bis been；Care；Dare been。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 该句为倒装结构：In the foreign languages bookstore ____ to be found books in various languages，真正的主语是books（复数），故谓语用C「are」。A「is」为单数，与复数主语不一致；B「is been」与D「are been」的被动形式均不成立，因为be to be found本身已是「可以被找到」的结构。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
