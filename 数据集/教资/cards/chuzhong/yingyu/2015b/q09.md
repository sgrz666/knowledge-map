【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Which of the following is an entailment of the utterance "Annie broke the window"?

- A. Annie was careless.
- B. Annie was disruptive.
- C. Annie did something to the window.
- D. It is Annie who broke the window.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is an entailment of the utterance "Annie broke the window"?。选项：AAnnie was careless.；BAnnie was disruptive.；CAnnie did something to the window.；DIt is Annie who broke the window.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 蕴含（entailment）指由原句必然推出的命题。由「Annie broke the window」可必然推出C「Annie did something to the window」（安妮对窗户做了某事），因为打破窗户即是施加动作于窗户。A「Annie was careless」与B「Annie was disruptive」是对行为的评价性推断，并非逻辑必然；D「It is Annie who broke the window」是原句的强调变体而非新的蕴含。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
