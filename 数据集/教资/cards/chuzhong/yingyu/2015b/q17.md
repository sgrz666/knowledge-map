【2015b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

When a teacher organizes group work which of the following might be of the least con-cem?

- A. Increasing peer interaction.
- B. Increasing individual practice.
- C. Developing language accuracy.
- D. Providing variety and dynamics.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher organizes group work which of the following might be of the least con-cem?。选项：AIncreasing peer interaction.；BIncreasing individual practice.；CDeveloping language accuracy.；DProviding variety and dynamics.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 小组活动的主要价值在于增加同伴互动、扩大个体练习机会、提供课堂变化与活力，而语言准确性通常不是小组活动最关注的——小组交流中语言误差往往更多，准确性更适合通过controlled practice来关注。A「Increasing peer interaction」、B「Increasing individual practice」、D「Providing variety and dynamics」都是小组活动的常见目的。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
