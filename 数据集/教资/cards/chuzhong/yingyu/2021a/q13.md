【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

When a teacher says “Is this sentence right?” he/she may be doing all of the following EXCEPT ______.

- A. giving students feedback
- B. eliciting students’ responses
- C. inviting students’ judgments
- D. correcting a mistake explicitly

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher says “Is this sentence right?” he/she may be doing all of the following EXCEPT ______.。选项：Agiving students feedback；Beliciting students’ responses；Cinviting students’ judgments；Dcorrecting a mistake explicitly。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师问「Is this sentence right?」是在给出反馈、引出学生回应、邀请学生判断；但这是以提问方式引导学生自行判断，并非直接明确地纠错——明确纠错应直接指出错误所在。D「correcting a mistake explicitly」是EXCEPT项。A、B、C三项均为该提问的功能。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
