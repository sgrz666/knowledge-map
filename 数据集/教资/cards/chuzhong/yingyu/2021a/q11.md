【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Problem-solving activities role-play information gap etc. are typical classroom activities of ______.

- A. the Direct Method
- B. Total Physical Response
- C. the Audio-lingual Method
- D. Communicative Language Teaching

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Problem-solving activities role-play information gap etc. are typical classroom activities of ______.。选项：Athe Direct Method；BTotal Physical Response；Cthe Audio-lingual Method；DCommunicative Language Teaching。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 解决问题、角色扮演、信息差等活动以真实交际为目的，是交际语言教学法的典型课堂活动。D「Communicative Language Teaching」符合。A「the Direct Method」以直接法用目标语授课、B「Total Physical Response」以身体动作回应指令、C「the Audio-lingual Method」以句型操练为主。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
