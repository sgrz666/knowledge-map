【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

Which of the following activities is NOT appropriate for developing students’ awareness of discourse coherence?

- A. Writing a conclusion for a passage.
- B. Underlining the topic sentences of each paragraph.
- C. Changing sentences from active forms into passive ones.
- D. Arranging all the sentences to produce a meaningful passage.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities is NOT appropriate for developing students’ awareness of discourse coherence?。选项：AWriting a conclusion for a passage.；BUnderlining the topic sentences of each paragraph.；CChanging sentences from active forms into passive ones.；DArranging all the sentences to produce a meaningful passage.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 培养语篇连贯意识需关注句子之间的衔接与逻辑关系。C「Changing sentences from active forms into passive ones」是语态转换，属句法练习，与语篇连贯无关，故不适合。A「Writing a conclusion for a passage」需与前文衔接、B「Underlining the topic sentences」把握段落主旨结构、D「Arranging all the sentences to produce a meaningful passage」重组句子成篇，三者均直接涉及语篇连贯。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章、语义语用与语言学、英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、coherence、cohesion、composition、discourse、essay、gerund
