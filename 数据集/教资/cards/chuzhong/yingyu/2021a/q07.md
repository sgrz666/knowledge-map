【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

She thought I was talking about her daughter ______ in fact I was talking about my daughter.

- A. when
- B. where
- C. which
- D. while

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：She thought I was talking about her daughter ______ in fact I was talking about my daughter.。选项：Awhen；Bwhere；Cwhich；Dwhile。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** while可表示对比，「她以为我在说她女儿，而实际上我在说我女儿」，前后形成对照。D「while」符合。A「when」表时间；B「where」表地点；C「which」为关系代词，不能引导此处的对比状语。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
