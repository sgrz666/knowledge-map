【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

It is commonly believed that solar power is an environmentally ______ technology operating with essentially no emissions.

- A. benign
- B. capricious
- C. culpable
- D. detrimental

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It is commonly believed that solar power is an environmentally ______ technology operating with essentially no emissions.。选项：Abenign；Bcapricious；Cculpable；Ddetrimental。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「太阳能是几乎零排放的技术」，说明其对环境无害，A「benign」（温和的、无害的）符合environmentally benign这一固定搭配。B「capricious」（反复无常的）、C「culpable」（应受谴责的）、D「detrimental」（有害的）均与「无排放」的正面语义矛盾。故选A。
