【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

The ultimate goal of middle school English teaching and learning is to ______.

- A. enhance certain basic language knowledge
- B. develop integrative abilities in language use
- C. increase learners’ interest in studying English
- D. foster listening speaking reading and writing skills

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The ultimate goal of middle school English teaching and learning is to ______.。选项：Aenhance certain basic language knowledge；Bdevelop integrative abilities in language use；Cincrease learners’ interest in studying English；Dfoster listening speaking reading and writing skills。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 《义务教育英语课程标准》规定，基础教育阶段英语课程的总体目标是培养学生的综合语言运用能力。B「develop integrative abilities in language use」符合。A「enhance certain basic language knowledge」仅指知识、C「increase learners' interest」仅指兴趣、D「foster listening, speaking, reading and writing skills」仅指技能，均为下位目标，不能概括总体目标。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、英语写作与篇章；关键词：audio-lingual、coherence、cohesion、communicative、composition、essay、listening、oral
