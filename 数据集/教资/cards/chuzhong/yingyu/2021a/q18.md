【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following activities has a focus on grammar?

- A. Read a story and act it out in groups.
- B. Read a story and answer comprehension questions.
- C. Read a story and underline the most difficult sentences.
- D. Read a story and analyze how past events are described.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities has a focus on grammar?。选项：ARead a story and act it out in groups.；BRead a story and answer comprehension questions.；CRead a story and underline the most difficult sentences.；DRead a story and analyze how past events are described.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 关注语法的活动需引导学生注意语言形式及其规则。D「analyze how past events are described」分析过去事件的描述方式，即关注一般过去时的语法形式。A「act it out」属表演、B「answer comprehension questions」属理解、C「underline the most difficult sentences」属难句识别，均未聚焦特定语法结构。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
