【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

The Arctic wolf is clearly a very ______ animal for it is able to survive subzero temperatures long periods of darkness and days without food.

- A. ferocious
- B. hardy
- C. aggressive
- D. docile

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The Arctic wolf is clearly a very ______ animal for it is able to survive subzero temperatures long periods of darkness and days without food.。选项：Aferocious；Bhardy；Caggressive；Ddocile。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「北极狼能在零度以下、长期黑暗与断食条件下生存」，说明其耐受力强，B「hardy」（耐寒耐苦的、顽强的）符合。A「ferocious」（凶猛的）与C「aggressive」（好斗的）描述攻击性而非耐受能力；D「docile」（温顺的）与生存能力无关。故选B。
