【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

In a process-based writing class after students have finished drafting their teacher would most probably have them ______.

- A. edit their drafts
- B. do brainstorming on the topic
- C. analyze the topic
- D. learn useful words and expressions

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In a process-based writing class after students have finished drafting their teacher would most probably have them ______.。选项：Aedit their drafts；Bdo brainstorming on the topic；Canalyze the topic；Dlearn useful words and expressions。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 过程写作法中，学生完成初稿（drafting）之后的环节是对初稿进行修改（editing）。A「edit their drafts」符合。B「brainstorming」与C「analyze the topic」属写前构思阶段；D「learn useful words and expressions」也属写前准备。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
