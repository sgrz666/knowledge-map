【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

What textual pattern can be found between the two sentences “Clare loves potatoes. She was born in Ireland.”?

- A. Problem-solution.
- B. Cause-effect.
- C. Phenomenon-example.
- D. Instrument-achievement.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What textual pattern can be found between the two sentences “Clare loves potatoes. She was born in Ireland.”?。选项：AProblem-solution.；BCause-effect.；CPhenomenon-example.；DInstrument-achievement.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「Clare loves potatoes. She was born in Ireland.」第一句陈述偏好，第二句提供原因解释，构成因果（cause-effect）关系。B「Cause-effect」符合。A「Problem-solution」需有问题与对策、C「Phenomenon-example」需有概括与例证、D「Instrument-achievement」需手段与结果，均不匹配。故选B。
