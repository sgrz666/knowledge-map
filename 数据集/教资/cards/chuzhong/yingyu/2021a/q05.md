【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

It is said that Metis the innermost ______ of Jupiter completes a full revolution around this giant planet within seven hours.

- A. comet
- B. meteor
- C. galaxy
- D. satellite

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It is said that Metis the innermost ______ of Jupiter completes a full revolution around this giant planet within seven hours.。选项：Acomet；Bmeteor；Cgalaxy；Dsatellite。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干描述Metis绕木星公转，是围绕行星运行的星体，即卫星。D「satellite」符合。A「comet」是彗星、B「meteor」是流星、C「galaxy」是星系，均不绕行星运行。故选D。
