【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

In English teaching mechanical activities such as ______ may be helpful for teaching particular structures but they should not be overused.

- A. transformation and role-play
- B. translation and word-matching
- C. gap-filling and topic discussion
- D. memorization and pattern drilling

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In English teaching mechanical activities such as ______ may be helpful for teaching particular structures but they should not be overused.。选项：Atransformation and role-play；Btranslation and word-matching；Cgap-filling and topic discussion；Dmemorization and pattern drilling。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 机械性活动指不需要真实交际、以重复记忆和形式操练为主的活动。D「memorization and pattern drilling」背诵与句型操练属典型机械活动。A「transformation and role-play」含角色扮演、B「translation and word-matching」含词义匹配、C「gap-filling and topic discussion」含话题讨论，三者均含一定程度的交际或意义加工成分。故选D。
