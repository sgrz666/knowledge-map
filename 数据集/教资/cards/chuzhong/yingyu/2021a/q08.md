【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

The main difference between the two words “childlike” and “childish” lies in ______.

- A. emotive meaning
- B. stylistic meaning
- C. metaphorical meaning
- D. collocative meaning

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The main difference between the two words “childlike” and “childish” lies in ______.。选项：Aemotive meaning；Bstylistic meaning；Cmetaphorical meaning；Dcollocative meaning。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** childlike（天真无邪，褒义）与childish（幼稚的，贬义）概念意义相同而感情色彩相反，差别在情感意义。A「emotive meaning」符合。B「stylistic meaning」指语体色彩、C「metaphorical meaning」指隐喻意义、D「collocative meaning」指搭配意义，均不解释褒贬对立。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
