【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

David took a test and his score was at the top 10 percent in his class. This is an interpretation of the result of a ______ test.

- A. diagnostic
- B. formative
- C. norm-referenced
- D. criterion-referenced（一）请阅读Passage 1，完成第

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：David took a test and his score was at the top 10 percent in his class. This is an interpretation of the result of a ______ test.。选项：Adiagnostic；Bformative；Cnorm-referenced；Dcriterion-referenced（一）请阅读Passage 1，完成第。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** David的成绩处于班级前10%，是与同班同学（常模）比较得出的相对位置，属常模参照测试的解释。C「norm-referenced」符合。A「diagnostic」诊断性、B「formative」形成性、D「criterion-referenced」标准参照（与固定标准比较，不与他人比较），均不符合「在班级中排前10%」的表述。故选C。
