【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

Which of the following is most effective for a teacher when students do not understand how to do an activity that he/she has described?

- A. Demonstrating how to do it.
- B. Asking students to talk freely.
- C. Encouraging students to share their ideas.
- D. Walking around the classroom and answering questions.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is most effective for a teacher when students do not understand how to do an activity that he/she has described?。选项：ADemonstrating how to do it.；BAsking students to talk freely.；CEncouraging students to share their ideas.；DWalking around the classroom and answering questions.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 学生不理解活动做法时，最有效的做法是教师亲自示范如何操作，使指令具象化。A「Demonstrating how to do it」符合。B「Asking students to talk freely」与C「Encouraging students to share their ideas」并未解决理解障碍；D「Walking around and answering questions」是补救措施，但不如直接示范高效。故选A。
