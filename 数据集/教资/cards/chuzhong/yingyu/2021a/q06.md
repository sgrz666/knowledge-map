【2021a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

In 1840 both Lucretia Mott and Elizabeth Cady Stanton resented ______ proper seating at the World’s Anti-slavery Convention in London because of their sex.

- A. to be denied
- B. having denied
- C. being denied
- D. to deny

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In 1840 both Lucretia Mott and Elizabeth Cady Stanton resented ______ proper seating at the World’s Anti-slavery Convention in London because of their sex.。选项：Ato be denied；Bhaving denied；Cbeing denied；Dto deny。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** resent后接动名词，且两位女性是「被拒绝座位」的接受者，需用被动形式的动名词。C「being denied」符合。A「to be denied」为不定式，resent不接不定式；B「having denied」为主动完成，与「被拒绝」不符；D「to deny」为主动不定式。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
