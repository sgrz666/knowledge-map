English teachers often ask students to__________ a passage to get the gist of it.

- A. skim
- B. scan
- C. predict
- D. infer

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：English teachers often ask students to__________ a passage to get the gist of it.。选项：Askim；Bscan；Cpredict；Dinfer。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 为抓大意学生常skim（略读）。B scan跳读找细节、C预测、D推断均不符。故选A。
