【2015a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

A/An __________ language test such as IELTS or TOEFL is developed on the basis of a fixed standard.

- A. individual-referenced
- B. peer-referenced
- C. norm-referenced
- D. criterion-referenced

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：A/An __________ language test such as IELTS or TOEFL is developed on the basis of a fixed standard.。选项：Aindividual-referenced；Bpeer-referenced；Cnorm-referenced；Dcriterion-referenced。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** IELTS/TOEFL基于固定标准，是标准参照测验（criterion-referenced）。A个体、B同伴、C常模参照均错。故选D。
