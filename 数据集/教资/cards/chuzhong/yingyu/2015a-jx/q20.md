A systematic textbook evaluation is NOT to examine whether a textbook

- A. covers all grammatical rules
- B. Trovides authentic language
- C. matches the needs of learners
- D. can help realize the objectives of a language program

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：A systematic textbook evaluation is NOT to examine whether a textbook。选项：Acovers all grammatical rules；BTrovides authentic language；Cmatches the needs of learners；Dcan help realize the objectives of a language program。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 系统教材评估不应只考察『是否覆盖所有语法规则』。B真实语料、C符合需求、D实现目标均属应考察项。故选A。
