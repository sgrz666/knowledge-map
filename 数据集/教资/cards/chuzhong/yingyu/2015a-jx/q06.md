【2015a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

__________conscious of her moral obligations as a citizen.

- A. Marsha was and always will be
- B. Marsha has to be and always will be
- C. Marsha had been and will always be
- D. Marsha has been and will always be

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：__________conscious of her moral obligations as a citizen.。选项：AMarsha was and always will be；BMarsha has to be and always will be；CMarsha had been and will always be；DMarsha has been and will always be。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『Marsha ____ and will always be conscious…』现在完成与将来呼应，用has been and will always be。A、B、C时态不连贯。故选D。
