【2015a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

Jimmy asked Mary to lend him some money which she agreed to __________ that he paid her back the following month.

- A. on occasion
- B. in case
- C. under circumstances
- D. on condition

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Jimmy asked Mary to lend him some money which she agreed to __________ that he paid her back the following month.。选项：Aon occasion；Bin case；Cunder circumstances；Don condition。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『she agreed to ____ that he paid her back』表『在…条件下』用on condition（that）。A偶尔、B以防、C在…情况下均不符。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇、英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、lexical、morpheme、morphology、participle
