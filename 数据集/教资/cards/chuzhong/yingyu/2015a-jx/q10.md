【2015a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Which of the following best represents the order from a harsh command to a very polite__________request?
Could you close the door?
Close the door.
Please close the door.
Would you please close the door?

- A. b-c-a-d
- B. c-b-a-d
- C. d-a-c-b
- D. c-b-d-a

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following best represents the order from a harsh command to a very polite__________request?
Could you close the door?
Close the door.
Please close the door.
Would you please close the door?。选项：Ab-c-a-d；Bc-b-a-d；Cd-a-c-b；Dc-b-d-a。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 由命令到客气的顺序：Close the door(b)＜Please close(c)＜Could you(a)＜Would you please(d)。A b-c-a-d正确；其余顺序错。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
