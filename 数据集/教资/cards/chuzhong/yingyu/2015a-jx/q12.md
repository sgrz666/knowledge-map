【2015a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

The main objective of mechanical practice is to help students learn by heart the of a language item.__________

- A. meaning
- B. function
- C. context
- D. form

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The main objective of mechanical practice is to help students learn by heart the of a language item.__________。选项：Ameaning；Bfunction；Ccontext；Dform。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 机械练习主要帮学生背诵语言项目的『形式（form）』。A意义、B功能、C语境均非机械练习重点。故选D。
