【2015a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

There are two syllables in the word "photo". occurs in both syllables but the first one is__________the second because it is stressed.

- A. longer than
- B. shorter than
- C. as long as
- D. as short as

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：There are two syllables in the word "photo". occurs in both syllables but the first one is__________the second because it is stressed.。选项：Alonger than；Bshorter than；Cas long as；Das short as。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『photo』中两个音节的/u/，首音节因重读而更长（longer than）。B更短、C一样长、D一样短均错。故选A。
