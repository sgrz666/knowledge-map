【2014a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Whichof the following may illustrate the difference between "competence" and "performance" ?

- A. What a person "knows" and what he / she "does"．
- B. What a person "can do" and what he / she "does"．
- C. What a person "does" and what he / she "knows"．
- D. What a person "does" and what he / she "can do"．

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Whichof the following may illustrate the difference between "competence" and "performance" ?。选项：AWhat a person "knows" and what he / she "does"．；BWhat a person "can do" and what he / she "does"．；CWhat a person "does" and what he / she "knows"．；DWhat a person "does" and what he / she "can do"．。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** competence指人『can do（能做）』的能力，performance指实际『does（做）』。B『can do』与『does』正确对应；A把knows与does并列、C/D顺序错位。故选B。
