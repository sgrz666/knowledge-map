【2014a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

In a listening activity students are asked to note down the time and places of events． The aim of this activity is to develop the skill of __________．

- A. listening for gist
- B. listening for vocabulary
- C. listening for structure
- D. listening for specific information

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In a listening activity students are asked to note down the time and places of events． The aim of this activity is to develop the skill of __________．。选项：Alistening for gist；Blistening for vocabulary；Clistening for structure；Dlistening for specific information。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 听力中记录事件的时间与地点，训练的是获取特定信息（listening for specific information）。A听大意、B听词汇、C听结构均非此举目的。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、词法与词汇；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、lexical、listening、morpheme、morphology、oral、role play
