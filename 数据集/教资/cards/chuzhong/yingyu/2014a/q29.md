Which of the following is closest in meaning to the underlined word"unfazed" in the last PARAGRAPH?

- A. undisturbed
- B. infuriated
- C. unconcerned
- D. frustrated

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is closest in meaning to the underlined word"unfazed" in the last PARAGRAPH?。选项：Aundisturbed；Binfuriated；Cunconcerned；Dfrustrated。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『unfazed』意为不被打扰、镇定，近义undisturbed。B激怒、C不关心、D挫败均不符。故选A。
