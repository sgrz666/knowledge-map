【2014a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

In a few years an intelligent computer will certainly be an__________ diagnostic．

- A. indirect
- B. inseparable
- C. indisputable
- D. indispensable

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In a few years an intelligent computer will certainly be an__________ diagnostic．。选项：Aindirect；Binseparable；Cindisputable；Dindispensable。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『an ____ diagnostic tool』指不可或缺的工具，用indispensable。其余形容词义不符。故选D。
