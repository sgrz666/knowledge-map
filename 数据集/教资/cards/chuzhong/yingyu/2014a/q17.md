【2014a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

Which of the following should a teacher avoid when using an ELT course book?

- A. Selecting appropriate supporting materials and resources．
- B. Interpreting curriculum goals and its expectations for the course．
- C. Planning lessons in relation to specific goals topics texts and tasks．
- D. Implementing everything in the book without considering students' needs and levels．

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following should a teacher avoid when using an ELT course book?。选项：ASelecting appropriate supporting materials and resources．；BInterpreting curriculum goals and its expectations for the course．；CPlanning lessons in relation to specific goals topics texts and tasks．；DImplementing everything in the book without considering students' needs and levels．。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 使用教材应避免『不顾学生需求与水平照搬全书』。A选补充材料、B解读课标、C按目标备课都是合理做法。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课程目标与课程内容标准、学情分析与教材分析；关键词：学习基础、学情、学生基础、教材内容、教材分析、教材片段、核心素养、课标
