【2014a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

Which of the following can NOT be used as a pre-reading task?

- A. Predicting what a passage is about．
- B. Creating a word web related to a topic．
- C. Listening to the recording of a passage．
- D. Sharing what is already known about a topic．

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following can NOT be used as a pre-reading task?。选项：APredicting what a passage is about．；BCreating a word web related to a topic．；CListening to the recording of a passage．；DSharing what is already known about a topic．。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 读前任务不含『听文章录音』，那是听/读中活动；A预测内容、B建主题词网、D分享已知均属读前。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语言技能综合教学、听说与交际教学、英语阅读理解；关键词：according to the、audio-lingual、author、communicative、infer、listening、main idea、oral
