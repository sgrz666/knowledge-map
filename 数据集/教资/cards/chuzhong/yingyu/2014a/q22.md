【2014a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第22题】

Which of the following best describes the effect produced by Berg's efforts?

- A. They have spared more rooms for shelter dogs to live in．
- B. They have gained her more professional fame as a photographer．
- C. They have enabled her to adopt one more dog from Dallas rescue group．
- D. They have made online adoptions increase twice as much as last year．

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following best describes the effect produced by Berg's efforts?。选项：AThey have spared more rooms for shelter dogs to live in．；BThey have gained her more professional fame as a photographer．；CThey have enabled her to adopt one more dog from Dallas rescue group．；DThey have made online adoptions increase twice as much as last year．。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** Berg照片的效果是网站领养量较去年翻倍。A腾出空间、B个人名气、C多领养一只均非文中所说。故选D。
