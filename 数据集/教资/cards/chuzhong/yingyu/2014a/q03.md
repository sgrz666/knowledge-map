【2014a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

The student from a __________family can afford most of the books he wants．

- A. well-to-do
- B. to-do-well
- C. do-to-well
- D. do-well-to

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The student from a __________family can afford most of the books he wants．。选项：Awell-to-do；Bto-do-well；Cdo-to-well；Ddo-well-to。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『a ____ family』指富裕家庭，固定搭配well-to-do。其余词序错误。故选A。
