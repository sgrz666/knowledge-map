【2014a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

I like going to the cinema __________ TV.

- A. rather than to watch
- B. than watching
- C. more than to watch
- D. more than watching

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：I like going to the cinema __________ TV.。选项：Arather than to watch；Bthan watching；Cmore than to watch；Dmore than watching。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 平行结构：『I like going to the cinema ____ TV』，going与watching平行，用more than watching。A rather than接to watch不平行；B than watching缺少more；C more than to watch不平行。故选D。
