【2014a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

After __________ seemed an endless wait it was her turn to enter the chief manager's

- A. that
- B. it
- C. what
- D. there

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：After __________ seemed an endless wait it was her turn to enter the chief manager's。选项：Athat；Bit；Cwhat；Dthere。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『After ____ seemed an endless wait』中需what引导名词性从句作After宾语（after what seemed…）。A that、B it、D there均不能作从句引导词。故选C。
