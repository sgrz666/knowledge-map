【2014a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

If a teacher gives commands in English and asks students to show understanding by action_______or gestures he/she is most probably using

- A. Communicative Approach
- B. Audio-Lingual Approach
- C. Grammar Translation Method
- D. Total Physical Response

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher gives commands in English and asks students to show understanding by action_______or gestures he/she is most probably using。选项：ACommunicative Approach；BAudio-Lingual Approach；CGrammar Translation Method；DTotal Physical Response。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师用英语下指令并让学生用动作/手势回应，是全身反应法（TPR）。A交际法、B听说法、C语法翻译法均不符。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、audio-lingual、clause、communicative、gerund、grammar、listening、oral
