【2014a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

__________ her mother started to go to the meditation class.

- A. It was at about this time that
- B. That was about this time
- C. It was at about this time when
- D. It was about this time that

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：__________ her mother started to go to the meditation class.。选项：AIt was at about this time that；BThat was about this time；CIt was at about this time when；DIt was about this time that。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 强调句『It was at about this time that her mother started…』。A正确；C用when错误（强调句用that）；D缺at about；B非强调结构。故选A。
