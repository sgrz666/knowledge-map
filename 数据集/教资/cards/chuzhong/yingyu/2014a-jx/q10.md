【2014a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

The question "How fast did he drive when he ran the red light?" __________ "He ran the red light".

- A. presupposes
- B. entails
- C. contradicts
- D. includes

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The question "How fast did he drive when he ran the red light?" __________ "He ran the red light".。选项：Apresupposes；Bentails；Ccontradicts；Dincludes。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『他闯红灯时开多快』这句话预设（presupposes）了『他闯了红灯』。B蕴含、C矛盾、D包含均不符预设定义。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
