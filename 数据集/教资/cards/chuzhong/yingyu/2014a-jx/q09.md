【2014a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Which of the following may illustrate the difference between "competence" and__________ "performance"?

- A. What a person "knows" and what he/she "does".
- B. What a person "can do" and what he/she "does".
- C. What a person "does" and what he/she "knows".
- D. What a person "does" and what he/she "can do".

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following may illustrate the difference between "competence" and__________ "performance"?。选项：AWhat a person "knows" and what he/she "does".；BWhat a person "can do" and what he/she "does".；CWhat a person "does" and what he/she "knows".；DWhat a person "does" and what he/she "can do".。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 语言能力（competence）指人所『知道』的语法知识，语言运用（performance）指人实际『做』出的言语。A『knows』与『does』正对应此区分；B、C、D均未正确对应。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
