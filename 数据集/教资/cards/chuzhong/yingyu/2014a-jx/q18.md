【2014a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

To assess how well students are able to apply what they have learned in completing a given task a teacher would use _______assessment.

- A. Performance
- B. self
- C. Competence
- D. peer

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：To assess how well students are able to apply what they have learned in completing a given task a teacher would use _______assessment.。选项：APerformance；Bself；CCompetence；Dpeer。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 评估学生运用所学完成任务的情况用表现性评价（performance assessment）。B自评、C能力、D同伴评价均不符。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：学习评价方法与证据；关键词：作业、学习任务单、形成性、测试、测验、终结性、评价、评分
