【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

What would he/she do in a reading class if a teacher wants to develop students’ inferential comprehension?

- A. Asking them to retell the story.
- B. Asking them to underline difficult sentences.
- C. Asking them to read the text sentence by sentence.
- D. Asking them to read the text for implied meaning.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What would he/she do in a reading class if a teacher wants to develop students’ inferential comprehension?。选项：AAsking them to retell the story.；BAsking them to underline difficult sentences.；CAsking them to read the text sentence by sentence.；DAsking them to read the text for implied meaning.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 推断性理解（inferential comprehension）要求读出字面之外的隐含意义。D「Asking them to read the text for implied meaning」符合。A「retell the story」是复述、B「underline difficult sentences」是识别难句、C「read sentence by sentence」是逐句读，均停留在字面层面。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语阅读理解、语言技能综合教学；关键词：according to the、author、infer、main idea、passage、post-reading、pre-reading、reading comprehension
