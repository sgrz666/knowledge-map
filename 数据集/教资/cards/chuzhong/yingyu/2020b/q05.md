【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

We don’t know what experiment those researchers would on females to test this hypothesis.

- A. apply
- B. carry
- C. deliver
- D. perform

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：We don’t know what experiment those researchers would on females to test this hypothesis.。选项：Aapply；Bcarry；Cdeliver；Dperform。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「做实验」的固定搭配是perform/conduct/carry out an experiment。D「perform」符合。A「apply」多用于apply...to...（应用于）；B「carry」须与out搭配才表示实施，单用carry后不能直接接experiment；C「deliver」意为递送或发表。故选D。
