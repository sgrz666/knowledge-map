【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Which of the following describes the function of the sentence “It is a nice day isn’t it”?

- A. Informative.
- B. Phatic.
- C. Directive.
- D. Performative.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following describes the function of the sentence “It is a nice day isn’t it”?。选项：AInformative.；BPhatic.；CDirective.；DPerformative.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「It is a nice day, isn't it?」并不传递实质信息，而是通过寒暄维持社交关系，属寒暄功能。B「Phatic」符合，phatic communion即寒暄交际。A「Informative」传递信息、C「Directive」发出指令、D「Performative」以言行事，均不描述此类天气寒暄。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
