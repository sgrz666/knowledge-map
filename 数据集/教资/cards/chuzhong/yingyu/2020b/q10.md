【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Which of the following describes the process in which one’s language ability is developed in natural and everyday situation?

- A. Performance.
- B. Competence.
- C. Learning.
- D. Acquisition.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following describes the process in which one’s language ability is developed in natural and everyday situation?。选项：APerformance.；BCompetence.；CLearning.；DAcquisition.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 在自然日常环境中习得语言能力的过程称为语言习得（acquisition），与课堂上有意识的learning相对。D「Acquisition」符合。A「Performance」是语言运用表现、B「Competence」是语言能力（与performance相对）、C「Learning」指有意识的学习，与题干「natural and everyday situation」不符。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：二语习得与学习策略；关键词：acquisition、interlanguage、learning strateg、motivation、transfer、二语、语言习得
