【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

In the sentence “It’s no use crying over the spilt milk” the underlined part is .

- A. an object
- B. an adverbial
- C. a subject
- D. a complement

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In the sentence “It’s no use crying over the spilt milk” the underlined part is .。选项：Aan object；Ban adverbial；Ca subject；Da complement。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「It's no use crying over the spilt milk」中It为形式主语，真正的主语是动名词短语crying over the spilt milk，划线部分作主语。C「a subject」符合。A「an object」是宾语、B「an adverbial」是状语、D「a complement」是补语，均与形式主语句式中动名词短语的功能不符。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
