【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

What does he/she intend to do when a teacher writes the following sentences “She gets up early. She wears a uniform. She works very hard.” on the blackboard at the presentation stage?

- A. Practicing sentence patterns using model sentences.
- B. Checking if students can pronounce the sentences correctly.
- C. Monitoring whether students can accurately express their ideas.
- D. Drawing students’ attention to the form of a new language item.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What does he/she intend to do when a teacher writes the following sentences “She gets up early. She wears a uniform. She works very hard.” on the blackboard at the presentation stage?。选项：APracticing sentence patterns using model sentences.；BChecking if students can pronounce the sentences correctly.；CMonitoring whether students can accurately express their ideas.；DDrawing students’ attention to the form of a new language item.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 在呈现阶段把「She gets up early. She wears a uniform. She works very hard.」写在黑板上，这些句子均含一般现在时第三人称单数形式，目的是引导学生注意新语言项目的形式。D「Drawing students' attention to the form of a new language item」符合。A「Practicing sentence patterns」属练习阶段；B「Checking pronunciation」不是呈现阶段的主要意图；C「Monitoring」属练习或产出阶段。故选D。
