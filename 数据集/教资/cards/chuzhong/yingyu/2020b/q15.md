【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

Which of the following is least recommended at the lead-in stage in a reading class?

- A. Activating students’ schema of the topic.
- B. Giving advice on how to use reading strategies.
- C. Sharing background information about the text.
- D. Correcting language mistakes students have made.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is least recommended at the lead-in stage in a reading class?。选项：AActivating students’ schema of the topic.；BGiving advice on how to use reading strategies.；CSharing background information about the text.；DCorrecting language mistakes students have made.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 读前导入（lead-in）阶段的功能是激活背景图式、提示阅读策略、提供背景信息，而纠正学生此前的语言错误与导入无关，且会干扰注意焦点。D「Correcting language mistakes students have made」最不推荐。A、B、C三项均为导入阶段的常见做法。故选D。
