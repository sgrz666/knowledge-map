【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

It is forbidden faster than 60 km/h on this highway.

- A. to driving
- B. to drive
- C. being driven
- D. to be driven

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It is forbidden faster than 60 km/h on this highway.。选项：Ato driving；Bto drive；Cbeing driven；Dto be driven。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** It is forbidden to do sth是固定句型，forbidden后接不定式的一般式表示被禁止的行为。B「to drive」符合。A「to driving」混用不定式与动名词；C「being driven」为被动，题干从句中主语泛指驾驶者，无需被动；D「to be driven」同样为被动且语义不通。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
