【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

Which of the following activities can be used if the focus is on developing students’ oral fluency in English?

- A. Blank-filling.
- B. Story-telling.
- C. Transformation.
- D. Translation.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities can be used if the focus is on developing students’ oral fluency in English?。选项：ABlank-filling.；BStory-telling.；CTransformation.；DTranslation.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 口语流利度训练应让学生连续表达、关注意义传达。B「Story-telling」讲述故事需连续输出语言，符合。A「Blank-filling」填空、C「Transformation」句型转换、D「Translation」翻译均以书面形式操练语言点，不训练口语流畅表达。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
