【2020b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

What skill does he/she use when a student uses language knowledge and contextual clues to figure out the meaning of a new word?

- A. Contrasting.
- B. Summarizing.
- C. Deducing.
- D. Predicting.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What skill does he/she use when a student uses language knowledge and contextual clues to figure out the meaning of a new word?。选项：AContrasting.；BSummarizing.；CDeducing.；DPredicting.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 学生利用语言知识与上下文线索推断生词意义，属推理性策略。C「Deducing」意为推断，符合根据线索推出词义的过程。A「Contrasting」是对比、B「Summarizing」是概括、D「Predicting」是预测后文内容而非推断词义。故选C。
