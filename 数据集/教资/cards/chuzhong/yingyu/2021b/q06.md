【2021b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

The two cats could be ______ only by the number of the rings on their tails otherwise they were exactly alike.

- A. separated
- B. divided
- C. disconnected
- D. differentiated

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The two cats could be ______ only by the number of the rings on their tails otherwise they were exactly alike.。选项：Aseparated；Bdivided；Cdisconnected；Ddifferentiated。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「两只猫只能靠尾巴上环的数目来___，否则完全一样」，需表示区分。D「differentiated」意为区分、辨别，符合。A「separated」（分开）、B「divided」（分割）、C「disconnected」（断开）均指物理分离，而题干强调的是辨认区别。故选D。
