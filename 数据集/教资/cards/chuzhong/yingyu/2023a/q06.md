【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

Would you like a cup of coffee ______ shall we get down to business right away?

- A. or
- B. and
- C. then
- D. otherwise

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Would you like a cup of coffee ______ shall we get down to business right away?。选项：Aor；Band；Cthen；Dotherwise。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「Would you like a cup of coffee ___ shall we get down to business right away?」是两种选择的并列疑问，需用or连接。A「or」符合。B「and」表并列肯定、C「then」表顺序、D「otherwise」表否则，均不能构成选择疑问。故选A。
