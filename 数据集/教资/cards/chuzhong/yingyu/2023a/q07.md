【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

Alex specializes in ______ medical science in this graduate school and plans to work as______ doctor upon graduation.

- A. a; the
- B. the; a
- C. /; a
- D. a; /

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Alex specializes in ______ medical science in this graduate school and plans to work as______ doctor upon graduation.。选项：Aa; the；Bthe; a；C/; a；Da; /。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 第一空：specialize in后接学科名称，表示研究领域时表抽象概念，不加冠词，为medial science；第二空：泛指一种职业身份，应用不定冠词a doctor。C「/; a」符合。A、B、D三项分别在第一空或第二空的冠词使用上有误。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
