【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

What is the relationship between the two sentences “The stress is on documentary and rightly so. Arty photographs are a bore.”

- A. Phenomenon-reason
- B. Problem-solution
- C. Instrument-achievement
- D. Cause-consequence

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What is the relationship between the two sentences “The stress is on documentary and rightly so. Arty photographs are a bore.”。选项：APhenomenon-reason；BProblem-solution；CInstrument-achievement；DCause-consequence。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「The stress is on documentary and rightly so. Arty photographs are a bore.」第一句陈述观点，第二句给出支持该观点的理由，构成现象-原因关系。A「Phenomenon-reason」符合。B「Problem-solution」需问题与对策、C「Instrument-achievement」需手段与目标、D「Cause-consequence」是因果导致结果，与「陈述+论证理由」的模式不同。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
