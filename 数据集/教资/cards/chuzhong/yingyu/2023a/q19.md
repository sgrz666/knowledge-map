【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

Which of the following strategies would best facilitate peer learning in writing?

- A. Asking students to grade their peers' writing.
- B. Providing students with an evaluation checklist.
- C. Encouraging students to give only positive feedback.
- D. Instructing students to focus on only one type of error.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following strategies would best facilitate peer learning in writing?。选项：AAsking students to grade their peers' writing.；BProviding students with an evaluation checklist.；CEncouraging students to give only positive feedback.；DInstructing students to focus on only one type of error.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 促进写作中的同伴学习，需让同伴之间产生实质的相互评价与反馈。A「Asking students to grade their peers' writing」让学生评判同伴作文，需阅读理解并给出判断，最有利于同伴互学。B「Providing an evaluation checklist」仅提供工具，缺少同伴间的互动；C「give only positive feedback」限制反馈的实质作用；D「focus on only one type of error」把注意力窄化。故选A。
