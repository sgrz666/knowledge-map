【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

Which of the following might be the most appropriate in developing students’ critical thinking through reading?

- A. Students read a story and study grammar rules.
- B. Students listen to a story and read it aloud.
- C. Students read a story and evaluate it.
- D. Students read a story and recite it.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following might be the most appropriate in developing students’ critical thinking through reading?。选项：AStudents read a story and study grammar rules.；BStudents listen to a story and read it aloud.；CStudents read a story and evaluate it.；DStudents read a story and recite it.。给定答案：C
