【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

That prestigious university has made it clear to all applicants that nobody can be ______ for a scholarship this quarter without a good internship.

- A. eligible
- B. legible
- C. compliable
- D. compliant

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：That prestigious university has made it clear to all applicants that nobody can be ______ for a scholarship this quarter without a good internship.。选项：Aeligible；Blegible；Ccompliable；Dcompliant。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「没有良好实习经历的学生本季度无法___申请奖学金」，需表示「有资格的」。A「eligible」意为有资格的，eligible for a scholarship即符合奖学金申请条件。B「legible」（字迹清晰的）、C「compliable」（顺从的，拼写变体）、D「compliant」（服从的）均与资格无关。故选A。
