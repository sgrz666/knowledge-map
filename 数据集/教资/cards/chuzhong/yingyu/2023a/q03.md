【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

Mr. Smith is surprised to learn that Siamak has written ______ essays on Victorian novels in his class.

- A. the next
- B. the other
- C. the most
- D. the more

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Mr. Smith is surprised to learn that Siamak has written ______ essays on Victorian novels in his class.。选项：Athe next；Bthe other；Cthe most；Dthe more。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「Mr. Smith很惊讶地得知Siamak在课上写了___关于维多利亚小说的论文」，需表示「最多的」。C「the most」符合「the most essays」（数量最多）的语义。A「the next」表顺序、B「the other」表另外一个（通常接单数或复数但指特定范围）、D「the more」用于两者比较，均不表示「数量最多」。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
