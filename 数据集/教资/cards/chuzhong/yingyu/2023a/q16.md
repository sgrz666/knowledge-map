【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

When a teacher says“Sorry I didn't get what you said.Can you say it again?” he/she is ______.

- A. offering an opinion
- B. asking for advice
- C. asking for clarification
- D. checking instruction

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher says“Sorry I didn't get what you said.Can you say it again?” he/she is ______.。选项：Aoffering an opinion；Basking for advice；Casking for clarification；Dchecking instruction。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师说「Sorry, I didn't get what you said. Can you say it again?」是在请求学生澄清或重复，属请求澄清。C「asking for clarification」符合。A「offering an opinion」是提出观点、B「asking for advice」是征求意见、D「checking instruction」是核对学生是否听懂指令，均与语境不符。故选C。
