【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

When students are asked to listen to a recording and read after it they are doing ______.

- A. free practice
- B. guided practice
- C. meaningful practice
- D. controlled practice

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When students are asked to listen to a recording and read after it they are doing ______.。选项：Afree practice；Bguided practice；Cmeaningful practice；Dcontrolled practice。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 听录音跟读，学生的语言输出完全由录音材料控制，属控制性练习。D「controlled practice」符合。A「free practice」为自由练习、B「guided practice」为有指导的半开放练习、C「meaningful practice」为意义性练习，三者均允许学生自主产出，与跟读不符。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、学科教学方法与学习理论；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、合作学习
