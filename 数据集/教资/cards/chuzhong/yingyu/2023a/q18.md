【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following is least important in developing school-based teaching materials?

- A. Students' level and interest.
- B. Teachers' professional expertise.
- C. The aim and need of your school.
- D. The form of the teaching materials.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is least important in developing school-based teaching materials?。选项：AStudents' level and interest.；BTeachers' professional expertise.；CThe aim and need of your school.；DThe form of the teaching materials.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 校本教材开发需考虑学生水平与兴趣、教师专业能力、学校目标与需求等实质要素，而教材的形式属技术性细节，相对最不重要。D「The form of the teaching materials」符合。A、B、C三项均直接影响教材的适切性与质量。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：教学资源与板书设计、课程目标与课程内容标准；关键词：多媒体、实验设计、导入、教具、教学资源、板书、核心素养、课件
