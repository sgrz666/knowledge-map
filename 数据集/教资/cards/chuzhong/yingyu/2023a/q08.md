【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

The reason why I want to give up the plan is ______ if I don't my employer will be mad at me.

- A. because
- B. that
- C. for
- D. since

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The reason why I want to give up the plan is ______ if I don't my employer will be mad at me.。选项：Abecause；Bthat；Cfor；Dsince。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** The reason why... is that... 是固定句型，表语从句由that引导。B「that」符合。A「because」与The reason连用构成冗余；C「for」不能引导表语从句；D「since」表原因或时间，不能在此作表语从句引导词。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
