【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

Which of the following activities is suitable to be conducted at the “production” step?

- A. Debating.
- B. Mimicking.
- C. Reciting.
- D. Repeating.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities is suitable to be conducted at the “production” step?。选项：ADebating.；BMimicking.；CReciting.；DRepeating.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 产出（production）阶段要求学生自由运用所学语言完成真实交际任务。A「Debating」辩论需即时组织语言、表达观点，属产出性活动。B「Mimicking」模仿、C「Reciting」背诵、D「Repeating」重复均属控制性操练，发生在呈现与练习阶段。故选A。
