【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Which of the following correctly describes the semantic features of the word “girl”?

- A. [-Human-Male-Adult]
- B. [+Human+Male-Adult]
- C. [+Human-Male+Adult]
- D. [+Human-Male-Adult]

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following correctly describes the semantic features of the word “girl”?。选项：A[-Human-Male-Adult]；B[+Human+Male-Adult]；C[+Human-Male+Adult]；D[+Human-Male-Adult]。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** girl的语义特征为[+Human]（人）、[-Male]（非男性）、[-Adult]（非成年）。D「[+Human-Male-Adult]」符合。A项漏标[+Human]；B项[+Male]表明为男性，与girl矛盾；C项[+Adult]表明成年，将girl误作成年女性（woman）。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
