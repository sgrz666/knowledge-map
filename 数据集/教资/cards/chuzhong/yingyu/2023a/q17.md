【2023a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

When students take an end-of-term exam to assess how well they have learnt in the past term they are taking a(n) ______.

- A. aptitude test
- B. proficiency test
- C. placement test
- D. achievement test

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When students take an end-of-term exam to assess how well they have learnt in the past term they are taking a(n) ______.。选项：Aaptitude test；Bproficiency test；Cplacement test；Dachievement test。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 学期末考试检验学生一学期所学，与教学内容直接对应，属成绩测试。D「achievement test」符合。A「aptitude test」测语言学习潜能、B「proficiency test」测总体语言水平、C「placement test」用于分班，均不以特定教学内容为依据。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：学习评价方法与证据；关键词：作业、学习任务单、形成性、测试、测验、终结性、评价、评分
