【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

In trying to get across a message an EFL learner may use __________ strategies to make up for a lack of knowledge of grammar or vocabulary.

- A. communicative
- B. cognitive
- C. resourcing
- D. affective

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In trying to get across a message an EFL learner may use __________ strategies to make up for a lack of knowledge of grammar or vocabulary.。选项：Acommunicative；Bcognitive；Cresourcing；Daffective。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 学习者语法或词汇知识不足时，会用迂回描述、借用近义词等方式弥补交际障碍，这类策略属于交际策略。A「communicative」符合。B「cognitive」指认知策略，用于信息加工与记忆；C「resourcing」是学习策略中的一种具体手段；D「affective」指情感策略。题干「make up for a lack of knowledge」正是交际策略的功能。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇、英语语法与句法、听说与交际教学；关键词：agreement、audio-lingual、clause、communicative、gerund、grammar、lexical、listening
