【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

Which indefinite article "a" should be read emphatically in the following sentences?

- A. He is a handsome boy but not smart.
- B. He is not a suspect he is the suspect.
- C. He bought a cartoon book for his son.
- D. He is talking with a middle-aged man.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which indefinite article "a" should be read emphatically in the following sentences?。选项：AHe is a handsome boy but not smart.；BHe is not a suspect he is the suspect.；CHe bought a cartoon book for his son.；DHe is talking with a middle-aged man.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 不定冠词a通常弱读，只有在表示强调「某一个（而非其他）」时才重读。B「He is not a suspect, he is the suspect.」中a与the形成对比，强调「不是普通嫌犯而是那名嫌犯」，故a须重读以突出对比。A、C、D三句中的a均为泛指，不承担对比强调功能，正常弱读。故选B。
