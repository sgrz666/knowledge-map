【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第1题】

It was such a (an) __________ when they met each other in Beijing because each thought that the other was still in Hong Kong.

- A. occurrence
- B. chance
- C. coincidence
- D. occasion

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It was such a (an) __________ when they met each other in Beijing because each thought that the other was still in Hong Kong.。选项：Aoccurrence；Bchance；Ccoincidence；Doccasion。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「在北京相遇时真是一次___，因为两人都以为对方还在香港」，强调巧合。C「coincidence」（巧合）符合。A「occurrence」（发生的事）侧重事件本身，不含巧合义；B「chance」（机会、偶然）不能与such a an搭配表示「巧合事件」；D「occasion」（场合、时机）指特定时刻。故选C。
