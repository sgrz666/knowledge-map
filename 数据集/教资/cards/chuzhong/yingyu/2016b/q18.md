【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following is a typical feature of formal writing?

- A. Archaic words are usually preferred.
- B. The precision of language is a priority.
- C. Short and incomplete sentences are preferred.
- D. An intimate relationship with the audience is established.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is a typical feature of formal writing?。选项：AArchaic words are usually preferred.；BThe precision of language is a priority.；CShort and incomplete sentences are preferred.；DAn intimate relationship with the audience is established.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 正式写作以语言的准确性为首要追求。B「The precision of language is a priority」符合。A「Archaic words are usually preferred」错误——古旧词汇并非正式写作的普遍偏好；C「Short and incomplete sentences are preferred」是非正式写作的特征；D「An intimate relationship with the audience is established」也是非正式写作的特点。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
