【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

We have no trust in him because he has never__________ the grandiose promises he makes.

- A. delivered on
- B. eaten off
- C. forgotten about
- D. abided by

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：We have no trust in him because he has never__________ the grandiose promises he makes.。选项：Adelivered on；Beaten off；Cforgotten about；Dabided by。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「他许下宏大承诺却从不___」，需表示「履行（承诺）」。A「delivered on」意为「履行、兑现」，deliver on a promise即兑现承诺。B「eaten off」无此义；C「forgotten about」（忘记）与「不信任他」的因果关系较弱；D「abided by」意为「遵守（规则、决定）」，宾语通常为法律、协议而非promise。故选A。
