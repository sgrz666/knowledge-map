【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Due to the __________influence some Chinese learners of English wrongly passivize intransitive verbs like "die" as in "John was died last year".

- A. intedingual
- B. intercultural
- C. intralingual
- D. intmcultural

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Due to the __________influence some Chinese learners of English wrongly passivize intransitive verbs like "die" as in "John was died last year".。选项：Aintedingual；Bintercultural；Cintralingual；Dintmcultural。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干描述的是学习者因目标语内部规则的影响而错误类推（把不及物动词die误用为被动），这属于语内迁移（intralingual transfer），即目标语内部规则的过度泛化。C「intralingual」符合。A「intedingual」拼写有误且非标准术语；B「intercultural」（跨文化的）与语言迁移无关；D「intmcultural」拼写错误。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：二语习得与学习策略；关键词：acquisition、interlanguage、learning strateg、motivation、transfer、二语、语言习得
