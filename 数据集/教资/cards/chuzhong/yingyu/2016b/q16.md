【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

It is suggested that lower-level EFL learners learn to read by reading __________ materials.

- A. simple and authentic
- B. academic and authentic
- C. original and classical
- D. classical and authentic

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It is suggested that lower-level EFL learners learn to read by reading __________ materials.。选项：Asimple and authentic；Bacademic and authentic；Coriginal and classical；Dclassical and authentic。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 低水平EFL学习者阅读的材料应兼具「简单」与「真实」——语言难度适中以保证可理解，同时保留真实语料的自然特征以培养真实阅读能力。A「simple and authentic」符合。B「academic and authentic」中academic材料难度过高；C「original and classical」与D「classical and authentic」中的classical（古典的）语言难度大、时代感强，不适合低水平学习者。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语阅读理解、语言技能综合教学；关键词：according to the、author、infer、main idea、passage、post-reading、pre-reading、reading comprehension
