【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Which of the following assumptions fails to describe the nature of vocabulary or vocabulary learning?

- A. Words are best learned in context.
- B. A lexical item can be more than one word.
- C. All words in one language have equivalents in another.
- D. Learning a word includes learning its form meaning and use.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following assumptions fails to describe the nature of vocabulary or vocabulary learning?。选项：AWords are best learned in context.；BA lexical item can be more than one word.；CAll words in one language have equivalents in another.；DLearning a word includes learning its form meaning and use.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「一种语言中的所有词在另一种语言中都有对应词」这一假设不成立——不同语言的词汇系统并不同构，许多词在另一语言中找不到完全对等的表达（如文化特有词）。C项是失败的假设。A「Words are best learned in context」符合语境词汇学习观；B「A lexical item can be more than one word」符合词块理论；D「Learning a word includes learning its form, meaning and use」是国家英语课程标准提出的词汇学习三维度。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇；关键词：lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary、word formation、构词、词汇
