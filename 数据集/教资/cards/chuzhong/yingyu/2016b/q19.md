【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

Which of the following writing activities may be used to develop students´ skill of planning?

- A. Editing their writing in groups.
- B. Self-checking punctuations in their writing.
- C. Sorting out ideas and putting them in order.
- D. Cross-checking the language in their writing.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following writing activities may be used to develop students´ skill of planning?。选项：AEditing their writing in groups.；BSelf-checking punctuations in their writing.；CSorting out ideas and putting them in order.；DCross-checking the language in their writing.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 写作过程中的「构思/规划」（planning）阶段需梳理观点并确定顺序。C「Sorting out ideas and putting them in order」符合。A「Editing their writing in groups」属修改阶段；B「Self-checking punctuations」与D「Cross-checking the language」属校对（editing）阶段，均发生在写作之后。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
