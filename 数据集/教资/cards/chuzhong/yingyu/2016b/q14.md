【2016b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

When asking students to quickly run their eyes over a whole text to get the gist we are training their skill of__________.

- A. scanning
- B. mapping
- C. predicting
- D. skimming

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When asking students to quickly run their eyes over a whole text to get the gist we are training their skill of__________.。选项：Ascanning；Bmapping；Cpredicting；Dskimming。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 快速浏览全文以获取大意是略读技能。D「skimming」（略读）符合。A「scanning」（扫读）是快速查找特定信息；B「mapping」（图式化）指将信息组织成图形；C「predicting」（预测）指根据线索推测后续内容。题干「quickly run their eyes over a whole text to get the gist」正是略读的定义。故选D。
