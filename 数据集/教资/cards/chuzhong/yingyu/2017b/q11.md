【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Which of the following instructions is helpful in developing students´ ability to make inferences?

- A. Listen to a story and write a summary．
- B. Listen to a story and work out the writer' s intention．
- C. Listen to the story of a boy and then draw a picture of him．
- D. Listen to a story and note down the specific date of an event．

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following instructions is helpful in developing students´ ability to make inferences?。选项：AListen to a story and write a summary．；BListen to a story and work out the writer' s intention．；CListen to the story of a boy and then draw a picture of him．；DListen to a story and note down the specific date of an event．。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 推断（make inferences）要求依据已知信息推出未明说的内容。B「Listen to a story and work out the writer's intention」要求推断作者意图，属推断技能。A「write a summary」是概括；C「draw a picture of him」是信息转换；D「note down the specific date」是获取具体信息。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语阅读理解、听说与交际教学；关键词：according to the、audio-lingual、author、communicative、infer、listening、main idea、oral
