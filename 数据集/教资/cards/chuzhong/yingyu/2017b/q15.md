【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

If a teacher asks students to make their own learning plan he/she is trying to develop their_________．

- A. cognitive strategy
- B. affective strategy
- C. communicative strategy
- D. metacognitive strategy

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher asks students to make their own learning plan he/she is trying to develop their_________．。选项：Acognitive strategy；Baffective strategy；Ccommunicative strategy；Dmetacognitive strategy。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 制定个人学习计划、监控并调整自身学习进程，属于元认知策略。D「metacognitive strategy」符合。A「cognitive strategy」指直接处理学习材料的具体方法（如推理、笔记）；B「affective strategy」指调节情绪与态度；C「communicative strategy」指克服交际障碍的策略。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
