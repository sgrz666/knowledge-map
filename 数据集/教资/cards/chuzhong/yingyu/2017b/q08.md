【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

_________ ministers decide to instigate an inquiry we -----welcome it．

- A. Should ．．． would
- B. Suppose ．．． could
- C. If．．． would
- D. Would ．．． will

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：_________ ministers decide to instigate an inquiry we -----welcome it．。选项：AShould ．．． would；BSuppose ．．． could；CIf．．． would；DWould ．．． will。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** Should ministers decide to instigate an inquiry 是虚拟条件句的倒装形式，相当于If ministers should decide...，主句用would welcome。A「Should...would」符合。B「Suppose...could」中suppose虽可引导条件但could与语义不合；C「If...would」中if从句须用were to或should，直接接动词原形不成立；D「Would...will」不能构成条件句。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
