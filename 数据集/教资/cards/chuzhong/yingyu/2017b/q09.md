【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

What is missing at the discourse level between the two sentences "Carol loves tomatoes． She was born in Africa．"?

- A. Reference．
- B. Cohesion．
- C. Coherence．
- D. Substitution．

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What is missing at the discourse level between the two sentences "Carol loves tomatoes． She was born in Africa．"?。选项：AReference．；BCohesion．；CCoherence．；DSubstitution．。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 两句语法均正确、各自完整，但「Carol loves tomatoes」与「She was born in Africa」之间缺乏语义上的逻辑关联，读者无法建立意义连接，缺少的是连贯性（coherence）。C符合。A「Reference」指指代关系，第二句的She已正确指代Carol；B「Cohesion」指衔接手段，此处it/she等衔接词并不缺失；D「Substitution」指替代，非问题所在。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学、英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、discourse、essay、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics
