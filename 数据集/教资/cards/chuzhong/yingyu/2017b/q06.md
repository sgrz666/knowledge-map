【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

--Will John come to the party?
--No he won´t come and__________．

- A. Antony neither won' t
- B. won' t Antony neither
- C. either will Antony
- D. neither will Antony

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：--Will John come to the party?
--No he won´t come and__________．。选项：AAntony neither won' t；Bwon' t Antony neither；Ceither will Antony；Dneither will Antony。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「No, he won't come」表示否定，后接「也不……」应用neither引导的倒装句，即neither will Antony。D符合。A「Antony neither won't」语序与双重否定均误；B「won't Antony neither」语序错误；C「either will Antony」either用于否定句时须置于句末且不倒装。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
