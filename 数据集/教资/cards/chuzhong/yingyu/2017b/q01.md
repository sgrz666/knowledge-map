【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第1题】

The main difference between/e/and/ə/lies in the__________．

- A. tongue position
- B. sound duration
- C. openness of the mouth
- D. shape of the lips

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The main difference between/e/and/ə/lies in the__________．。选项：Atongue position；Bsound duration；Copenness of the mouth；Dshape of the lips。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** /e/与/ə/均为中元音且双唇不收圆，主要区别在于舌位：/e/为前元音（舌前部抬起），/ə/为央元音（舌中部抬起）。A「tongue position」符合。B「sound duration」并非二者的区分特征；C「openness of the mouth」二者开合度相近；D「shape of the lips」二者均不圆唇，无区别。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
