【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

When a teacher tells students that the word "dog" may imply "loyalty" he/she is teaching the _________of the word．

- A. denotative meaning
- B. conocative meaning
- C. conceptual meaning
- D. connotative meaning

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher tells students that the word "dog" may imply "loyalty" he/she is teaching the _________of the word．。选项：Adenotative meaning；Bconocative meaning；Cconceptual meaning；Dconnotative meaning。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** dog一词暗示「忠诚」，是词在概念意义之外附加的联想意义，即内涵意义。D「connotative meaning」符合。A「denotative meaning」与C「conceptual meaning」均指词的字面概念所指（犬类动物）；B「conocative meaning」拼写有误且非标准术语。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
