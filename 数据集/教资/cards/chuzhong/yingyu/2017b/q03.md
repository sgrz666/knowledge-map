【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

We´re all keeping our__________ crossed that the surgeons do not find anything too serious with the patient．

- A. fingers
- B. thumbs
- C. hands
- D. feet

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：We´re all keeping our__________ crossed that the surgeons do not find anything too serious with the patient．。选项：Afingers；Bthumbs；Chands；Dfeet。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** keep one's fingers crossed是英语习语，意为「交叉手指祈求好运」，符合「企盼外科医生不要发现严重问题」的语境。B「thumbs」无此习语搭配；C「hands」与D「feet」同样不构成该习语。故选A。
