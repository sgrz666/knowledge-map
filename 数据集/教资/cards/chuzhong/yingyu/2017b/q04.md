【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

When implementing the project we__________a lot of unexpected opposition from our colleagues．

- A. run for
- B. make against
- C. run up against
- D. make away with

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When implementing the project we__________a lot of unexpected opposition from our colleagues．。选项：Arun for；Bmake against；Crun up against；Dmake away with。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「实施该项目时，我们___来自同事的许多意外反对」需表示「遭遇、碰到（困难）」。C「run up against」意为「遭遇（困难、反对）」，符合语义。A「run for」意为「竞选」；B「make against」无此搭配；D「make away with」意为「偷走、带走」。故选C。
