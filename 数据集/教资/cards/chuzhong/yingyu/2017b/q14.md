【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

Which of the following activities is often used to develop students´ speaking accuracy?

- A. Identifying and correcting oral mistakes．
- B. Acting out the dialogue in the text．
- C. Having discussions in groups．
- D. Describing people in pair．

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities is often used to develop students´ speaking accuracy?。选项：AIdentifying and correcting oral mistakes．；BActing out the dialogue in the text．；CHaving discussions in groups．；DDescribing people in pair．。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 口语准确性（speaking accuracy）训练需关注语言形式的正确性。A「Identifying and correcting oral mistakes」识别并纠正口语错误，直接指向准确性。B「Acting out the dialogue」表演对话侧重流利与情境运用；C「Having discussions in groups」与D「Describing people in pair」均以交际流畅为主，不特别关注形式准确。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
