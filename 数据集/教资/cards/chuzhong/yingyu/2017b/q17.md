【2017b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

Which of the following is the last step in the process of writing essays?

- A. Editing the writings．
- B. Writing topic sentences for paragraphs．
- C. Gathering information and ideas relevant to the topic．
- D. Organizing the information and ideas into a logical sequence．

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is the last step in the process of writing essays?。选项：AEditing the writings．；BWriting topic sentences for paragraphs．；CGathering information and ideas relevant to the topic．；DOrganizing the information and ideas into a logical sequence．。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 写作过程的环节依次为：搜集信息（C）→ 组织信息（D）→ 撰写段落主题句（B）→ 修改校对（A）。故最后一步为编辑修改。A「Editing the writings」符合。B、C、D均发生在成文之前或之中。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
