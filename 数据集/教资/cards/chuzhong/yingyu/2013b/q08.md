【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

In most circumstances，the assumption of cooperation is so pervasive that it can be stated as a cooperative principle of conversation and elaborated in maxims below except（ ）.

- A. manner maxim
- B. quality maxim
- C. quantity maxim
- D. agreement maxim

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In most circumstances，the assumption of cooperation is so pervasive that it can be stated as a cooperative principle of conversation and elaborated in maxims below except（ ）.。选项：Amanner maxim；Bquality maxim；Cquantity maxim；Dagreement maxim。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** Grice合作原则四准则：数量（quantity）、质量（quality）、关系（relation）、方式（manner）。无『agreement maxim（一致准则）』。A、B、C均属四准则。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
