【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

If a teacher attempts to implement the top-down model to teach listening，he is likely to present（ ）.

- A. new words after playing the tape
- B. new words before playing the tape
- C. background information after playing the tape
- D. background information before playing the tape

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher attempts to implement the top-down model to teach listening，he is likely to present（ ）.。选项：Anew words after playing the tape；Bnew words before playing the tape；Cbackground information after playing the tape；Dbackground information before playing the tape。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 听力采用自上而下（top-down）模式时，应先呈现背景信息（background information before playing）。A、B放音后呈现属bottom-up；C放音后也不符。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
