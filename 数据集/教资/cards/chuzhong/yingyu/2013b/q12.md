【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

When a teacher intends to introduce a new grammar item，which of the following strategies can be used to get students to notice it?（ ）.

- A. Transformation
- B. Input enhancement
- C. Expansion
- D. Substitution

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher intends to introduce a new grammar item，which of the following strategies can be used to get students to notice it?（ ）.。选项：ATransformation；BInput enhancement；CExpansion；DSubstitution。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 引导学生注意新语法点可用输入强化（input enhancement）。A转换、C扩展、D替换均非『引起注意』的典型策略。故选B。
