【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Despite differences in conversation style speakers are helped by automatic patterns in daily interactions such as “Hi” known as（ ）.

- A. adjacency pairs
- B. preference structure
- C. dispreference structure
- D. incomplete sequence

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Despite differences in conversation style speakers are helped by automatic patterns in daily interactions such as “Hi” known as（ ）.。选项：Aadjacency pairs；Bpreference structure；Cdispreference structure；Dincomplete sequence。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 日常互动中『Hi』这类自动化问候模式属于偏好结构（preference structure）中的偏好回应。A相邻对、C不偏好结构、D不完整序列均不符。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
