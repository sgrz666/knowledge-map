【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

When checking students’ understanding of a certain language point in class，which of the following utterances is a teacher expected to make?（ ）

- A. “Is it okay?”
- B. “Is it clear to you?”
- C. “Are you clear?”
- D. “Is it all fight to everyone?”

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When checking students’ understanding of a certain language point in class，which of the following utterances is a teacher expected to make?（ ）。选项：A“Is it okay?”；B“Is it clear to you?”；C“Are you clear?”；D“Is it all fight to everyone?”。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 检查学生是否理解某语言点，应问『Is it clear to you?（你清楚吗）』。A『Is it okay?』、C『Are you clear?』、D错误表达均不如B自然得体。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
