【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

It was with great joy（ ）he knew that his GMAT score as 670，high enough to apply to a top university of business.

- A. when
- B. with
- C. what
- D. that

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It was with great joy（ ）he knew that his GMAT score as 670，high enough to apply to a top university of business.。选项：Awhen；Bwith；Cwhat；Dthat。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『It was with great joy ____ he knew…』为强调句，强调介词短语用『It was…that』。D that正确；A when、B with、C what均不能引导强调句。故选D。
