【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

At a higher level of writing which of the following cognitive skills should NOT be encouraged?（ ）

- A. Constructing grammatically correct sentences only.
- B. Gathering information and ideas relevant to the topic.
- C. Using cohesive devices to create sections and paragraphs.
- D. Organizing the information and ideas into a logical sequence.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：At a higher level of writing which of the following cognitive skills should NOT be encouraged?（ ）。选项：AConstructing grammatically correct sentences only.；BGathering information and ideas relevant to the topic.；CUsing cohesive devices to create sections and paragraphs.；DOrganizing the information and ideas into a logical sequence.。给定答案：A
