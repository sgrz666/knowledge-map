【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

（ ）your valuable help we couldn’t have finished the experiment ahead of time.

- A. If it were not for
- B. Had it not been for
- C. Were it not for
- D. If it has not been for

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：（ ）your valuable help we couldn’t have finished the experiment ahead of time.。选项：AIf it were not for；BHad it not been for；CWere it not for；DIf it has not been for。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『____ your valuable help we couldn’t have finished』与过去事实相反，需用『if it had not been for』或其倒装『Had it not been for』。B符合；A、C为现在虚拟，D时态错误。故选B。
