【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

When a student has made a sentence “I borrowed a paper from Miss Li"，the teacher says，“Do you mean‘ a piece of paper’?” Here the teacher is playing the role of a(n)（ ）.

- A. controller
- B. prompter
- C. assessor
- D. organizer

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a student has made a sentence “I borrowed a paper from Miss Li"，the teacher says，“Do you mean‘ a piece of paper’?” Here the teacher is playing the role of a(n)（ ）.。选项：Acontroller；Bprompter；Cassessor；Dorganizer。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师说『你是说a piece of paper吗』是在指出并纠正学生的量词错误，扮演评价/反馈者（assessor）。A控制者、B推动者、D组织者均不符。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织、教学诊断与反思改进；关键词：不足、学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、改进、教学反思
