The underlined word “scribbled” in Paragraph 4 means“（ ）”.

- A. pasted
- B. written
- C. scrawled
- D. attached

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The underlined word “scribbled” in Paragraph 4 means“（ ）”.。选项：Apasted；Bwritten；Cscrawled；Dattached。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『scribbled』意为潦草写、乱涂，近义scrawled（潦草地写）。A粘贴、B写（泛称）、D附着均不符。故选C。
