【2013b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

In a complex integration task a teacher is expected to devise a series of activities which are（ ）linked.

- A. thematically
- B. syntactically
- C. semantically
- D. linguistically

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In a complex integration task a teacher is expected to devise a series of activities which are（ ）linked.。选项：Athematically；Bsyntactically；Csemantically；Dlinguistically。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 综合任务中活动应『主题上』关联（thematically linked）。B句法、C语义、D语言学的关联不如主题关联贴切。故选A。
