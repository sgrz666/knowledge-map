【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

In writing students may not know how to put something into proper English and thus ask their teacher for help． Here the teacher is to play the role of a/an __________．

- A. facilitator
- B. assessor
- C. controller
- D. participant

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In writing students may not know how to put something into proper English and thus ask their teacher for help． Here the teacher is to play the role of a/an __________．。选项：Afacilitator；Bassessor；Ccontroller；Dparticipant。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 学生写作遇到表达困难时向教师求助，教师提供语言支持与帮助，扮演的是A「facilitator」（促进者）。B「assessor」负责评价学生表现；C「controller」控制课堂进程与内容；D「participant」作为参与者加入活动。题干情境是提供帮助而非评价或控制。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
