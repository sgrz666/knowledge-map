Zhang Jun´s teacher suggests that he take a (n) _________ language course to improve his English．

- A. intermediate
- B. middle
- C. medium
- D. average

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Zhang Jun´s teacher suggests that he take a (n) _________ language course to improve his English．。选项：Aintermediate；Bmiddle；Cmedium；Daverage。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 描述语言课程的级别用A「intermediate」（中级的），与beginner、advanced构成级别序列。B「middle」指空间或顺序上的中间；C「medium」指媒介或尺寸上的中等；D「average」指平均水平。三者均不用于课程分级。故选A。
