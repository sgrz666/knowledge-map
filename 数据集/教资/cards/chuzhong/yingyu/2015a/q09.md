【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

What would you say to the hotel receptionist to get a double room?

- A. Rent us a double room．
- B. Book us a double room．
- C. We'd like to have a double room．
- D. Let' s have a double room．

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What would you say to the hotel receptionist to get a double room?。选项：ARent us a double room．；BBook us a double room．；CWe'd like to have a double room．；DLet' s have a double room．。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 对酒店接待员提出要求应使用礼貌的意愿表达。C「We'd like to have a double room.」以We'd like to表达意愿，语气得体。A「Rent us...」与B「Book us...」均为命令式，对服务人员不礼貌；D「Let's have...」是向同伴提议，不是向接待员请求。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
