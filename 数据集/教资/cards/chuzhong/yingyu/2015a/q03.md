【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

Jimmy asked Mary to lend him some money which she agreed to _________ that he paid her back the following month．

- A. on occasion
- B. in case
- C. under circumstances
- D. on condition

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Jimmy asked Mary to lend him some money which she agreed to _________ that he paid her back the following month．。选项：Aon occasion；Bin case；Cunder circumstances；Don condition。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「agreed to lend him money ___ that he paid her back」需要一个引出条件的短语。D「on condition that」意为「条件是」，正合语境。A「on occasion」意为「偶尔」，表频率；B「in case」意为「万一」，表预防；C「under circumstances」搭配不完整（通常为under the circumstances「在这种情况下」或under no circumstances「决不」）。故选D。
