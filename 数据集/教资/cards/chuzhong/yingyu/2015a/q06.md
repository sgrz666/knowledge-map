【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

_________conscious of her moral obligations as a citizen．

- A. Marsha was and always will be
- B. Marsha has to be and always will be
- C. Marsha had been and will always be
- D. Marsha has been and will always be

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：_________conscious of her moral obligations as a citizen．。选项：AMarsha was and always will be；BMarsha has to be and always will be；CMarsha had been and will always be；DMarsha has been and will always be。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 并列结构要求时态在语义上连贯衔接。D「has been and will always be」用现在完成时表示「一直如此至今」，与will always be构成「过去延续到现在并将继续」的完整语义链。A「was and always will be」的过去时与将来时之间缺少连接现在的时间点，语义断裂；B「has to be」意为「必须」，与「意识到义务」的状态描述不符；C「had been」为过去完成时，与后面的将来时不协调。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
