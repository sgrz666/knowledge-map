【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

There are two syllables in the word "photo"．/əu/occurs in both syllables but the first one is__________the second because it is stressed．

- A. longer than
- B. shorter than
- C. as long as
- D. as short as

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：There are two syllables in the word "photo"．/əu/occurs in both syllables but the first one is__________the second because it is stressed．。选项：Alonger than；Bshorter than；Cas long as；Das short as。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 英语重读音节的元音在时长上明显长于非重读音节。/əu/在photo的两个音节中均出现，但第一音节为重读，故其元音更长。B「shorter than」与重读元音更长的规律相反；C「as long as」与D「as short as」否认了重读带来的时长差异。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
