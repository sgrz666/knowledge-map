【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

The first P in the PPP teaching model stands for __________ which aims to get learners to perceive the form and meaning of a structure．

- A. practice
- B. production
- C. presentation
- D. preparation

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The first P in the PPP teaching model stands for __________ which aims to get learners to perceive the form and meaning of a structure．。选项：Apractice；Bproduction；Cpresentation；Dpreparation。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** PPP教学模式指Presentation（呈现）、Practice（练习）、Production（产出）三阶段。第一个P为C「presentation」，其目的正是让学生初步感知新语言结构的形式与意义。A「practice」是第二个P；B「production」是第三个P；D「preparation」不属于该模型。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：学科教学方法与学习理论、听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、合作学习
