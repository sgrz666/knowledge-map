【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

If a teacher asks students to concentrate on such features as structure coherence and cohesion of a text he/she aims at developing students´__________．

- A. strategic competence
- B. cultural awareness
- C. communicative competence
- D. discourse awareness

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher asks students to concentrate on such features as structure coherence and cohesion of a text he/she aims at developing students´__________．。选项：Astrategic competence；Bcultural awareness；Ccommunicative competence；Ddiscourse awareness。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干涉及structure、coherence（连贯）、cohesion（衔接），均属语篇层面的特征，对应D「discourse awareness」（语篇意识）。A「strategic competence」指交际策略运用能力；B「cultural awareness」指文化意识；C「communicative competence」涵盖面过宽，不是针对语篇特征的精准表述。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章、语义语用与语言学、听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、coherence、cohesion、communicative、composition、discourse、essay、implicature
