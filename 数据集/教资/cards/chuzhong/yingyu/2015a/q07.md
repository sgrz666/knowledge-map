【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

Potential subjects should be informed of the research and __________ an opportunity to decide if they want to participate．

- A. gave
- B. giving
- C. give
- D. be given

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Potential subjects should be informed of the research and __________ an opportunity to decide if they want to participate．。选项：Agave；Bgiving；Cgive；Dbe given。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 句中should be informed of the research 与____ an opportunity 为并列的被动结构，故需D「be given」。其完整形式为 should be informed ... and (should) be given an opportunity。A「gave」与C「give」为主动语态，主语subjects是接受动作的一方；B「giving」为非谓语形式，不能与前面的被动结构并列。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
