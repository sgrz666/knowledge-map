【2015a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

The __________ method is more fitted to the explicit presentation of grammar when the basic structure is being identified．

- A. inductive
- B. contrastive
- C. comparative
- D. deductive

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The __________ method is more fitted to the explicit presentation of grammar when the basic structure is being identified．。选项：Ainductive；Bcontrastive；Ccomparative；Ddeductive。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 演绎法（D「deductive」）先呈现语法规则再举例说明，适合在识别基本结构时显性呈现语法。A「inductive」归纳法从例句中总结规则，方向相反；B「contrastive」对比法侧重两种语言的差异比较；C「comparative」比较法侧重相似结构对比。故选D。
