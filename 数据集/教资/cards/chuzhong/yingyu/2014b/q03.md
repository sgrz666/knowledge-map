【2014b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

The study highlights how our sense of right and wrong isn't just based on __________religion and philosophy but also on the biology of our brain．

- A. rising up
- B. bring up
- C. uprising
- D. upbringing

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The study highlights how our sense of right and wrong isn't just based on __________religion and philosophy but also on the biology of our brain．。选项：Arising up；Bbring up；Cuprising；Dupbringing。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 是非对错基于教养（upbringing）而非仅宗教哲学。A rise up、B bring up（动词短语非名词）、C uprising起义均错。故选D。
