【2014b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

Founded in 2003 the company specializes in canned fruits and vegetables and __________food export business．

- A. quick-freezing
- B. quick-frozen
- C. quickly-freezing
- D. quickly-frozen

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Founded in 2003 the company specializes in canned fruits and vegetables and __________food export business．。选项：Aquick-freezing；Bquick-frozen；Cquickly-freezing；Dquickly-frozen。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『____ food』作定语用过去分词frozen，quick为副词修饰，即quick-frozen（速冻的）。A现在分词表进行、C/D副词+分词搭配错。故选B。
