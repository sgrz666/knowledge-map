【2014b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

In teaching pronunciation the teacher should tell the students that__________ can be used to convey more important messages．

- A. rhyme
- B. stress
- C. devoicing
- D. rhythm

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：In teaching pronunciation the teacher should tell the students that__________ can be used to convey more important messages．。选项：Arhyme；Bstress；Cdevoicing；Drhythm。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 语音教学中重音（stress）可传达更重要信息。A韵、C清化、D节奏均不如重音贴切。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
