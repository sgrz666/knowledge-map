How many morphemes does the word "impossible" consist of?

- A. One．
- B. Two．
- C. Three．
- D. Four．

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：How many morphemes does the word "impossible" consist of?。选项：AOne．；BTwo．；CThree．；DFour．。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『impossible』词素为im-（否定）+poss（词根）+ible（后缀），共三个。A一、B二、D四均错。故选C。
