【2014b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

What rhetorical device is used in the underlined part of the sentence There was an elephant pause after the story was told?

- A. Pun．
- B. Simile．
- C. Metaphor．
- D. Transferred epithet．

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What rhetorical device is used in the underlined part of the sentence There was an elephant pause after the story was told?。选项：APun．；BSimile．；CMetaphor．；DTransferred epithet．。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『elephant/eloquent pause』把修饰人的词移用于pause，是移就（transferred epithet）。A双关、B明喻、C暗喻均不符。故选D。
