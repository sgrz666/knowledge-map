【2014b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

When a teacher asks students to discuss how the writer's ideas are organized in the texthe / she intends to develop students' skill of

- A. recognizing the textual structure
- B. understanding the writer's intention
- C. distinguishing facts from opinions
- D. commenting on the content of the text

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher asks students to discuss how the writer's ideas are organized in the texthe / she intends to develop students' skill of。选项：Arecognizing the textual structure；Bunderstanding the writer's intention；Cdistinguishing facts from opinions；Dcommenting on the content of the text。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 让学生讨论作者如何组织观点，培养识别语篇结构（textual structure）。B写作意图、C事实观点、D评论内容均偏。故选A。
