【2014b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

Not a single word__________when he was forced to leave home and join the army in 1937．

- A. did Mark leave
- B. left Mark
- C. did leave Mark
- D. Mark left

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Not a single word__________when he was forced to leave home and join the army in 1937．。选项：Adid Mark leave；Bleft Mark；Cdid leave Mark；DMark left。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 否定词『Not a single word』置句首引起部分倒装『did Mark leave』。B、D未倒装；C『did leave Mark』语序错。故选A。
