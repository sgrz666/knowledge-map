【2014b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第29题】

Which of the following represents the National Commission of Audit's view on school funding?

- A. There should be a national funding model．
- B. Cost of delivering education should not be the major concern of school funding．
- C. There is a close relationship between increased expenditure and raising standards．
- D. The educational department school controls the allocation of funding to independent school．

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following represents the National Commission of Audit's view on school funding?。选项：AThere should be a national funding model．；BCost of delivering education should not be the major concern of school funding．；CThere is a close relationship between increased expenditure and raising standards．；DThe educational department school controls the allocation of funding to independent school．。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 审计委员会认为应有全国统一拨款模式（national funding model）。B成本非重点、C支出与标准相关、D州教育部门控制均与其观点相反。故选A。
