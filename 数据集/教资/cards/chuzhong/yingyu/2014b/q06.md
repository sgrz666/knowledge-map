【2014b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

Tom's school was on the other side of the road so he was told to watch out for cars when __________ the street．

- A. crossing
- B. cross
- C. crossed
- D. to cross

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Tom's school was on the other side of the road so he was told to watch out for cars when __________ the street．。选项：Acrossing；Bcross；Ccrossed；Dto cross。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『when ____ the street』主语he与cross主动，用现在分词crossing作时间状语。B原形、C过去分词、D不定式均错。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
