【2014b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第28题】

How does the author make his arguments convincing to his readers?__________

- A. He cites noted authorities as a means of supporting his opinions.
- B. He presents a thesis and then lists evidence to supporting his opinions.
- C. He summarized an official document and then discusses it in detail.
- D. He uses official documents and then gives his personal interpretation.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：How does the author make his arguments convincing to his readers?__________。选项：AHe cites noted authorities as a means of supporting his opinions.；BHe presents a thesis and then lists evidence to supporting his opinions.；CHe summarized an official document and then discusses it in detail.；DHe uses official documents and then gives his personal interpretation.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 作者援引审计报告、国际研究、学者分析等权威来支撑观点。B提论点再列证据、C总结文件、D官方文件加个人解读均不如A准确（文末明确citing）。故选A。
