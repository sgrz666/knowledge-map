【2014b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

The study highlights how our sense of right and wrong isn't just based on__________ religion and philosophy but also on the biology of our brain.

- A. rising-up
- B. bring-up
- C. uprising
- D. upbringing

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The study highlights how our sense of right and wrong isn't just based on__________ religion and philosophy but also on the biology of our brain.。选项：Arising-up；Bbring-up；Cuprising；Dupbringing。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『right and wrong… based on ____』指教养（upbringing）。A rise up、B bring up（动词短语，非名词）、C uprising起义均错。故选D。
