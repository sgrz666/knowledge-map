【2014b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第24题】

Which of the following is the closest in meaning to "pep up" in the last paragraph?__________

- A. Cheer up.
- B. Feed up.
- C. Steer up.
- D. Take up.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is the closest in meaning to "pep up" in the last paragraph?__________。选项：ACheer up.；BFeed up.；CSteer up.；DTake up.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『pep up』意为使振奋、cheer up。B喂饱、C steer up、D从事均不符。故选A。
