【2014b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

He resented to wait and__________ expected the minister__________him immediately upon his arrival.

- A. to be asked; to see
- B. being asked; to see
- C. to be asked; seeing
- D. being asked; seeing

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：He resented to wait and__________ expected the minister__________him immediately upon his arrival.。选项：Ato be asked; to see；Bbeing asked; to see；Cto be asked; seeing；Dbeing asked; seeing。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『resented ____ to wait』中resent后接动名词being asked（被动）；『expected the minister ____ him』expect后接不定式to see。A、C用to be asked错误；D seeing错误。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
