【2014b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

__________ is NOT included in the factors used in analyzing vowels.

- A. Position of the tongue
- B. Shape of lips
- C. Openness of the mouth
- D. Openness of the vocal cords

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：__________ is NOT included in the factors used in analyzing vowels.。选项：APosition of the tongue；BShape of lips；COpenness of the mouth；DOpenness of the vocal cords。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 分析元音的因素有舌位、唇形、开口度，不含声带张开度（openness of vocal cords）。故选D。
