【2014b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

When a teacher says "Next please pay attention to the time of arrival and departure of the planes in the recording ." he/she intends to develop the students' skill of __________.

- A. predicting
- B. getting the general pictures
- C. distinguishing sounds
- D. getting specific information

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When a teacher says "Next please pay attention to the time of arrival and departure of the planes in the recording ." he/she intends to develop the students' skill of __________.。选项：Apredicting；Bgetting the general pictures；Cdistinguishing sounds；Dgetting specific information。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师说『注意录音中飞机到达与离开时间』，培养获取特定信息（specific information）。A预测、B抓大意、C辨音均非。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
