【2014b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

If a teacher asks students to list as many ways as they can to tell someone to open the door and list the possible functions of a sentence in different contexts he/she is probably trying to highlight __________ .

- A. the potential meanings of a sentence
- B. different realizations of the same function
- C. the relationship between form and function
- D. different ways of getting people to do the same thing

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If a teacher asks students to list as many ways as they can to tell someone to open the door and list the possible functions of a sentence in different contexts he/she is probably trying to highlight __________ .。选项：Athe potential meanings of a sentence；Bdifferent realizations of the same function；Cthe relationship between form and function；Ddifferent ways of getting people to do the same thing。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 列举开门的多种方式并讨论不同语境中句子功能，旨在凸显形式与功能的关系（form and function）。A潜在意义、B同功能不同实现、D不同方式均片面。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
