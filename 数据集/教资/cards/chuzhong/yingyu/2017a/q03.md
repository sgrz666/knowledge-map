【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

Tom is snobbish always _________the influential people."

- A. making out for
- B. making up
- C. making up to
- D. making up for

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Tom is snobbish always _________the influential people."。选项：Amaking out for；Bmaking up；Cmaking up to；Dmaking up for。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「Tom is snobbish, always ___ the influential people」需表示「讨好、巴结」。C「making up to」意为「讨好、奉承（某人）」，符合势利者巴结有权势者的语义。A「making out for」无此搭配；B「making up」意为「编造、化妆、和好」；D「making up for」意为「弥补」。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇；关键词：lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary、word formation、构词、词汇
