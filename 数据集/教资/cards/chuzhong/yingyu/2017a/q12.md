【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

For more advanced learners group work may be more appropriate than pair work for tasks that are __________.

- A. linguistically simple
- B. structurally controlled
- C. cognitively challenging
- D. thematically non-demanding

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：For more advanced learners group work may be more appropriate than pair work for tasks that are __________.。选项：Alinguistically simple；Bstructurally controlled；Ccognitively challenging；Dthematically non-demanding。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 高水平学习者在认知要求高（cognitively challenging）的任务中更需要组内多人协作与观点碰撞，故小组活动比两人活动更合适。C「cognitively challenging」符合。A「linguistically simple」与B「structurally controlled」的简单任务，两人互动即可；D「thematically non-demanding」主题要求低的简单任务同样无需小组。故选C。
