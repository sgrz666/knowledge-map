【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

When you focus on "utterance function" and "expected response" by using examples like"Here you are " "Thanks" you are probably teaching language at the__________.

- A. lexical level
- B. sentence level
- C. grammatical level
- D. discourse level

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When you focus on "utterance function" and "expected response" by using examples like"Here you are " "Thanks" you are probably teaching language at the__________.。选项：Alexical level；Bsentence level；Cgrammatical level；Ddiscourse level。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 「utterance function」（话语功能）与「expected response」（预期回应）关注的是相邻话语之间的功能关系，如「Here you are」触发「Thanks」，属语篇层面的衔接与功能关系。D「discourse level」符合。A「lexical level」关注词汇；B「sentence level」关注单句结构；C「grammatical level」关注语法规则。故选D。
