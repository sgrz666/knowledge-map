【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第16题】

Which of the following statements about task design is incorrect?

- A. Activities must have clear and attainable objectives.
- B. Activities should be confined to the classroom context.
- C. Activities must be relevant to students' life experiences.
- D. Activities should help develop students' language ability.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following statements about task design is incorrect?。选项：AActivities must have clear and attainable objectives.；BActivities should be confined to the classroom context.；CActivities must be relevant to students' life experiences.；DActivities should help develop students' language ability.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 任务设计不应局限于课堂情境——真实语言任务应尽可能联系生活实际、延伸到课外。B「Activities should be confined to the classroom context」说法不正确。A「Activities must have clear and attainable objectives」目标明确可达，正确；C「Activities must be relevant to students' life experiences」联系学生生活经验，正确；D「Activities should help develop students' language ability」促进语言能力发展，正确。故选B。
