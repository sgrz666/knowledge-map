【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

Britain_________ for control of the sea in the 17th century.

- A. contended
- B. contrived
- C. contented
- D. confined

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Britain_________ for control of the sea in the 17th century.。选项：Acontended；Bcontrived；Ccontented；Dconfined。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「Britain ___ for control of the sea in the 17th century」需表示「争夺、竞争」。A「contended」与contend for搭配意为「争夺」，符合英国在17世纪争夺海上霸权的史实。B「contrived」意为「谋划、设计」；C「contented」意为「使满足」，且content意为满足；D「confined」意为「限制」。故选A。
