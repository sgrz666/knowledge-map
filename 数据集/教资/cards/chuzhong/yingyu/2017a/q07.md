【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

You´ll find yourself thinking about nothing _________when you´re very nervous.

- A. wherever
- B. whatsoever
- C. whenever
- D. however

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：You´ll find yourself thinking about nothing _________when you´re very nervous.。选项：Awherever；Bwhatsoever；Cwhenever；Dhowever。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** whatsoever是whatever的强调形式，用于否定句或nothing等之后表示强调，意为「根本、丝毫」。B「whatsoever」符合「thinking about nothing ___」（什么也不想）。A「wherever」表地点；C「whenever」表时间；D「however」表方式或程度。三者均不能与nothing搭配表示「一点也不」。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇、英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、lexical、morpheme、morphology、participle
