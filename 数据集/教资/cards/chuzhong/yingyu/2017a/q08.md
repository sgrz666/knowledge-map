【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

Mr. Johnson has a habit of asking questions _________.

- A. and then not listen to the answers
- B. but then not listen for the answers
- C. and then not listening to the answers
- D. and then doesn't listen to the answers

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Mr. Johnson has a habit of asking questions _________.。选项：Aand then not listen to the answers；Bbut then not listen for the answers；Cand then not listening to the answers；Dand then doesn't listen to the answers。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** a habit of asking questions构成的平行结构为asking questions and then not listening to the answers，与前面的动名词asking保持形式一致。C「and then not listening to the answers」符合动名词并列。A「not listen」为动词原形，与asking不平衡；B「not listen for」动词形式错误且介词不当；D「doesn't listen」为限定动词，不能与动名词并列。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
