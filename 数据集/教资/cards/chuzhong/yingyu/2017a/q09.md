【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

On hearing the utterance "It´s hot here" the listener opened the door. It is a(n) __________.

- A. locutionary act
- B. illocutionary act
- C. direct speech act
- D. perlocutionary act

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：On hearing the utterance "It´s hot here" the listener opened the door. It is a(n) __________.。选项：Alocutionary act；Billocutionary act；Cdirect speech act；Dperlocutionary act。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 听话人听到「It's hot here」后打开了门，话语产生了实际效果，属取效行为。D「perlocutionary act」指话语在听话人身上产生的实际效果，符合「listener opened the door」的结果。A「locutionary act」指说话本身；B「illocutionary act」指说话人的意图（如请求开窗）；C「direct speech act」指字面意义与言外之意一致的行为类型。题干问的是「开门」这一结果行为。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
