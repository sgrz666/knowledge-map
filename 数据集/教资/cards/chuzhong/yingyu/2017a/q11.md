【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

When the teacher attempts to elicit more information from the students by saying "And ...?"Good. A nything else ? etc he/she is playing the role of a__________.

- A. prompter
- B. participant
- C. manager
- D. consultant

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When the teacher attempts to elicit more information from the students by saying "And ...?"Good. A nything else ? etc he/she is playing the role of a__________.。选项：Aprompter；Bparticipant；Cmanager；Dconsultant。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师说「And...?」「Good. Anything else?」是在学生回答不充分时给予引导和鼓励，促使其补充更多信息，扮演提示者角色。A「prompter」符合。B「participant」是作为参与者加入活动；C「manager」负责组织与管理课堂；D「consultant」指咨询者，多指教师个别指导时的角色。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
