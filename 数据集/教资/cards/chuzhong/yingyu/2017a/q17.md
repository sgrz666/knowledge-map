【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

If someone says "I know the word" he should not only understand its meaning but also be able to pronounce spell and__________ it.

- A. explain
- B. recognize
- C. memorize
- D. use

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If someone says "I know the word" he should not only understand its meaning but also be able to pronounce spell and__________ it.。选项：Aexplain；Brecognize；Cmemorize；Duse。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 真正「认识」一个单词，除理解词义、会读会拼外，还必须能在实际语言表达中运用该词。D「use」符合词汇学习的形、义、用三维度。A「explain」（解释）、B「recognize」（识别）、C「memorize」（记忆）都不是衡量「掌握单词」的运用维度。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇；关键词：lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary、word formation、构词、词汇
