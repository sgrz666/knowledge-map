【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第19题】

When students are asked to go to the local museum libraries etc. to find out information about endangered animals and work out a plan for an exhibition they are doing a(n)__________.

- A. survey
- B. experiment
- C. project
- D. presentation

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When students are asked to go to the local museum libraries etc. to find out information about endangered animals and work out a plan for an exhibition they are doing a(n)__________.。选项：Asurvey；Bexperiment；Cproject；Dpresentation。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 学生到博物馆、图书馆搜集濒危动物资料并制定展览计划，是围绕一个主题、整合多种资源、产出实际成果的综合性任务，属于项目（project）学习。C「project」符合。A「survey」侧重调查统计；B「experiment」侧重实验验证；D「presentation」侧重展示汇报，只是项目的一个环节。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：教学活动与问题链设计、语言技能综合教学；关键词：post-reading、pre-reading、reading lesson、scanning、skimming、writing lesson、教学环节、教学设计
