【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Teachers could encourage students to use __________ to gather and organize their ideas for writing.

- A. eliciting
- B. mind mapping
- C. explaining
- D. brainstorming

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Teachers could encourage students to use __________ to gather and organize their ideas for writing.。选项：Aeliciting；Bmind mapping；Cexplaining；Dbrainstorming。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 思维导图（mind mapping）用于汇集并组织写作观点与素材，以图形化方式呈现观点间的层级与关联。B符合。A「eliciting」意为「引出」，是教师行为而非学生整理观点的工具；C「explaining」是解释；D「brainstorming」是发散列举观点，侧重生成而非组织。题干强调gather and organize，故思维导图更贴合。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
