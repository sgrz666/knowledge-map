【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

Which of the following tasks fails to develop students´ skill of recognizing discourse patterns?

- A. Analyzing the structure of difficult sentences.
- B. Checking the logic of the author' s arguments.
- C. Getting the scrambled sentences into a paragraph.
- D. Marking out common openers to stories and jokes.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following tasks fails to develop students´ skill of recognizing discourse patterns?。选项：AAnalyzing the structure of difficult sentences.；BChecking the logic of the author' s arguments.；CGetting the scrambled sentences into a paragraph.；DMarking out common openers to stories and jokes.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 识别语篇模式（discourse patterns）关注篇章的组织结构、逻辑推进与语篇标记。A「Analyzing the structure of difficult sentences」（分析难句结构）属于句法层面，与语篇模式无关，故不能发展该技能。B「Checking the logic of the author's arguments」检查论证逻辑、C「Getting the scrambled sentences into a paragraph」将打乱句子重组成段、D「Marking out common openers to stories and jokes」标记常见开篇语，均属语篇层面。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学、英语阅读理解；关键词：according to the、author、discourse、implicature、infer、linguistic、main idea、passage
