【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

Which of the following tasks fails to encourage active language use?

- A. Reciting a text.
- B. Bargaining in a shop
- C. Writing an application letter.
- D. Reading to get a message.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following tasks fails to encourage active language use?。选项：AReciting a text.；BBargaining in a shop；CWriting an application letter.；DReading to get a message.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 背诵课文（reciting a text）是机械复现，学生无需主动组织语言表达意义，不能促进主动的语言运用。A符合题干「fails to encourage active language use」。B「Bargaining in a shop」购物砍价、C「Writing an application letter」写申请信、D「Reading to get a message」阅读获取信息均为有真实目的的语言活动。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
