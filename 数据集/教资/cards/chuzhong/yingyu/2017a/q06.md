【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

Of the people who work here _________ are French and _________ English.

- A. half... half
- B. the half... the half
- C. a half... a half
- D. a half... the half

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Of the people who work here _________ are French and _________ English.。选项：Ahalf... half；Bthe half... the half；Ca half... a half；Da half... the half。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** half作代词表示「一半（的人）」时，前不加冠词，故为half...half。B、D中「the half」不能单独作主语表示「一半的人」；C「a half」通常表示「两个半边」且不与are French连用。题干「Of the people who work here, half are French and half English」中后半句省略了are，构成并列。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
