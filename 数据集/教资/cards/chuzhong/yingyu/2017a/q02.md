【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

_________ is the main rhyming pattern in the sentence"Alice´s aunt ate apples and acorns around A ugust. "

- A. Reverse rhyme
- B. End rhyme
- C. Assonance
- D. Alliteration

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：_________ is the main rhyming pattern in the sentence"Alice´s aunt ate apples and acorns around A ugust. "。选项：AReverse rhyme；BEnd rhyme；CAssonance；DAlliteration。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 句中「Alice's aunt ate apples and acorns around August」的相邻及相近单词均以元音字母a开头，重复的是词首辅音/元音音素，属于头韵（alliteration）。A「Reverse rhyme」指重读元音及后续音素相同而首辅音不同；B「End rhyme」要求行尾押韵，本句无行末韵脚；C「Assonance」指元音相同而辅音不同。故选D。
