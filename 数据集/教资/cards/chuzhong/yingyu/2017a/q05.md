【2017a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

Which of the following best describes the relation between night and knight?

- A. metonymy
- B. homonymy
- C. antonymy
- D. hyponymy

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following best describes the relation between night and knight?。选项：Ametonymy；Bhomonymy；Cantonymy；Dhyponymy。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** night（夜晚）与knight（骑士）拼写不同但发音完全相同，属同音异形异义词，即homonymy。B「homonymy」符合。A「metonymy」是转喻（以容器代内容等）；C「antonymy」是反义关系；D「hyponymy」是上下义关系。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
