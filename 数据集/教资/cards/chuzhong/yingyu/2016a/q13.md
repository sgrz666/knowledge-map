【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

Which of the following activities may be more appropriate to help students practice a new structure immediately after presentation in class?

- A. Role play.
- B. Group discussion.
- C. Pattern drill.
- D. Written homework.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities may be more appropriate to help students practice a new structure immediately after presentation in class?。选项：ARole play.；BGroup discussion.；CPattern drill.；DWritten homework.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 呈现新结构后需要立即进行控制性操练以固化形式。C「Pattern drill」（句型操练）是典型的机械性练习，适合呈现后立即进行。A「Role play」（角色扮演）与B「Group discussion」（小组讨论）属产出性活动，难度高于即时操练；D「Written homework」（书面作业）在课后完成，不能起到课堂上即时巩固的作用。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
