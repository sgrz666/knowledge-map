【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

Which of the following shows the correct sentence stress in normal cases?

- A. His 'brother is my 'best Mend.
- B. They 'help one 'another in their work.
- C. They have 'been in the 'countryside recently.
- D. She 'thought herself 'better 'than 'anyone else.

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following shows the correct sentence stress in normal cases?。选项：AHis 'brother is my 'best Mend.；BThey 'help one 'another in their work.；CThey have 'been in the 'countryside recently.；DShe 'thought herself 'better 'than 'anyone else.。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 英语句子重音落在实词（名词、动词、形容词、副词）上，虚词（冠词、介词、助动词、连词）一般不带重音。A「'brother」「'best」均为实词，重音位置正确。B中「one another」为固定短语，通常只重读another，标注重读one不当；C重读了助动词「been」，助动词在句中应弱读；D重读了连词「than」，而连词属虚词，不应带句子重音。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语音与音系；关键词：consonant、intonation、phoneme、phonetic、phonology、pronunciation、stress、vowel
