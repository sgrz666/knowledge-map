【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

Johnson is a man of great experience _________ much can be learned.

- A. for whom
- B. for which
- C. from that
- D. from whom

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Johnson is a man of great experience _________ much can be learned.。选项：Afor whom；Bfor which；Cfrom that；Dfrom whom。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「a man of great experience ___ much can be learned」指「一位经验丰富的人，从他身上可以学到很多」，需用「介词+关系代词」引导定语从句，learn from sb表示「向某人学习」，故用from whom。A「for whom」与learn不搭配（learn for无此用法）；B「for which」指物且介词不当；C「from that」错误——介词后不能用that引导定语从句。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
