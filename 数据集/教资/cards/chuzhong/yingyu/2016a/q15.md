【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

Which of the following activities can help develop the skill of listening for gist?

- A. Listen and find out where Jim lives.
- B. Listen and decide on the best title for the passage.
- C. Listen and underline the words the speaker stresses.
- D. Listen to pains of words and tell if they are the same.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities can help develop the skill of listening for gist?。选项：AListen and find out where Jim lives.；BListen and decide on the best title for the passage.；CListen and underline the words the speaker stresses.；DListen to pains of words and tell if they are the same.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 听主旨（listening for gist）要求把握材料的整体大意。B「Listen and decide on the best title for the passage」要求为全文择题，正是对整体大意的把握。A「find out where Jim lives」属听具体信息（listening for specific information）；C「underline the words the speaker stresses」关注语音细节；D「tell if the words are the same」属音素辨别。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、英语阅读理解；关键词：according to the、audio-lingual、author、communicative、infer、listening、main idea、oral
