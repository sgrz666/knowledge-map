【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第2题】

John felt great __________ about his upcoming trip to Sidney; indeed he could hardly contain his enthusiasm.

- A. unrest
- B. uncertainty
- C. anxiety
- D. excitement

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：John felt great __________ about his upcoming trip to Sidney; indeed he could hardly contain his enthusiasm.。选项：Aunrest；Buncertainty；Canxiety；Dexcitement。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干后半句「he could hardly contain his enthusiasm」（几乎按捺不住热情）直接提示情绪是积极的。D「excitement」（兴奋、激动）与enthusiasm语义一致。A「unrest」（不安）、B「uncertainty」（不确定）、C「anxiety」（焦虑）均为负面情绪，与后半句的热情相矛盾。故选D。
