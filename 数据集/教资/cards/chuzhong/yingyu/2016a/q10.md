【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

When using the imperative "Turn it off" to give an order the speaker highlights the _________of the utterance.

- A. locutionary act
- B. illocutionary act
- C. perlocutionary act
- D. indirect speech act

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When using the imperative "Turn it off" to give an order the speaker highlights the _________of the utterance.。选项：Alocutionary act；Billocutionary act；Cperlocutionary act；Dindirect speech act。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 言语行为理论中，locutionary act（发话行为）指说出有意义的语句本身；illocutionary act（施事行为）指说话人通过话语实施的意图行为，如命令、请求、承诺；perlocutionary act（取效行为）指话语在听话人身上产生的效果。用祈使句「Turn it off」下命令，体现的正是话语的施事意图，即illocutionary act。A「locutionary act」只关注话语的字面意义；C「perlocutionary act」关注结果效果；D「indirect speech act」指间接言语行为，与直接的祈使命令不符。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
