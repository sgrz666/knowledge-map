【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following is a typical feature of informal writing?

- A. A well-organized structure is preferred.
- B. Short and incomplete sentences are common.
- C. Technical terms and definitions are required.
- D. A wide range of vocabulary and structural patterns are used.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is a typical feature of informal writing?。选项：AA well-organized structure is preferred.；BShort and incomplete sentences are common.；CTechnical terms and definitions are required.；DA wide range of vocabulary and structural patterns are used.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 非正式写作的特征是句子简短、结构不完整、口语化。B「Short and incomplete sentences are common」符合。A「A well-organized structure is preferred」是正式写作的要求；C「Technical terms and definitions are required」属学术写作特征；D「A wide range of vocabulary and structural patterns are used」偏向正式书面语的特征。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章、词法与词汇；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary
