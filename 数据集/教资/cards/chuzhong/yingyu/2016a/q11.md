【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Fluent and appropriate language use requires knowledge of_________ and this suggests that we should teach lexical chunks rather than single words.

- A. connotation
- B. denotation
- C. morphology
- D. collocation

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Fluent and appropriate language use requires knowledge of_________ and this suggests that we should teach lexical chunks rather than single words.。选项：Aconnotation；Bdenotation；Cmorphology；Dcollocation。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 流利得体的语言使用需要掌握词与词之间的搭配关系，即D「collocation」（搭配），故教学应教词块（lexical chunks）而非孤立单词。A「connotation」（内涵意义）与B「denotation」（外延意义）属词义层面；C「morphology」（形态学）研究词的构词与屈折变化，均不解释词块教学的理论依据。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇；关键词：lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary、word formation、构词、词汇
