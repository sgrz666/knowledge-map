【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

When teaching students how to give appropriate responses to a congratulation or an apologythe teacher is probably teaching at _________.

- A. lexical level
- B. sentence level
- C. grammatical level
- D. discourse level

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When teaching students how to give appropriate responses to a congratulation or an apologythe teacher is probably teaching at _________.。选项：Alexical level；Bsentence level；Cgrammatical level；Ddiscourse level。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 如何对祝贺或道歉作出恰当回应，涉及话语在具体语境中的得体性与前后语篇的衔接，属于D「discourse level」（语篇层面）的教学。A「lexical level」关注词汇；B「sentence level」关注单句结构；C「grammatical level」关注语法规则。回应得体的关键在于语境适切，超出单句语法层面。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学、词法与词汇；关键词：discourse、implicature、lexical、linguistic、morpheme、morphology、pragmatics、presupposition
