【2016a · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第3题】

The professor´s classroom manner was quite__________，never revealing the warmth and playfulness she showed in private.

- A. lively
- B. amiable
- C. formal
- D. cheerful

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The professor´s classroom manner was quite__________，never revealing the warmth and playfulness she showed in private.。选项：Alively；Bamiable；Cformal；Dcheerful。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「never revealing the warmth and playfulness she showed in private」说明课堂上的表现与私下相反——私下热情活泼，课堂上则克制。C「formal」（正式的、拘谨的）与私下形成对比。A「lively」（活泼的）与B「amiable」（和蔼的）与「未流露热情」矛盾；D「cheerful」（愉快的）同样与反差描述不符。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：词法与词汇；关键词：lexical、morpheme、morphology、vocabulary、word formation、构词、词汇
