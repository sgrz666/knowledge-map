【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第20题】

According to the passage which of the following statements is INCORRECT? （ ）

- A. Donna was a volunteer teacher of the training program.
- B. The author knew what was going to happen when he/she entered the classroom.
- C. Donna’s fourth grade classroom appeared to be traditional resembling many others the author had seen in the past.
- D. Everyone regardless of gender age and occupation may encounter many “I can’t”in life.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：According to the passage which of the following statements is INCORRECT? （ ）。选项：ADonna was a volunteer teacher of the training program.；BThe author knew what was going to happen when he/she entered the classroom.；CDonna’s fourth grade classroom appeared to be traditional resembling many others the author had seen in the past.；DEveryone regardless of gender age and occupation may encounter many “I can’t”in life.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 问哪项错误。B称『作者进教室时就知道会发生什么』，但原文说『something seemed different that day when I entered it for the first time』，作者初次进入并不知情，故B错；A、C、D均与原文相符。故选B。
