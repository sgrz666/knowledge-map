【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

After working for the firm for ten years he finally（ ）the rank of deputy director.

- A. achieved
- B. approached
- C. attained
- D. acquired

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：After working for the firm for ten years he finally（ ）the rank of deputy director.。选项：Aachieved；Bapproached；Cattained；Dacquired。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 搭配『the rank of deputy director（副主任一职）』。A『achieved』多接目标、成就；B『approached』接近；C『attained』常接职位、头衔，表『获得（地位）』；D『acquired』多接技能、财产、知识。故选C。
