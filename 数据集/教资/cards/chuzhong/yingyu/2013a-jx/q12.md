【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

Which of the following materials is NOT appropriate for a teacher to use in listening practice?（ ）

- A. Materials with different dialects.
- B. Comprehensible authentic materials.
- C. Materials with comprehensible new words for students.
- D. Materials with contents beyond students’ comprehension capacity.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following materials is NOT appropriate for a teacher to use in listening practice?（ ）。选项：AMaterials with different dialects.；BComprehensible authentic materials.；CMaterials with comprehensible new words for students.；DMaterials with contents beyond students’ comprehension capacity.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 听力练习应选可理解的真实材料。A不同口音、B可理解真实材料、C含可理解生词的材料都合适；D『内容超出学生理解能力』违背i+1可理解输入原则，不适合。故选D。
