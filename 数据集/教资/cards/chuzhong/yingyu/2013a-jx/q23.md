【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第23题】

According to the context the underlined word“they”in the last paragraph refers to（ ）

- A. the students
- B. Donna and her students
- C. The “I can’t”
- D. “I Can” “I Will”and“I’m Going to”

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：According to the context the underlined word“they”in the last paragraph refers to（ ）。选项：Athe students；BDonna and her students；CThe “I can’t”；D“I Can” “I Will”and“I’m Going to”。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 末段前文写埋葬『I Can’t』后，众人又写下『I Can』『I Will』『I’m Going to』，故they指代这三者。A学生、B师生、C『I Can’t』均不符。故选D。
