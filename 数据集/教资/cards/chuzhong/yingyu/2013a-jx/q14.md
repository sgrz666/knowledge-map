【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

Teachers believing in the（ ）model in a general sense usually follow the sequence of teaching new words sentences and then the whole passage in the reading class.

- A. interactive
- B. bottom-up
- C. top-down
- D. interactional

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Teachers believing in the（ ）model in a general sense usually follow the sequence of teaching new words sentences and then the whole passage in the reading class.。选项：Ainteractive；Bbottom-up；Ctop-down；Dinteractional。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 『先教生词→句子→整篇』是自底向上（bottom-up）加工模式，由局部到整体。A互动、C自顶向下（先整体后局部）、D交互均不符。故选B。
