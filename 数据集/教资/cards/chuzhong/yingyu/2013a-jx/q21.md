【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第21题】

It can be inferred from the passage that what Donna and the students were doing in theclassroom （ ）the author’s perceptions of touching.

- A. agrees with
- B. contradicts
- C. goes beyond
- D. imparts

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：It can be inferred from the passage that what Donna and the students were doing in theclassroom （ ）the author’s perceptions of touching.。选项：Aagrees with；Bcontradicts；Cgoes beyond；Dimparts。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 文中Donna带学生埋葬『I Can’t』并写『I Can』等，作者受其触动，态度上认同（agrees with）这种赋能理念。B矛盾、C超越、D赋予均不符文意。故选A。
