（ ）helps students facilitate their process of accumulating vocabulary broadening scope of vision and increasing target language exposure.

- A. Scanning
- B. Skimming
- C. Extensive reading
- D. Intensive reading

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：（ ）helps students facilitate their process of accumulating vocabulary broadening scope of vision and increasing target language exposure.。选项：AScanning；BSkimming；CExtensive reading；DIntensive reading。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 泛读（Extensive reading）有助于积累词汇、拓宽视野、增加目标语接触。A略读、B跳读是阅读技巧而非持续阅读活动；D精读侧重深度理解。故选C。
