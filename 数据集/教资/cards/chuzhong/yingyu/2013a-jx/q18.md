【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

Which of the following nominating patterns can a teacher adopt to ensure that all students are actively involved in classroom activities?（ ）

- A. Nominating those who are good at English.
- B. Asking questions in a predicable sequence.
- C. Nominating students after the question is given.
- D. Nominating students before giving the question.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following nominating patterns can a teacher adopt to ensure that all students are actively involved in classroom activities?（ ）。选项：ANominating those who are good at English.；BAsking questions in a predicable sequence.；CNominating students after the question is given.；DNominating students before giving the question.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 为确保全体学生积极参与，应在提出问题后再点名（nominating after the question）。A只点优生、B按可预测顺序、D先点名再提问都会使未点名者松懈。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
