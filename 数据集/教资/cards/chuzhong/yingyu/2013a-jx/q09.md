【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

The conversational implication of “John is a machine”as a response to “what do you think of John”is context-bound. It possesses all the following distinguishing properties but（ ）.

- A. non—detachability
- B. calculability
- C. conventionality
- D. defeasibility

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The conversational implication of “John is a machine”as a response to “what do you think of John”is context-bound. It possesses all the following distinguishing properties but（ ）.。选项：Anon—detachability；Bcalculability；Cconventionality；Ddefeasibility。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 会话含义（conversational implicature）的特征包括可推导性（calculability）、不可分离性（non-detachability）、可取消性（defeasibility）。A、B、D均为其特征；C『conventionality（约定性）』不是会话含义的特征，因为含义是非约定俗成、靠推理得出的。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
