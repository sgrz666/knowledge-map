【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

The（ ）approach to writing teaching pays attention to not only what to write but also how to write.

- A. product-oriented
- B. process-oriented
- C. form-focused
- D. meaning-focused

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The（ ）approach to writing teaching pays attention to not only what to write but also how to write.。选项：Aproduct-oriented；Bprocess-oriented；Cform-focused；Dmeaning-focused。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 写作教学中『既关注写什么也关注怎么写』是过程法（process-oriented）。A成果法只重最终结果；C重形式、D重意义，均片面。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语写作与篇章；关键词：coherence、cohesion、composition、essay、writing、写作
