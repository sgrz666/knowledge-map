【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

The factory operated（ ）until the order was filled.

- A. at times
- B. to the minute
- C. day by day
- D. around the clock

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：The factory operated（ ）until the order was filled.。选项：Aat times；Bto the minute；Cday by day；Daround the clock。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 句意为工厂____运转直到订单完成。A『at times』有时；B『to the minute』恰好、一分不差；C『day by day』一天天；D『around the clock』昼夜不停。只有D能表达为满足订单而连续不停地生产。故选D。
