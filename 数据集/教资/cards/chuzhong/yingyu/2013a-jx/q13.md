【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

（ ）may be defined as any kind of engaging with the language on the part of the learners usually under the teacher’s supervision whose primary objective is to consolidate learning.

- A. Presentation
- B. Practice
- C. Production
- D. Preparation

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：（ ）may be defined as any kind of engaging with the language on the part of the learners usually under the teacher’s supervision whose primary objective is to consolidate learning.。选项：APresentation；BPractice；CProduction；DPreparation。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干定义『学习者在教师监督下接触语言以巩固学习』正是Practice（练习）环节。A呈现、C产出、D准备均不符该定义。故选B。
