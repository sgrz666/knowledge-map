【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

If we（ ）our test tomorrow I would have gone to the concert.

- A. Weren’t to have
- B. Hadn’t been to have
- C. Aren’t to have
- D. Hadn’t had

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：If we（ ）our test tomorrow I would have gone to the concert.。选项：AWeren’t to have；BHadn’t been to have；CAren’t to have；DHadn’t had。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 主句『I would have gone』为与过去事实相反的虚拟结果，if从句表与将来事实相反须用『were to do』。A『Weren’t to have』即『if we weren’t to have』，符合结构；B『Hadn’t been to have』为过去完成，不表将来；C『Aren’t to have』用现在时，不表虚拟将来；D『Hadn’t had』为过去完成，亦不符。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
