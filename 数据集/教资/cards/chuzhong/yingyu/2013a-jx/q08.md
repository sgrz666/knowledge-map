【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第8题】

（ ）you said is true there are still other factors to be considered.

- A. Supposed that what
- B. Supposing what that
- C. Granted that what
- D. Considered what that

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：（ ）you said is true there are still other factors to be considered.。选项：ASupposed that what；BSupposing what that；CGranted that what；DConsidered what that。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 句意『____你说的是真的，仍有其他因素要考虑』，需表『即使/承认』的连词。A『Supposed that what』结构错误；B『Supposing what that』语序错误；C『Granted that what』中『Granted that』表『即使/承认』，what引导主语从句，结构正确；D『Considered what that』搭配不当。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
