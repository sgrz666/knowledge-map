【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

When learners come across new words they are required to focus on（ ）.

- A. spelling
- B. semantic features
- C. form meaning and use
- D. word formation

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：When learners come across new words they are required to focus on（ ）.。选项：Aspelling；Bsemantic features；Cform meaning and use；Dword formation。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 词汇学习中，学习者遇生词应关注『形、义、用』（form, meaning and use）。A拼写、B语义特征、D构词法都只是其中单一侧面，C最完整。故选C。
