【2013a-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Which of the following is NOT exhibited by the deductive method?（ ）

- A. It saves time.
- B. It pays more attention to form.
- C. It teaches grammar in a decontextualized way.
- D. It encourages students to work out the grammatical rules.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is NOT exhibited by the deductive method?（ ）。选项：AIt saves time.；BIt pays more attention to form.；CIt teaches grammar in a decontextualized way.；DIt encourages students to work out the grammatical rules.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 演绎法（deductive）特点：先给规则再举例，省时、重形式、脱离语境教语法（A、B、C均属）。D『encourages students to work out the grammatical rules（让学生自己总结出规则）』是归纳法（inductive）的特征，非演绎法。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
