【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第9题】

Which of the following suggests an illocutionary act by the speaker when he says “Is there salt in the jar at the dinner table?”

- A. To send somebody to buy some salt.
- B. To ask somebody where he can get some salt.
- C. To ask somebody to hand the salt over to him.
- D. To double check whether there is any salt left.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following suggests an illocutionary act by the speaker when he says “Is there salt in the jar at the dinner table?”。选项：ATo send somebody to buy some salt.；BTo ask somebody where he can get some salt.；CTo ask somebody to hand the salt over to him.；DTo double check whether there is any salt left.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 说话人说「Is there salt in the jar at the dinner table?」，字面是询问，实际意图是请人把盐递过来，这是通过询问实施的请求行为，属施事行为（illocutionary act）。C「To ask somebody to hand the salt over to him」符合施事意图。A「To send somebody to buy some salt」与餐桌语境不符；B「To ask where he can get salt」是字面理解的另一种；D「To double check whether there is any salt left」属字面的验证意图，而非言外之力。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语义语用与语言学；关键词：discourse、implicature、linguistic、pragmatics、presupposition、semantics、speech act、语用
