【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第18题】

What technique was he/she using if a teacher kept saying “Yes anything else”？to a student who was sharing ideas about English learning？

- A. Elicitation
- B. Explanation
- C. Paraphrasing
- D. Exemplification

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What technique was he/she using if a teacher kept saying “Yes anything else”？to a student who was sharing ideas about English learning？。选项：AElicitation；BExplanation；CParaphrasing；DExemplification。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 教师用「Yes? Anything else?」不断追问，是在诱导学生继续输出更多想法，帮助其自行生成内容，属启发诱导技术。A「Elicitation」符合。B「Explanation」是讲解、C「Paraphrasing」是释义重述、D「Exemplification」是举例，均不描述循序追问以引出内容的行为。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
