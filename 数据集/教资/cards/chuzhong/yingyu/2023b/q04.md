【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第4题】

Teachers should give some ______ to how they can best cope with the mental and intellectual challenges that students may face in a new environment.

- A. opinions
- B. thoughts
- C. ideas
- D. advice

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Teachers should give some ______ to how they can best cope with the mental and intellectual challenges that students may face in a new environment.。选项：Aopinions；Bthoughts；Cideas；Dadvice。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** give some thought(s) to sth是固定搭配，意为「对…加以考虑」，符合「教师应当考虑如何应对学生在新环境中面临的心理与智力挑战」。B「thoughts」符合。A「opinions」（观点）、C「ideas」（想法）与give...to搭配不表示「考虑」；D「advice」为不可数名词，不能受some直接修饰成some advice to how（搭配应为advice on）。故选B。
