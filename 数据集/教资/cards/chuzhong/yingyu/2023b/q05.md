【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第5题】

One-fourth of the committee who opposed to the teaching reform were told that since the teaching reform had already started there was no point in ______.

- A. competing
- B. stopping
- C. protesting
- D. denying

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：One-fourth of the committee who opposed to the teaching reform were told that since the teaching reform had already started there was no point in ______.。选项：Acompeting；Bstopping；Cprotesting；Ddenying。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 题干「委员会中四分之一的反对者被告知教改既已启动，再___也没有意义」，需表示抗议。C「protesting」符合oppose to reform者继续抗议的语境。A「competing」竞争、B「stopping」阻止、D「denying」否认，均与「教改已开始、继续反对无意义」的逻辑不符。故选C。
