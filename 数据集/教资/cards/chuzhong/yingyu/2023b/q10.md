【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Some learners of English in South China tend to pronounce “night”as“light”，this is an example of ______.

- A. over-generalization
- B. cross-association
- C. interlingual interference
- D. intralingual interference

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Some learners of English in South China tend to pronounce “night”as“light”，this is an example of ______.。选项：Aover-generalization；Bcross-association；Cinterlingual interference；Dintralingual interference。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 华南地区部分英语学习者把night读作light，是受汉语（或方言）中/n/、/l/不分的母语习惯影响，属母语对目标语的干扰，即语际干扰。C「interlingual interference」符合。A「over-generalization」过度概括与D「intralingual interference」语内干扰均为目标语内部规则泛化所致；B「cross-association」是交叉联想。故选C。
