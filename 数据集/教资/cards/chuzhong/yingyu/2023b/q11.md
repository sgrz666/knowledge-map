【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

What strategy does the teacher want the students to develop if he/she encourages them to use mime or gestures to make themselves understood？

- A. Cognitive strategy
- B. Communicative strategy
- C. Affective strategy
- D. Meta-cognitive strategy

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What strategy does the teacher want the students to develop if he/she encourages them to use mime or gestures to make themselves understood？。选项：ACognitive strategy；BCommunicative strategy；CAffective strategy；DMeta-cognitive strategy。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 用手势、模仿等非语言手段让对方理解自己，是为克服交际障碍而采取的手段，属交际策略。B「Communicative strategy」符合。A「Cognitive strategy」用于加工学习材料；C「Affective strategy」用于调节情绪；D「Meta-cognitive strategy」用于计划监控学习过程。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
