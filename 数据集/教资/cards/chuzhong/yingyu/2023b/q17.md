【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

Which of the following activities can be used to engage students in communication at the post-reading stage？

- A. Reciting the text.
- B. Reading the text aloud.
- C. Exchanging personal views on the text.
- D. Filling in the blanks of the text summary.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities can be used to engage students in communication at the post-reading stage？。选项：AReciting the text.；BReading the text aloud.；CExchanging personal views on the text.；DFilling in the blanks of the text summary.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 读后（post-reading）阶段应引导学生超越文本、进行输出与交流。C「Exchanging personal views on the text」交换对文本的个人看法，属读后交流活动。A「Reciting the text」背诵与B「Reading the text aloud」朗读属读中或机械练习；D「Filling in the blanks of the text summary」填空仍停留在文本理解层面。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语言技能综合教学；关键词：post-reading、pre-reading、reading lesson、scanning、skimming、writing lesson、语言技能
