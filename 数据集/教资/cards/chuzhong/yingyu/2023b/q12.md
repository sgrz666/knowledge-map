【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

Deducing the meaning of a word from context is one of the sub-skills of ______.

- A. listening and speaking
- B. listening and reading
- C. speaking and writing
- D. reading and writing

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Deducing the meaning of a word from context is one of the sub-skills of ______.。选项：Alistening and speaking；Blistening and reading；Cspeaking and writing；Dreading and writing。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 根据上下文推断词义是阅读与听力共有的理解性微技能——两者都需在语流中通过语境线索推断生词含义。B「listening and reading」符合。A「listening and speaking」与C「speaking and writing」都含产出性技能，产出时不涉及「推断词义」；D「reading and writing」含写作，同样不匹配。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、英语写作与篇章；关键词：audio-lingual、coherence、cohesion、communicative、composition、essay、listening、oral
