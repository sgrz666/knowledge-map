【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第6题】

I’d like to play chess with Cliff better than ______.

- A. with Perry
- B. that with Perry
- C. playing with Perry
- D. play chess with Perry

**答案:A**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：I’d like to play chess with Cliff better than ______.。选项：Awith Perry；Bthat with Perry；Cplaying with Perry；Dplay chess with Perry。给定答案：A
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 比较结构I'd like to play chess with Cliff better than with Perry中，than后应与前面的比较对象保持平行，比的是「和谁下棋」，故用with Perry，省略了play chess。A「with Perry」符合平行结构。B「that with Perry」多出that；C「playing with Perry」与to play chess with Cliff不平行；D「play chess with Perry」重复了前面的动词短语，且形式不一致。故选A。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
