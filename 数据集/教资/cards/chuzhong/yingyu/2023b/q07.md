【2023b · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第7题】

To their grief they have done things they ought not to have done and ______ undone what they ought to.

- A. leave
- B. left
- C. leaving
- D. will leave

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：To their grief they have done things they ought not to have done and ______ undone what they ought to.。选项：Aleave；Bleft；Cleaving；Dwill leave。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** done things they ought not to have done 与 ___ undone what they ought to 为并列的完成时结构，共用助动词have，故用过去分词left。B「left」符合。A「leave」为原形、C「leaving」为现在分词、D「will leave」为将来时，均与前面的完成时并列结构不一致。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：英语语法与句法；关键词：agreement、clause、gerund、grammar、participle、passive、subjunctive、syntax
