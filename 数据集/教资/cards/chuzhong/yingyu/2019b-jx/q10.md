【2019b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第10题】

Which is not the major purpose of asking students to give a report after the group discussion in a reading class?

- A. To pool ideas.
- B. To correct mistakes.
- C. To encourage language use.
- D. To practice oral English.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which is not the major purpose of asking students to give a report after the group discussion in a reading class?。选项：ATo pool ideas.；BTo correct mistakes.；CTo encourage language use.；DTo practice oral English.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 小组讨论后作报告的目的是汇集观点、促进语言运用、练习口语表达，而纠正错误并非其主要目的——纠错应另择时机且不宜打断表达流畅性。B「To correct mistakes」不是该活动的主要目的。A「To pool ideas」汇集观点、C「To encourage language use」鼓励语言运用、D「To practice oral English」练习口语，均为合理目的。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
