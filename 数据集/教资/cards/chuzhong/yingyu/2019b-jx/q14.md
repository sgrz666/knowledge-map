【2019b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第14题】

Activities such as problem solving decision making and opinion exchanging are typical practices in ________.

- A. audio-lingual method
- B. grammar-translation method
- C. situational language teaching
- D. task-based language teaching

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Activities such as problem solving decision making and opinion exchanging are typical practices in ________.。选项：Aaudio-lingual method；Bgrammar-translation method；Csituational language teaching；Dtask-based language teaching。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 解决问题、做出决策、交换意见均为以完成任务为核心的活动，是任务型语言教学的典型做法。D「task-based language teaching」符合。A「audio-lingual method」以句型操练为主；B「grammar-translation method」以翻译和语法讲解为主；C「situational language teaching」以情景呈现句型为主。故选D。
