【2019b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第12题】

What would a teacher ask students to do if he/she intends to develop their skimming skills?

- A. To rewrite the text.
- B. To read for the specific information.
- C. To predict the content of the text.
- D. To read for the general idea of the text.

**答案:D**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What would a teacher ask students to do if he/she intends to develop their skimming skills?。选项：ATo rewrite the text.；BTo read for the specific information.；CTo predict the content of the text.；DTo read for the general idea of the text.。给定答案：D
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 略读（skimming）是快速浏览全文把握大意的技能。D「To read for the general idea of the text」符合。A「To rewrite the text」是改写；B「To read for the specific information」是查读（scanning）；C「To predict the content of the text」是预测，属读前活动。故选D。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：语言技能综合教学；关键词：post-reading、pre-reading、reading lesson、scanning、skimming、writing lesson、语言技能
