【2019b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第15题】

What type of question is more likely to motivate learners to talk more and get new information?

- A. Yes-no question.
- B. Closed question.
- C. Referential question.
- D. Display question.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What type of question is more likely to motivate learners to talk more and get new information?。选项：AYes-no question.；BClosed question.；CReferential question.；DDisplay question.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 参考性问题（referential question）答案开放，教师不知学生将如何回答，学生需提供新信息，因而更能激发表达。C符合。A「Yes-no question」只需回答是或否；B「Closed question」答案唯一；D「Display question」答案教师已知，仅供检查。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：课堂提问与互动组织；关键词：学生：、小组、师生、师：、提问、教师：、讨论、课堂互动
