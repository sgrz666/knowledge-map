【2019b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第13题】

What does a teacher focus on if he/she asks students to list their ideas select and expand those ideas and then make an outline?

- A. Students’ reading skills.
- B. Students’ translating skills.
- C. Students’ writing skills.
- D. Students’ listening skills.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：What does a teacher focus on if he/she asks students to list their ideas select and expand those ideas and then make an outline?。选项：AStudents’ reading skills.；BStudents’ translating skills.；CStudents’ writing skills.；DStudents’ listening skills.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 让学生列举观点、筛选扩展、列出提纲，是写作构思阶段的典型活动。C「Students' writing skills」符合。A「reading skills」阅读、B「translating skills」翻译、D「listening skills」听力，均与列提纲无关。故选C。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、英语写作与篇章；关键词：audio-lingual、coherence、cohesion、communicative、composition、essay、listening、oral
