【2019b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第11题】

Which of the following is a kind of information gap activities in class?

- A. Pattern drills.
- B. Making a survey.
- C. Sentence transformation.
- D. Forming sentences.

**答案:B**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following is a kind of information gap activities in class?。选项：APattern drills.；BMaking a survey.；CSentence transformation.；DForming sentences.。给定答案：B
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 信息差活动要求参与者各自掌握不完全的信息，通过交流互补才能完成任务。B「Making a survey」做调查时学生需互相询问以获取未知信息，构成信息差。A「Pattern drills」句型操练、C「Sentence transformation」句型转换、D「Forming sentences」造句均为形式操练，信息是已知的。故选B。
**考点溯源与理论依据:** 关联知识点：听说与交际教学、教学活动与问题链设计；关键词：audio-lingual、communicative、listening、oral、role play、speaking、交际、听说
