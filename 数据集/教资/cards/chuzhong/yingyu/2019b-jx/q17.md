【2019b-jx · chuzhong·yingyu · 单选 · 第17题】

Which of the following activities is most likely to be used to encourage students’ active language use?

- A. Reading a text and making true or false judgement.
- B. Listening to a passage and filling in the blank.
- C. Doing a role play of opening a bank account.
- D. Reordering the sentences into a passage.

**答案:C**

**【结构化解析】**
**考点剖析与关键信息:** 题干考查：Which of the following activities is most likely to be used to encourage students’ active language use?。选项：AReading a text and making true or false judgement.；BListening to a passage and filling in the blank.；CDoing a role play of opening a bank account.；DReordering the sentences into a passage.。给定答案：C
**选项深度对比/得分点:** 主动语言运用需学生为真实交际目的组织语言。C「Doing a role play of opening a bank account」模拟开银行账户，具备真实交际目的。A「true or false judgement」判断正误、B「filling in the blank」填空、D「Reordering the sentences」排序，均为理解或形式层面的被动练习。故选C。
